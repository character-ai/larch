# Collaborative Sketches
`/design` no longer runs collaborative sketches. The sketch phase and tier distinction have been removed. `/design` uses a single direct-drafting flow: Step 2a writes sentinel artifacts, Step 2b drafts the plan directly from the codebase, and Step 3 runs the plan-review panel.

## Difficulty-tiered plan review

Plan review uses tier caps 2/2/3. Design panels do not shed a vendor by tier. A round with at least two accepted in-scope high-severity findings escalates directly to HARD, skips pruning on the escalated round, and can authorize round 3.
