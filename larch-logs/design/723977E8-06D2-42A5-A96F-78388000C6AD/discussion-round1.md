## Decision 2: Preserve harness-pinned literal prose alongside KV grammar
- **Question**: Beyond the issue's own "preserve KV grammar, manifest schema literals, fenced commands, and routing tokens verbatim" constraint, do the target files carry additional literal-text constraints from test harnesses?
- **Resolution**: Yes. `scripts/test-implement-structure.sh` and `scripts/test-implement-fence-shape.sh` pin dozens of exact prose substrings (full sentences, not just KV tokens) inside these files via `require()` / `forbid()` calls.
