### Failure-mode highlights
- **PID race on slow startup**: if monitor times out before the background script reaches `larch_quiet_write_paired_pid_file`, the pid file is empty/missing. WARN-and-skip path handles this gracefully; no kill attempted.
- **PID reuse**: a long-departed PID could be reused by another process by the time monitor sends SIGTERM. Mitigation: validate PID is a positive integer and ideally a child of the calling shell — but cross-Bash-3.2 child-process check is awkward; accept the small reuse risk as a known limitation and document it.
- **Hang during 5-second grace**: kill-loop must use `kill -0 "$pid"` polling, not blocking `wait` (wait can't be used on non-child PIDs). 5 retries × 1s sleep.
