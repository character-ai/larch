Extract changed symbols from fix diffs before history scans. Find external consumers, include them in bundle evidence, and widen history and revert scans. Evolve agent and ledger schemas additively. Render introduced risks and incomplete fix classes without changing filing approval.

## Edge cases

- Diffs without recognized symbols produce an empty consumer section and retain touched-file-only scans.
- Deleted names remain searchable so stale consumers of renamed fields are visible.
- Multiple symbols or references on one line remain deduplicated and deterministic.
- References inside touched files do not become consumers.
- Legacy rows remain usable but do not fabricate risk or class-completeness claims.
- `none found` risks and `class_complete=true` rows stay out of alert sections and follow-up content.

## Failure modes

- Reject malformed current-version agent rows rather than silently dropping fields.
- Do not treat grep matches in the changed files as blast-radius evidence.
- Do not overwrite a verified deep risk or sibling list with stale triage data.
- Do not create a follow-up issue directly. Only generate the body consumed by the existing approval gate.
