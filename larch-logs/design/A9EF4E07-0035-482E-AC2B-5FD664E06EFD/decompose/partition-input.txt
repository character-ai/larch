# contract-unification [FEATURE] Facade shrink + single cli.py registry

​#### Problem

Re-export facades freeze private names as de-facto API, and a dedicated lint (`monkeypatch-facade-binding`, with baseline) exists just to police the seams they create. `report/run_logs.py` re-exports 149 names, 116 of them private, with tests binding through it (59 `run_logs._*` references). `review/review_pipeline.py` (188 lines) re-exports ~120 names with ~6 real external uses. `issue/analyze_issues.py:17-90` imports 63 names, 35 re-export-only, with skill scripts consuming privates through it. `design/design_lifecycle.py` (268 lines) is pure re-imports (verified; carries `# pylint: disable=cyclic-import,unused-import`) yet `larch/cli.py` routes 28 design verbs through it, importing all step modules on any design verb. Separately, `larch/cli.py` maintains two parallel hand-edited tables: the 606-entry `_REGISTRY` and the 210-key `_MACHINE_STDOUT_KEYS` (lines 681-853); registering a machine-stdout verb requires two edits, and omitting the second silently leaves quiet-mode suppression on for KEY=value consumers.

​#### Goal

Repoint consumers and tests to the defining modules; shrink each facade to its genuinely-public symbols (measured call surface); delete `design_lifecycle.py` and route cli entries directly; merge the two cli.py tables into one registry row carrying a `machine_stdout` flag with the frozenset derived.

​#### Size

Net reduction 800-1,000 lines; removes 100+ dual import paths and a silent-omission failure class.

​#### Dependencies

Land after the plan-review merge, the run_log_corpus mandate, and the in-process-calls issue, which restructure the modules behind these facades.
