### FINDING_2: `threshold/2` feasibility bound is not the tight max-min spread lower bound
- **Reviewer(s)**: Codex-Pragmatic, Cursor-dyn-math-bound, Codex-dyn-math-bound
- **Severity**: important
- **Concern**: The plan’s proposed check `max_target_time > ideal_shard + balance_threshold / 2` is a conservative sufficient test, not the tight single-heavy-target lower bound for max-min spread across `n` shards. Step 9 verifies `max_shard - min_shard <= threshold` (lines 461–464). For `n > 2`, spread can still be `<= threshold` when the heaviest target lies between `ideal + threshold/2` and `ideal + threshold * (n-1) / n`. The preflight can then falsely warn that the threshold “cannot be met” even when the real lower bound is within threshold.
- **Suggested revisions (informational for voters; coder decides)**:
  - From Codex-Pragmatic: Use the necessary bound max_allowed = ideal_shard + balance_threshold * (n_shards - 1) / n_shards, or downgrade the message to a heuristic warning instead of "cannot be met".
  - From Cursor-dyn-math-bound: Keep the issue formula as the trigger but soften warning text to "may not meet" / "unlikely to meet"; optionally document in rebalance.md that the tight single-job impossibility check is m > (T + (n-1)*threshold)/n (equals T/n + threshold/2 only when n=2)
  - From Codex-dyn-math-bound: Compute lower_bound_spread = max_target_time - (total_seconds - max_target_time) / (n_shards - 1) for n_shards > 1, warn only when lower_bound_spread > balance_threshold, and keep empty medians plus one shard as no-warning early returns.
