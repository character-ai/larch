That would make the TSV schema internally inconsistent: missing voters belong in empty `vN_*` fields, not in `reviewer_slots`.

   Suggested revision: make the harness assert that `reviewer_slots` remains the ballot reviewer/proposer attribution, with missing judge state represented only by the corresponding empty vN group.
