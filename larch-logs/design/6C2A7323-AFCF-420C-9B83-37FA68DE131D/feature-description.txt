# /design: references raw-Bash sweep plus structural lint for script-only fences

**Problem.** After the per-step factoring issues land, residual raw Bash remains in references and minor SKILL.md sites: `references/brainstorm.md` collection fences carry raw `[ -f ... ] && source ...` preludes plus prompt-side dirty-tree and failure-log handling; `references/plan-review.md` retains a superseded collector fence and an operator-debug voter-dispatch snippet as executable bash fences; the Step 2b.5 rc=2 path and the validator-autofix branches direct capture-then-append command pairs. Nothing durably prevents new raw fences from creeping back in.

**Proposal.**

- `design-step1d5.sh --mode collect` absorbs brainstorm collection, the dirty-tree checkpoint, and failure logging for launched externals; the entry guard completes the skip path itself so entry plus complete collapse to one call when brainstorm is off.
- Demote the superseded `plan-review.md` collector fence and the voter-dispatch argv reference to `text` fences (documentation, not executable).
- Fold the Step 2b.5 rc=2 capture-then-append pair into `design-step2b5.sh` and the validator-autofix Warnings appends into `design-step-validator-autofix.sh`.
- Extend `scripts/test-design-structure.sh` (or agent-lint) with a check: every `bash` fence under `skills/design/` invokes a `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}` script or the shared session launcher as its first command. Provide a suppression comment for deliberate exceptions, mirroring `lint-bare-grep-probe.sh`.

**Acceptance.**

- Zero raw-Bash bash fences under `skills/design/`; the new lint fails on regression and passes on the full tree.
- Brainstorm-off path: one Bash call; brainstorm externals collection: one call after launches.
