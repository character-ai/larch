# [BUG] Codex lint-fix sandbox blocks exec_command, causing 300 s timeout and stall instead of fast-fail

## Summary

When Codex is used for the lint-fix loop (`checks lint-fix`), its sandbox policy (`workspace-write` / `--full-auto`) blocks arbitrary `exec_command` calls such as `bash -lc '... python3 python/cli.py checks run-relevant ...'`. This forces a full stall/recovery/retry cycle (re-running the entire Step 5 review loop) when the lint was already clean or could have been verified by the main orchestrator directly.
