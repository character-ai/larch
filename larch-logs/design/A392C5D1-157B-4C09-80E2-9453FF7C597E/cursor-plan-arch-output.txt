## Findings

### 1. `REVIEW_CORE_*_SH` stub harness contract missing from the port plan (blocking)

`review-core.sh` delegates gather, dispatch, collect, and threshold work through overrideable script paths:

```83:91:python/legacy_review_shell/review-core.sh
GATHER_CONTEXT_SH="${REVIEW_CORE_GATHER_CONTEXT_SH:-$SCRIPT_DIR/gather-context.sh}"
DISPATCH_PANEL_SH="${REVIEW_CORE_DISPATCH_PANEL_SH:-$SCRIPT_DIR/dispatch-panel.sh}"
COLLECT_FINDINGS_SH="${REVIEW_CORE_COLLECT_FINDINGS_SH:-$SCRIPT_DIR/collect-findings.sh}"
...
CHECK_THRESHOLD_SH="${REVIEW_CORE_CHECK_THRESHOLD_SH:-$SCRIPT_DIR/check-reviewer-failure-threshold.sh}"
```

`review_test_support.build_review_core_env` sets those variables to stub executables for the bulk of `test_review_pipeline.py` review_core coverage. The plan rewrites `review_core` in Python and updates `review_test_support.py`, but it never states whether the `REVIEW_CORE_*_SH` seams survive. A straight in-process port will fail `make py-test` unless the plan picks and documents one of the two valid strategies: preserve the env overrides at subprocess boundaries, or migrate every stub-based test to native monkeypatching.

### 2. `run_legacy` relay tests must move to `review_legacy.py`, not disappear (important)

`test_run_legacy_relays_stdout_kv_without_quiet_disable` and `test_run_legacy_relays_lib_quiet_emit_kv_without_quiet_disable` guard KV relay behavior still required by `review_aggregate.py`, `review_tally.py`, and `compose_review.py` after G1. Step 2 correctly introduces `python/review_legacy.py`, but step 8 says to replace `run_legacy` tests without requiring equivalent relay tests on the new module. That is a completeness gap for retained façade behavior.

### 3. Embedded plan-review prune cutover should be machine-audited across all assets (important)

Step 4 correctly prioritizes gzip embedded assets before prune script deletion, and the failure-mode section calls out rounds 3–4 breakage. The planned pytest only asserts `dispatch-plan-review-panel.sh` and `plan-review-loop.sh` (mirroring the collector regression at `test_embedded_plan_review_loop_uses_migrated_collector`). Given `_LEGACY_ASSETS` holds many embedded bodies and step 4 allows other prune consumers, a single-loop audit over all decoded assets is the safer minimum before step 6 deletes `scripts/reviewer-prune.sh` and `scripts/lib-prune-decision.sh`.
