## Findings

### 1. code-quality — `python/tests/report/test_report_tokens_cli.py`

The planned CLI cleanup tests cover empty scan, no-plot success, failure-before-post, and macOS preserve, but they do not cover the main non-auto-open plot path: **plots generated on a non-darwin platform**.

Current leak site at ```66:66:python/larch/report/report_tokens_cli.py``` creates the root on every analyze run. The plan’s preserve gate at ```92:94:python/larch/report/report_tokens_plot.py``` is darwin-only. The complement (delete after plot success on Linux/CI) is the default fix path and is distinct from the no-plot cases the plan lists.

Without an explicit non-darwin + `plot_paths` test, a missing platform guard in `preserve_temp_root` would reintroduce unbounded top-level growth on the most common CI path while all planned tests pass.

**Suggested revision:** Add the non-darwin plot-success `TMPDIR` assertion described in the TSV row.
