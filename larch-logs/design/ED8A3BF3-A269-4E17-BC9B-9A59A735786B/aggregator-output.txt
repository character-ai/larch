### FINDING_1: Post-ship OOS batches miss committed run-log tree on merge runs
- **Reviewer(s)**: Cursor-Arch
- **Severity**: blocking
- **Concern**: Post-ship `oos file` runs after ship's terminal log flush on `--merge`. `python/ship.py` `run_postmerge_phase` calls `finalize_postmerge_logs` (sets `post-merge-sentinel`) before returning `outcome=OK`. `oos file` then writes `run-statistics.md` and `oos-issues.ndjson` into tmpdir only. - **Suggested revisions (informational for voters; coder decides)**:
  - From Cursor-Arch: Run `oos file` before ship's final log flush (e.g. after PR prep, before merge loop) while keeping `ship.py` OOS-free via orchestrator-only invocation; or write speculative OOS batches in the pre-merge flush per NEVER #16's pre-merge guidance.
