## Edge cases

- **Stall predicate**: active when value is non-empty and not exactly `false` (matches live SKILL / stall-recovery); do not narrow to a canonical truthy table.
- **Step 18a.5 placement**: always prompt-side after gate clearance / recovery / terminal completion and before `--phase finalize`; never inside gate early-exit.
- **Post-terminal recovery**: disk `STALL_TRACKING=true` after `seed-terminal-state` is expected; orchestrator skips gate re-entry and runs finalize after 18a.5 skip.
- **Plugin root rehydration**: support both `plugin-root.env` and `session-env.sh`.
- **`--step17-emitted true`**: wrapper creates sentinel before `step18b` only.
- **Step 18 first emission**: wrapper emits markers and touches `.step17-emitted` before teardown; orchestrator re-emits from captured stdout only.
- **Empty summary**: never emit markers when `summary-final.md` is absent or empty.
- **`final-report step18b` failure**: relay `WFR_RC`, append failure best-effort, no markers; closing marks and teardown still run.
- **Marker `cat` failure**: under `set +e`, failed `cat` of `summary-final.md` produces no marker pair but must not abort; closing marks and teardown still run (mirror `step-16-17.sh`).
- **Missing `--implement-tmpdir`**: bare `final-report step18b` fails argparse before refresh or teardown; always pass `--implement-tmpdir "$IMPLEMENT_TMPDIR"`.
- **`_restore_finalize` mismatch**: ship stall/bail truthy or `STALL_STEP` drift triggers `restore-finalize-state` before teardown; missing `finalize-state.sh` with `ship-pr-state.sh` present does the same.
- **Teardown deletion**: marker body, status KVs, and teardown tail records on stdout before teardown removes `$IMPLEMENT_TMPDIR`; no post-teardown Read or tmpdir writes.
- **Teardown tail relay**: orchestrator must relay `ISSUE_URL`, `RENAME_*`, `STASH_REF`, `SENTINEL_WRITTEN`, `FINALIZE_*` from captured finalize stdout; wrapper prints them before teardown.
- **Ordering**: closing token/timing marks before teardown (#3425); `_restore_finalize` after marks, before teardown.
- **Stdout capture**: no `larch_quiet_init` in `step-18.sh`.
- **Acceptance**: two Bash fences on no-stall path, not one; do not fold 18a.5 into the wrapper.

## Failure modes

- A gate-phase wrapper that exits non-zero on stall breaks recovery routing.
- Running `--phase finalize` before Step 18a.5 skips escalation-success reporting on happy paths.
- Re-running `--phase gate` after terminal recovery loops instead of reaching teardown.
- `set -e` aborting finalize on step18b failure leaves tmpdirs and session pointers uncleared.
- **`set -e` aborting finalize on marker `cat` failure** leaves tmpdirs and session pointers uncleared (#3425 violation); marker block must run under `set +e` like `step-16-17.sh`.
- A narrowed stall truthy table misses non-canonical active layer strings and skips recovery.
- Post-teardown Read fallback or `.step17-emitted` writes fail silently after teardown.
- Omitting `--implement-tmpdir` on `final-report step18b` fails argparse and blocks the entire finalize path.
- Dropping orchestrator teardown tail relay hides tracking-issue URL and rename status from top chat even when the wrapper printed them.
- Resurrecting dormant `step-18b-final-report.sh` steps changes production token/timing artifacts vs the live SKILL path.
- `larch_quiet_init` before stall KVs or markers hides contract output from Bash capture.
- Literal `<true|false>` in the finalize fence breaks `--step17-emitted` parsing.
- Adding live `step-18.*` paths to `migrated-scripts.tsv` breaks `lint-retired-scripts`.
- Missing `agent-lint.toml` exclusion breaks `make lint` on Makefile-only harness references.
- Stale machine-reachability pins to retired helpers break agent-lint G004 after deletion.
- Dropping `_restore_finalize` or bare `implement-finalize teardown` (no `--state-file` / `--implement-tmpdir`) skips rename/issue/stash reconciliation and omits teardown tail KVs.
- Fence-only `SKILL.md` rewrite drops escalation-recording, warning-repetition, or #3425 ordering prose while harness pins still pass.
