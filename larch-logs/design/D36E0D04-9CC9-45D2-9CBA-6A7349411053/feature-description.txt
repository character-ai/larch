# review-points-overhaul-II OOS over-proposal brake (tighten materiality bar, cap per-reviewer OOS)

Phase II of the reviewer point-competition overhaul. Independent, highest immediate leverage.

**Problem (measured on v51.3.4+ run logs).** Out-of-scope (OOS) proposals are heavily over-produced and mostly discarded. In `/implement` Step-5 code review across the full-system corpus, OOS findings were **430 total, 80.2% rejected, only 9.5% accepted** — OOS volume now exceeds in-scope volume. In `/design`, OOS file-worthy share is ~13% (the Phase I umbrella #4778 flagged 27.8%). Every rejected OOS still consumed three voter evaluations. #4776 added only a diagnostic fate report; nothing throttles proposal.

**Change.**
- Tighten the materiality bar in `skills/shared/oos-acceptance-rubric.md` and the OOS-proposal instructions in the reviewer prompts (`agents/reviewer-*.md`, regenerated from `skills/shared/reviewer-templates.md`).
- Add a per-reviewer OOS-proposal cap at aggregation (the dynamic-archetype / `python/review_aggregate.py` path) so one reviewer cannot emit 10+ OOS items.
- Extend the judge-side rule in `findings_ledger.prompt_section` (already says "vote NO on oos duplicates") to also down-vote OOS that does not meet the materiality bar, not just duplicates.

**Verify.** Re-run the corpus OOS share (`/fluff-analysis`, or the band harness used in the #4778 follow-up assessment). Success = OOS-rejected share and OOS volume per run fall materially while accepted-OOS count stays roughly flat.

**Risk.** Low. No scoring-math change. Changes behavior immediately. Tests: `python/test_oos_filer.py`, `python/test_findings_ledger.py`.

**Dependencies.** None. Cheapest first win; ship independently of the severity chain.
