## Key mechanics

- `larch_quiet_init` (already run by `run-step3-review.sh`) sets `exec 3>&1`
  then redirects FD 1 -> quiet log. `emit` writes to FD 3, which the
  orchestrator captures via `_plan_review_out=$(driver)`. So the folded preview

- The driver renders the preview by capturing `emit-design-plan-preview.sh`
  stdout and re-emitting it through `emit` (robust under `LARCH_QUIET_DISABLE`,
  unlike a raw `>&3`).
- The driver MUST keep emitting machine KVs on FD 3: `test-run-step3-review.sh`
  pins them as the symlink-outer fallback (when `phase_driver_write_result_env`
  refuses a symlinked `.step3-review-result.env` and exits 1, the orchestrator
  recovers KVs from stdout). So the captured stream carries preview display +
  KV lines together; the thin orchestrator fence echoes non-KV display lines
  and parses KV lines by allowlist.
- `assert_thin_fence` (added in Phase 1) forbids the literal
  `is a symlink; refusing to source` in the scoped region and requires the
  region's entry pause guard to thread `${REPO:+--repo "$REPO"}`. The thin Step
  3 fence therefore guards the result-env read with `! -L` (no message) and

- Sentinel ownership moves to the driver: `emit-design-plan-preview.sh
  --variant step3` becomes a pure renderer; `run-step3-review.sh` checks and
