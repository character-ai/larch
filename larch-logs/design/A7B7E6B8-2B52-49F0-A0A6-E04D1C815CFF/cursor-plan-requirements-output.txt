## 1. [architecture] `plan-review-loop.sh` slot metadata wiring

The plan says to emit `--voter Claude:$VOTER_1_PATH` etc., but `plan-review-loop.sh` only reads `VOTER_PATHS_FILE` (lines 587–606) and never binds `VOTER_1_PATH` / `VOTER_2_PATH` / `VOTER_3_PATH` from dispatch output, even though `dispatch-plan-voters.sh` emits them (lines 221–233). When a middle judge fails, `plan-voter-paths.txt` drops that slot but preserves order, so positional mapping would collapse v3 into v2 (violating FINDING_16 fixed canonical slots).

**Suggested revision:** Extend the dispatch KV parse block (~567–576) to capture per-slot path/status; assemble `--voter` args from slot metadata, skipping failed slots without compaction.
