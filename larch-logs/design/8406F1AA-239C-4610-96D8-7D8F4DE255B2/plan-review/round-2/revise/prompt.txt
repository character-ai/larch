## Failure modes

- If `NEXT_ACTION=continue` is emitted before a clean internal re-check, the workflow may skip required verification. Tests must assert the repair loop runs checks after lint-fix.

- If tail paths or `LINT_FIX_LEDGER_*` keys are not propagated/emitted, main-agent-edit branches lose escalation recording and operator-visible context.
- If Step 5 `continue` re-invokes checks or enters the stall path, MAV/coder defer record→commit→resume is broken.
- If Step 5 stall prose loses the record-only resume path, review timing and durable stall state may be incomplete.
