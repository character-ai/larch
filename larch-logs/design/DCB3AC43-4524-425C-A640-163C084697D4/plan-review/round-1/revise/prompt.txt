# review-points-overhaul Reward unique finding coverage to preserve panel diversity

Give a small bonus for being the sole finder of an accepted finding (experimental).

**Problem.** Deduplication awards every contributing reviewer the same `+1` for a merged finding. Nothing rewards being the *only* reviewer who caught a real bug. So reviewers are incentivized to converge on the obvious findings (a guaranteed shared `+1`) rather than dig for the non-obvious one only they would surface. That works against panel diversity, which is the entire reason for running multiple reviewers.

**Proposed change.** Add a small uniqueness bonus for the sole finder of an accepted finding. **Caveat (why experimental):** this can incentivize withholding a finding to "claim" uniqueness, or gaming around dedup. Gate it behind monitoring and treat as an experiment, not a default-on change.

**Affected surfaces.** Dedup credit-sharing in the tally layer, scoreboard, `docs/point-competition.md` (the shared-credit rule).

**Depends on.** The base scoring changes (land the point structure and neutral pricing first; uniqueness is the last layer on top).
