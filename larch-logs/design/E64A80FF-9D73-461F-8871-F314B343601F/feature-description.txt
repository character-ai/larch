Part of umbrella #3133 (extract `/design` deterministic logic into phase-driver scripts).

**Impact rank: 4 of 6.** Runs on every plan emit **and** on re-emits after revision (Gate B / discussion sub-rounds), on both SIMPLE and HARD.

## Region owned

The Step 2b deterministic actions **after** the LLM authors `plan.txt`:

- `ACTION=EMIT_PLAN` (via `design-driver.sh`; writes `diff-lines.txt`, fails closed on missing/malformed `diff_lines:`)
- HARD `plan.txt-original` snapshot (`snapshot-plan-round.sh write-original`)
- plan-command validator (`invoke-plan-validator.sh` → `ACTION=VALIDATE_PLAN_COMMANDS`), skipped when `review_budget=quick`
- the validator-result KV parse (`VALIDATE_STATUS`, defect/skip/unsafe-token counts, log file)

## Current inline cost

3 separate fences → 1; ~40 inline lines in `skills/design/SKILL.md` Step 2b (the EMIT_PLAN fence, the HARD-snapshot fence, the validator KV-parse fence).

## Responsibility

Fuse the three into one call that runs EMIT_PLAN, conditionally snapshots for HARD, conditionally runs the validator, and emits the combined status — calling `design-driver.sh` ACTIONs internally (no duplication).

## Stops before (LLM boundary)

The **plan-command-validator-defects** `AskUserQuestion` (shared Fix-and-retry / Override / Cancel body), then Step 2b.5 (plan-size gate, which has its own hard/partition prompt).

## Machine output

`EMIT_PLAN_STATUS`, `VALIDATE_STATUS` (+ `VALIDATE_DEFECT_COUNT`, `VALIDATE_SKIPPED_COUNT`, `VALIDATE_UNSAFE_TOKEN_COUNT`, `VALIDATE_LOG_FILE`), snapshot status. `defects-found` is **not** a script failure — surface it as a status the orchestrator gates on.
