# review-points-overhaul-III Calibration incentive: tie voter standing to severity High Rate

Phase III work item. Umbrella: #5459. **Cornerstone.**

## Problem

#5124 changed the points rule to majority-YES severity aggregation, but the **input** is still flat. Across the v51.3.19+ corpus, voters tag **80–100% of accepted in-scope findings major/blocker**. `/voter-calibration` (full corpus) flags `codex-plan-fidelity` (High Rate **1.000**), `codex-pragmatism` (**0.966**), and `design Cursor` (**0.901**, 5,763 votes) as `Uncalibrated`; the cursor-* code-review trio sits at 0.86.

**Root cause** (noted in #5128): a voter's severity tag sets the **proposer's** points, not the voter's own. Nothing rewards calibrated, honest tagging, so everyone tags major.

## Change

Introduce a **voter-side incentive**. Tie a voter's own standing/score to their severity calibration, reusing the `High Rate` signal #5125 already computes (`compute_voter_severity_distribution`). Reward a calibrated severity distribution; dock chronic all-major tagging. The goal is to make truthful, calibrated reporting the dominant strategy for the voter, not just the proposer.

## Guardrails

- Keep panel severity **judge-set**. Do not re-expose proposer-controlled `body_severity` (gameable).
- Surgical change; the `High Rate` / `Uncalibrated` columns already exist.

## Surfaces

- `python/voting.py` (`compute_voter_severity_distribution`, `accepted_finding_points_from_severities`, severity scoreboard)
- `docs/point-competition.md`

## Acceptance

- A voter who tags everything major earns lower standing than one who calibrates.
- Measurable drop in panel `High Rate` over the subsequent corpus.
- `make lint &amp;&amp; make py-lint &amp;&amp; make py-test`.

## Dependencies

Builds on #5125 (landed; supplies the `High Rate` signal). No live blocker.
