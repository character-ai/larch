Phase III work item. Umbrella: #5459.

## Problem

#5123's OOS over-proposal brake was prompt-level (tighter materiality rubric + a per-reviewer cap in instructions) and **did not bite**. Across the v51.3.19+ corpus, OOS is still:

- **~51% of implement findings** (still exceeds in-scope volume)
- **~79% rejected, ~8% accepted**

That is statistically identical to the pre-Phase-II baseline (80.2% rejected / 9.5% accepted). Voters still spend most of their OOS effort rejecting low-materiality proposals.

## Change

Move the brake from prompt-level to **structural**. Pick one (or both):

- **(a) Pipeline-enforced per-reviewer OOS cap.** Hard-limit OOS findings per reviewer in the aggregation/classification step, not in prompt text.
- **(b) Pre-vote scope gate.** Drop OOS-classified findings before the voting panel sees them, so voter effort is not spent rejecting them. Keep an audit trail of dropped OOS for `/issue` follow-up.

## Surfaces

- `python/review_pipeline.py` (OOS classification / aggregation)
- reviewer templates, `skills/shared/oos-acceptance-rubric.md`

## Acceptance

- OOS share of findings drops below in-scope volume.
- OOS rejection rate falls (fewer junk OOS reach voting).
- `make lint && make py-lint && make py-test`.

## Dependencies

Independent. Edits the OOS path, not the scoring math.
