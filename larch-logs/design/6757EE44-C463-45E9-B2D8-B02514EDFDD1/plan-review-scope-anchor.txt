Allocate future reviewer token budget by precision-value, not raw cumulative score.

**Problem.** `docs/point-competition.md` ("Future Plans") states token allocation will be weighted by reviewer score. Cumulative score is `Σ(accepted − rejected)`. That rewards volume, not precision.

Worked example: Reviewer A raises 100 findings → 48 accept, 24 reject = **+24**, precision 48%. Reviewer B raises 20 → 15 accept, 1 reject = **+14**, precision 75%. A outscores B despite 5x the noise. Wiring tokens to cumulative score funds the noisier reviewer and inverts the stated goal (more useful, fewer useless suggestions).

**Proposed change.** Allocate by a precision-value signal, not the raw sum. Candidates: acceptance rate, net-score-per-finding, or `precision × mean-accepted-value`. Pick before any token-allocation code ships.

**Evidence.** Across 902 `/implement` runs, in-scope findings reclassified under current rules split 48.1% accepted / 17.0% neutral / 34.9% rejected (n=17,555). High-volume low-precision behavior is the dominant equilibrium.

**Affected surfaces.** `docs/point-competition.md` (Future Plans), `skills/shared/voting-protocol.md` (scoreboard), wherever token allocation is implemented.

**Depends on.** Value-weighted points (so "value" is defined) and voter calibration (don't spend real budget on an unvalidated judge signal).

## Proposed Design Outline

### Goals
- Revise the token-allocation policy so future reviewer budget is allocated by precision-value (net-score-per-finding), not raw cumulative score.
- Add a short rationale to `docs/point-competition.md`: precision over volume, with the issue's worked example.
- State the two preconditions before allocation ships: value-weighted points (defines "value") and voter calibration (validates the signal).

### Non-goals

### Approach sketch
- In `docs/point-competition.md` "Future Plans", replace the score-weighted sentence with a precision-value statement, then add a short rationale subsection (precision-vs-volume, worked example, dependency note).

- Define net-score-per-finding inline: net competition score (Σ accepted − rejected, with the +2 major/blocker weight) divided by the reviewer's finding count.
