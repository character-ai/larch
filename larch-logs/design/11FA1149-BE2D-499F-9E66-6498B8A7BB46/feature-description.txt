# review-points-overhaul-II Make precision signal load-bearing via the reviewer prune gate

Phase II. The realistic, reality-grounded form of Phase I #4771 (token allocation), which shipped docs-only.

**Problem.** Value-weighted points feed only the advisory scoreboard. The only live decision point that consumes finding outcomes is `reviewer_prune_filter` (`python/review_pipeline.py`), and it uses the **unweighted** `accepted_sum - rejected_sum` plus a 1/3 acceptance floor. Panel size is a hard constant (`_PLAN_VOTER_PANEL_SIZE = 3`); there is no live consumer of net-score-per-finding.

**Change.** Once the severity signal is validated (the measurement item), replace the unweighted net in the prune gate with the value-weighted net (high-severity accepts count double; keep the 1/3 floor). Extend the prune ledger (`_read_classification_counts`, `_prune_ledger_header`) to carry weighted points.

**Honest caveats from the data.**
- Pruning is idle in `/implement` (activates only rounds 3–4; 28/38 corpus runs stop at round 1; it fired in 2/44 runs). Wiring it helps `/design` (reaches rounds 4–5), not implement.
- For implement leverage, the real lever is initial panel composition (the scout-archetype selection, `scout-archetype-yield.tsv`) keyed to a persistent cross-run precision signal. Bigger design; track separately. This is the honest reading of "allocate by precision-value."
- Do NOT value-weight pruning before the severity-rubric item lands — with today's inflated severity it just multiplies noise by two.

**Verify.** Prune decisions on `/design` rounds 3–4 change in the expected direction (low-precision reviewers dropped sooner) without dropping high-value-but-low-volume reviewers. Tests: `python/test_review_pipeline.py`, `python/test_plan_review_round.py`.

**Risk.** Medium. Gated on the measurement item.

**Dependencies.** Blocked by the severity-measurement item (and transitively by the severity-rubric item).
