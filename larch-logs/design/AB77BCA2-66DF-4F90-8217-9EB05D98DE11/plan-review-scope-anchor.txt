# contract-unification [FEATURE] step-8 grammar unification: assessment vocab + ship result-env

Part of the #6997 partition. Child of umbrella #7014. Blocked by the foundation piece. Converts the two step-8 adapters and removes their token-grammar forks so Bash keeps zero token knowledge.

​#### Problem

Two grammar forks ride on the step-8 scripts. `step-8-assessment.sh:334-377` re-types the full ASSESSMENT_RESULTS state/reason vocabulary that Python already owns (`architectural_assessment.py:750-831`, `config.py:95-97`). The ship outcome crosses four serialization hops: `ship_result.emit_result` JSON, a Bash stdout sniffer at `step-8-ship.sh:153-178`, a handoff env re-parsed by `dispatch_ship.ship_route_exit_main`, then a grep at `step-8-assessment.sh:193-207`.

​#### Goal

Convert the two step-8 adapters to thin `bgjob adapt` wrappers, and delete both grammar forks. Bash holds no token vocabulary; the ship child writes its result env directly.

​#### Scope

- Convert `skills/implement/scripts/step-8-assessment.sh` and `step-8-ship.sh` adapters to the thin `bgjob adapt` pattern.
- Move ASSESSMENT_RESULTS state/reason validation behind a cli verb; delete the Bash-retyped vocabulary at `step-8-assessment.sh:334-377`.
- Have the ship child write its result env directly; delete the stdout sniffer at `step-8-ship.sh:153-178` and the assessment-side grep at `step-8-assessment.sh:193-207`; collapse the redundant serialization hops.

​#### Non-goals

The KEY=value codec work (#6999), which lands after this partition.

​#### Acceptance

- Assessment and ship outcomes flow identically to before, with the token vocabulary owned only in Python.
- The stdout sniffer and the assessment-side grep are gone.
- `make lint` and `make py-test` green.

Run /design on this issue before /implement.
