## Approach

- Make final-summary emission a terminal-position contract:
  - read or capture the summary before cleanup when needed;
  - run all required cleanup, sentinel, warning, and tail-relay tool calls;
  - emit the cached verbatim summary body last, with no later tool calls or recap text.
- Keep generation unchanged. Only change prompt-side placement and outcome display.
- Preserve old artifact tolerance. Readers must accept prior `DONE`, `STALLED`, and `stalled` shapes where they already exist.

## Edge cases

- `/design` Step 6 deletes `$DESIGN_TMPDIR`: Read final summary and allowed sidecars before cleanup, then emit from the already-read content.
- `/design` plan-block write failure skips cleanup: emit the cached summary after the warning, since no cleanup tool remains.
- `/implement` Step 17 marker body exists: suppress Step 18 duplicate emission by passing the existing step17-emitted path, then emit cached Step 17 body last.
- `/implement` Step 17 marker body is absent: Step 18 remains responsible for terminal stall or recovery report markers.
- Legacy committed summaries with `- **Outcome**: stalled` or `STALLED` must still reconcile.
- Raw non-success outcomes must not gain emoji unless explicitly added later.

## Failure modes

- Emitting before cleanup will still be hidden by the current harness.
- Reading after cleanup on `/design` can lose the only local summary copy.
- Writing `.step17-emitted` too late can make Step 18 emit a duplicate body.
- Passing `--step17-emitted true` without a pending Step 17 body can suppress the only final report.
- Regexes that require exact `STALLED` can miss `❌ STALLED` and leave stale summaries unreconciled.
- Updating prompt prose without harness pins can leave green tests that do not protect the new ordering.
