# /design Step 2b: drafter wrapper owns prelude and postplan on the success path

**Problem.** Step 2b on the drafter-success path is three consecutive Bash calls with no judgment between them: `design-step2b-prelude.sh`, `design-step2b-drafter.sh`, and `design-step2b-postplan.sh --site step2b --snapshot-original`.

**Proposal.**

- Fold the prelude into `design-step2b-drafter.sh`. On drafter structural success and dirty-tree eligibility, the wrapper runs the merged postplan driver internally and emits the existing rc contract (0, 10, 12, 13) plus the drafter outcome rows.
- On drafter failure the wrapper still deletes stale `plan-summary.md`, logs the failed tool, and emits the fallback row; the orchestrator drafts inline (prose unchanged) and then runs the single retained postplan fence once. The `.step2b-postplan-inline-retry-pending` / `-done` sentinel semantics are preserved.
- Dirty-tree recovery rows (`STAGE=step-2b-drafter`, `RECOVERY_REQUIRED=true`) and their `AskUserQuestion` flow unchanged.

**Acceptance.**

- One Bash call covers Step 2b entry through postplan on the drafter-success path; the inline-fallback path keeps exactly one postplan fence.
- rc 10/12/13 operator routing byte-compatible; `test-design-postplan-emit.sh` and the structure harness extended.
