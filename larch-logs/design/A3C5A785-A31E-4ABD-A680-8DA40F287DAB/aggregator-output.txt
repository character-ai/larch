### FINDING_1: Emergency warning may use stale or empty TITLE
- **Reviewer(s)**: Cursor-Arch
- **Severity**: important
- **Concern**: The missing-designed-prefix emergency warning uses `TITLE` before `issue.json` exists, while later envelope binding gets `TITLE` from `issue.json`. This can produce an empty or stale title in byte-stable warning output.
- **Suggested revisions (informational for voters; coder decides)**:
  - From Cursor-Arch: At admission parse time, bind warning TITLE from captured admission stdout TITLE= when present; keep envelope TITLE from issue.json only after gh issue view

Drop the ambiguous empty token from the RESUME=<true|false|empty> template

  - From Cursor-dyn-harness-pin-coherence: Align `test-implement-preflight.sh` with stdout assertions (already used for malformed refusal) or grep stable substrings/`implement-preflight.md` contract literals; drop "script contains exact … with `<N>`" requirements for executable source.
