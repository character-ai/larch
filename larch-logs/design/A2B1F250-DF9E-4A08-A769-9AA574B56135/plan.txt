## Edge cases

- `mechanical_churn: true` and `mechanical_churn: false` keep current behavior.
- `mechanical_churn: 35`, `mechanical_churn: TRUE`, and `mechanical_churn:` are invalid.
- Invalid `mechanical_churn` is not treated as absent.
- Invalid `mechanical_churn` still counts as a present final-block optional trailer.
- Header-only TSV from a truly empty ballot remains non-degraded because `INSCOPE_REMAINING=0`.
- Header-only TSV from a non-empty ballot is degraded and emits `REASON=ballot-items-lost`.
- Missing classification TSV after ok tally is treated like zero data rows.
- Voter-dispatch failures, skipped-empty-findings rounds, and degraded-empty-collector rounds with zero accepted do not auto-continue unless the normalized Step 3 result carries the valid lost-ballot terminal shape.
- A clean later round must not inherit stale `REASON=ballot-items-lost`.

## Failure modes

- If awk emits the diagnostic but parse output line 4 remains `false`, `check-plan-size.sh` may still fall back to false.
- If invalid `mechanical_churn:` is not recorded in the final block, earlier valid trailers above it may be lost.
- If `plan-review-loop.sh` sets only `REASON` and not `LOOP_REASON`, stdout, env files, and summaries may drop `ballot-items-lost`.
- If `plan-review-loop.sh` overwrites `LOOP_REASON=ballot-items-lost` in terminal mapping, continuation will stop as generic degraded zero-accepted.
- If `run-step3-review.sh` drops `REASON`, the live Step 3 continuation decision may stop as `small-clean`.
- If `review-design-step3-loop.sh` overwrites `.step3-review-result.env` without `REASON`, loop envelopes may drop the signal.
- If `plan-review-continuation.sh` keys on bare degraded plus zero accepted, dispatch-degraded or empty-collector rounds may burn review rounds until cap.
- If `INSCOPE_REMAINING` is not persisted to `round-summary.env`, post-mortem audits cannot prove why the panel degraded.
