# Feature: Get rid of trivial mode in /design, consolidating SIMPLE and HARD

## Scope (decided in Step 1)

- **Two tiers only**: SIMPLE and HARD. Old TRIVIAL absorbed into new SIMPLE for the no-sketch behavior. Old SIMPLE (2 generic sketches + full review) is removed entirely.
- **run-params.json schema collapse**: single enum `design_classification: SIMPLE | HARD`. Fields `quick_mode`, `review_budget`, `workflow_path`, `sketch_budget`, `design_classification_source`, `design_classification_reason` are all dropped. Schema version bumps to 2. Keep `partition_requested` and `brainstorm_requested`.
- **`--trivial` argv** → hard error before Step 0. No deprecation warning, no silent upgrade, no backward-compat shim.
- **Tier-gate UX**: 2-option AskUserQuestion (SIMPLE / HARD); descriptions reflect per-tier emphasis (simplicity vs thoroughness).

  - **SIMPLE**: Step 2a sketches skipped (write sentinel stubs); Step 2a.5 dialectic skipped. Full external review panel runs (10 static + up to 12 dynamic). Plan-command validator runs. Gate C "Re-run review panel" capped at **3 total runs**. Designer + reviewer emphasis: **simplicity, minimizing changes**.
