# contract-unification [FEATURE] Ban pylint skip-file in runtime modules

​#### Problem

The duplication gates are being dodged exactly where duplication concentrates. `python/larch/issue/_oos.py:3` carries `# pylint: skip-file` (verified), exempting the OOS-parsing hotspot — the module at the center of the FINDING/OOS grammar fan-out — from the R0801 duplicate-code gate CI relies on. `design_step_log.py:25` documents a helper deliberately restructured to "avoid pylint R0801 duplicate-code across the two modules". A gate that can be silently opted out of underreports precisely in the worst files.

​#### Goal

An engine-based lint (per #6988) banning `pylint: skip-file` and blanket per-file R0801 disables in `python/larch/` runtime modules, with a reason-bearing baseline for any grandfathered row and a burndown of `_oos.py`'s entry by paying its lint debt. Complements #6968 (prevention cost gate) by keeping the existing gates airtight while new ones are added.

​#### Size

Small: ~100-150 lines of rule plus tests; closes the gate-evasion channel.
