### FINDING_1: Materialize wrapper retirement lacks explicit delete steps
- **Reviewer(s)**: Cursor-Arch, Cursor-Innovation
- **Severity**: important
- **Concern**: The plan explicitly deletes `step-architectural-guidelines-read.sh` and `step-architectural-guidelines-read.md`, but the `### UPDATED:` entries for `step-architectural-guidelines-materialize.sh` and `step-architectural-guidelines-materialize.md` are empty. An implementer can ship the `prepare` fold while the old materialize wrapper remains on disk and still referenced in sibling-contract prose, leaving two Phase A materialization entrypoints and defeating the stated retirement goal. Relying on `residual-bash-paths.txt` removal alone is easy to miss.
- **Suggested revisions (informational for voters; coder decides)**:
  - From Cursor-Arch: Mirror the read retirement bullets: delete `skills/implement/scripts/step-architectural-guidelines-materialize.sh` and `step-architectural-guidelines-materialize.md` with an explicit no-shim note.
  - From Cursor-Innovation: Mirror the read retirement bullets: delete `skills/implement/scripts/step-architectural-guidelines-materialize.sh` and `step-architectural-guidelines-materialize.md` with no shim, and list both paths wherever read deletions are enumerated.

### FINDING_2: prepare.md overclaims write-staged and durable-pin ownership
- **Reviewer(s)**: Cursor-Arch, Cursor-Innovation
- **Severity**: important
- **Concern**: The planned `prepare.md` sibling doc says Python owns staged assessment writes and durable pinning, but `prepare_main` only invalidates, reads guidelines, and materializes the diff. `write-staged-assessment` and `pin-note-from-staged` remain separate fences with prompt-side judgment between materialize and write-staged, which the issue scope requires. Misdocumented ownership can cause an implementer to fold those steps into `prepare` or drop the write-staged fence.
- **Suggested revisions (informational for voters; coder decides)**:
  - From Cursor-Arch: Limit prepare.md ownership to invalidation, parsing, path checks, and diff snapshot metadata emission. Point staged writes and durable pinning to `step-architectural-guidelines-write-staged.md` and `step-architectural-guidelines-pin-from-staged.md`.
  - From Cursor-Innovation: Limit prepare.md ownership to invalidation, guideline parsing, diff snapshot metadata, and stdout KVs. Point write-staged and pin-from-staged ownership at their existing wrappers and SKILL prose only.

### FINDING_3: prepare_main advertises --output but plan does not honor it
- **Reviewer(s)**: Codex-Arch, Codex-Innovation
- **Severity**: important
- **Concern**: `prepare_main` parses `--output`, but the plan never says to write through it. A caller can invoke `architectural-guidelines prepare` with `--output`, get silent success with no file written, break the command contract, and leave the combined verb incomplete relative to the existing `materialize-diff` write path.
- **Suggested revisions (informational for voters; coder decides)**:
  - From Codex-Arch: Either carry the existing materialize-diff file-write path into `prepare_main` or drop `--output` from the new verb, its wrapper, and its tests.
  - From Codex-Innovation: Explicitly write `diff_text` to `--output` the same way `materialize_diff_main` does, or remove `--output` from `prepare_main` if the new verb should not support it.
