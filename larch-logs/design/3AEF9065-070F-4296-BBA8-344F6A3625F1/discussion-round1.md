## Decision 7: B5 step-marker proximity tolerance
- **Question**: What proximity window (K lines) should anchor each MANDATORY directive to its step marker in SKILL.md?
- **Resolution**: Use lenient window of **80 lines** below each `<!-- step:N -->` marker. Rationale: SKILL.md steps vary widely in size, and a too-tight window risks false positives. Catches the spirit of "moved out of its intended step" without overconstraining. The manifest enriches each `orchestrator-inline` SKILL.md row with optional `step_markers` column listing the step IDs that must each have a directive.
- **Source**: codebase (`<!-- step:` markers in SKILL.md range from a few lines to ~80 lines apart based on `command grep -n '<!-- step:' skills/design/SKILL.md`)
