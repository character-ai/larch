## Summary

The plan’s minimum-change instrumentation model (launcher hooks, count-only TSV, best-effort append, `write-round` allowlist, `review log-phase` sibling publish) fits `/implement` Step 5 and standalone `/review` well: outputs already live in the round or review tmpdir that gets committed.

The main gap is **design plan-review path routing**. Specialists naturally land in `plan-review/round-N/`, but voters and the findings aggregator still emit under `$DESIGN_TMPDIR` root in current code, and the plan’s launcher instructions reinforce `output.parent` without a round-aware override. That conflicts with the plan’s own commit layout, edge-case note, and issue acceptance for all panel slot kinds.

Secondary gap: the plan introduces `panel_prompt_size_artifact_for_output()` but does not require launchers to use it, which weakens the single routing authority the design fix needs.

Implement and review publication paths, best-effort logging, and count-only storage are otherwise aligned with the spec. No rendered-prompt or tmpdir-path columns are proposed in the TSV schema; `_stage_round_artifact` / design publish redaction remain safety nets on committed bytes.
