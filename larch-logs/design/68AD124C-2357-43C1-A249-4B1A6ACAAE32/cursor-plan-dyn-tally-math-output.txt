## Mechanical tally audit

**Exact rule (proposed `tally-plan-assessor.sh`, plan.txt:87-89):**
- `successful` = count of assessors with parseable BETTER/WORSE/TIE (TIE **included** in `successful`, excluded from `worse_count`/`better_count`).
- WORSE-majority when: `(successful==3 AND worse_count>better_count)` OR `(successful==2 AND worse_count==2)` OR `(successful==1 AND worse_count==1)`.
- Otherwise NOT_WORSE.

**TIE resolution:** TIE votes count toward `successful` / `EFFECTIVE_ASSESSORS` but not toward `worse_count` or `better_count`.

## Numbered findings

1. **[correctness] `plan.txt:87-89` vs `plan.txt:203` — 0-2-1 rule/expectation mismatch.** Mechanical application: successful=3, worse=1, better=0, tie=2 → `(3 AND 1>0)` → WORSE-majority. Edge case expects NOT_WORSE with rationale "TIE does not count as WORSE." **Suggested revision:** adopt `worse_count*2 > successful` for the full panel (or `worse_count>=2` when successful==3).

2. **[correctness] `plan.txt:5,85-89` — "strict WORSE > BETTER" vs tie-aware majority.** The Approach and tally preamble claim Round 1 D1 + original "majority" semantics, but the 3-successful clause is pure `worse_count>better_count`, which treats a single WORSE as decisive whenever no BETTER votes exist, regardless of TIE count. That is not majority among three successful assessors. **Suggested revision:** replace the three disjunctive clauses with one majority predicate over `successful`, preserving unanimous-WORSE behavior for degraded 2- and 1-assessor panels as special cases of the same formula.

3. **[correctness] `plan.txt:137,203` — test contract collision.** Line 203 requires pinning 0-2-1 as NOT_WORSE; line 137 requires "strict WORSE > BETTER boundary at every relevant boundary." Those cannot both pass if the tally implements lines 87-88 verbatim. **Suggested revision:** after rule fix, add an explicit `0-2-1 → NOT_WORSE` test case and rename "WORSE > BETTER boundary" tests to "strict majority boundary."

4. **[correctness] `feature-description.txt:5,28` vs `plan.txt:85-89` — original majority-vote language.** Problem statement says "majority-vote"; embedded issue plan uses `WORSE > BETTER`; proposed plan adds 3/2/1/0 sub-rules. For **0-3-0 all-TIE**: both issue expectation and mechanical rule agree on NOT_WORSE. For **1-1-1**: both agree on NOT_WORSE. For **0-2-1**: neither bare WORSE>BETTER nor the proposed 3-successful clause matches the stated NOT_WORSE intent. **Suggested revision:** normative assessor.md should define WORSE-majority as strict majority among successful assessors, explicitly noting TIE inclusion in the denominator only.

5. **[latent] `plan.txt:203` vs `feature-description.txt:194` — tuple notation drift.** Plan `0-0-3` means three WORSE; issue `0-3-0` means three TIE. Implementers can transpose tuples. **Suggested revision:** always label `(BETTER,TIE,WORSE)` explicitly in assessor.md and tests.

6. **[latent] `plan.txt:203` — missing 0-3-0 all-TIE in plan Edge cases.** Issue lists it; plan relies on harness "all-tie" without naming 0-3-0 in Edge cases. Rule outcome is consistent (NOT_WORSE), but documentation is incomplete. **Suggested revision:** add `0-3-0 (all TIE) → NOT_WORSE` to plan Edge cases for parity with the issue.
