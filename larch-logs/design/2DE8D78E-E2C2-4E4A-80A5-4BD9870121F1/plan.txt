## Failure modes

- Shared reads retry transient failures. Caller tests must mock the wrapper rather than depend on retry call counts.
- `issue_list_read` raises `ShipError` for command and parse failures. Uncaught exceptions would change CLI contracts, so every status-emitting boundary must translate them.
- Audit concurrency preflight is the exception: it must deliberately convert `ShipError` to an empty list and continue.
- Collapsing transport and parse errors could change warning codes. Preserve distinctions where callers expose them.
- Missing fields could silently weaken filtering or reports. Tests must assert every wrapper field sequence.
