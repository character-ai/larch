- Keep bail semantics: a branch mismatch still routes the `main-branch-post-dispatch` reason to Step 12d with the same operator warning and the `python3 python/cli.py run-log append-entry` Warnings append.
- Keep today's carve-out: no probe and no assertion on `claude_fallback`.

**Acceptance.**

- The phantom-probe registry note and `scripts/test-step2-dispatch.sh` family updated accordingly.
