1. **[correctness] `scripts/dispatch-with-waterfall.sh:250-266`**  
The proposed `if grep -Eq ...; then ...; else failed+=("$idx"); continue; fi` shape conflicts with the plan’s own failure-mode claim that invalid EREs error out. With `set -e`, an `if` condition suppresses abort behavior, so grep rc `2` is handled the same as rc `1`. Capture `$?` and only waterfall on rc `1`; rc `>1` should be a dispatcher error.
