## Approach

Add the two bug-fix review questions as prompt-side guidance. Pin their exact text in the existing invariant harness. Do not add runtime gating or new harness machinery.

The plan-review instruction must apply only when both conditions hold:

- The source issue carries `[BUG]`.
- The firm plan files touch the recovery surfaces named by G-Fix-2.

It must require either a named offline harness or test case that replays the failure, or an explicit one-line no-repro justification.

The diff-review instruction must ask reviewers of `[BUG]` fixes to classify the change as class-wide or instance-only. Require the sibling sites checked, or a statement that a grep found none.

## Edge cases

- A `[BUG]` issue that changes ordinary product or documentation files must not imply that recovery-specific reproduction guidance applies.
- A recovery-path feature that is not sourced from a `[BUG]` issue must not be described as subject to the bug-only checklist.
- A legitimate live-state exception satisfies the prompt when the plan gives a one-line no-repro reason.
- A reviewer may find no sibling sites. The prompt must accept an explicit grep-found-none result.
- Generated reviewer content must remain synchronized with its canonical template.

## Failure modes

- Overbroad wording could make every bug plan appear to require a recovery harness.
- Vague wording could let a plan claim “tests added” without naming the replaying case.
- Editing only `agents/code-reviewer.md` would be overwritten by regeneration.
- Unpinned prompt text could disappear during later renderer or template refactors.
- A malformed G-Fix heading could prevent the coverage indexer from seeing the new guideline.
