## Edge cases

- A valid completed result must emit DONE instead of launching a duplicate child.
- A live identity-valid job must reattach without wrapper-specific liveness decisions.
- Unsafe, malformed, expired-live, or identity-unverifiable registry state must fail closed through stable adapter errors.
- Resume flags and public argv must reach the child unchanged.
- Child failure must still publish `BGJOB_RC` without allowing stale legacy sidecar KVs to satisfy the current run.
- Merge-env paths must remain regular, non-symlink files under `DESIGN_TMPDIR`.

## Failure modes

- Missing `DESIGN_TMPDIR` or plugin-root rehydration must return a machine-readable adapter error.
- Failure to publish required child KVs must prevent normal Step 3, Gate C, or Step 5c continuation.
- Adapter reattachment must not erase authoritative result envelopes or relaunch terminal work.
- Child-side merge publication failure must produce a non-zero bgjob result rather than a partial success envelope.
