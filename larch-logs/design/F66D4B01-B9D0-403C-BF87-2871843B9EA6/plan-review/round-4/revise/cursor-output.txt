## Failure modes

- Shipping with terminal libs still present despite DoD (treating libs as residual) leaves E3 incomplete.
- Leaving tracked `*.inc.bash` outside the manifest/compliance pass leaves E3 incomplete even when `.sh` inventory is clean.
- Deleting a lib or include before cutting non-test runtime `source` callers breaks hooks or kept wrappers at runtime.
- Retiring `lib-quiet.sh` without per-hook FD-3 replacement in `deny-edit-write.sh` weakens `/research` read-only enforcement.
- Leaving `scripts/extract-closes-issue-from-pr.sh` (or other non-thin utilities) outside the residual manifest leaves the DoD incomplete even when linters narrow correctly.
- Over-narrowing pre-commit or CI globs can drop shellcheck coverage for the combine-issues helper, manifest-listed includes, or kept hooks.
- Appending `python/migrated-scripts.tsv` rows while prose still references deleted paths fails `make lint-retired-scripts` and blocks merge.
- Adding a Bash enumerator script expands residual Bash beyond the allowed inventory categories.
- Hand-editing CI shards instead of using the rebalance workflow can desync harness coverage.
- CI shellcheck still scanning repo-wide `.sh` while pre-commit narrows creates a false sense of uniform residual scope.
- `residual-bash paths` without `--root` breaks `test-lint-bash32.sh`, `test-lint-awk-multibyte-regex.sh`, and `test-lint-renderer-substitution-safety.sh` fixture trees.
