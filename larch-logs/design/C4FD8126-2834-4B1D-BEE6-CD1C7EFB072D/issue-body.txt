Narrow Codex Step-2 implementer --add-dir grant (FINDING_17, split from #3305)

Split from #3305. The Round 1 scope decision on the #3305 design narrowed that issue to the review-and-fix pre-coder snapshot subsystem (FINDING_6/9/11/13); FINDING_17 is tracked separately here.

## Design constraint: mirror #3305's documented approach

**Blocked by #3305.** This issue and #3305 narrow the same species of Codex `--add-dir` grant (the Step 2 implementer launcher here; the Step 5 review-and-fix coder / pre-coder snapshot subsystem in #3305). The `/design` run for this issue MUST adopt the same grant-narrowing approach that #3305's design lands on — the `--add-dir` scoping pattern, the dedicated-output-subdir layout, any shared helper, and the launcher-parity / harness-coverage treatment — rather than inventing a parallel convention. Design (and ideally implement) #3305 first, then mirror its decided approach here so the two launch sites stay uniform.

## Problem

`scripts/launch-codex-implement.sh` (the /implement Step 2 implementer launcher) grants the entire session tmpdir to the sandboxed Codex process via `--add-dir "$SESSION_TMPDIR"` (~line 335), where `$SESSION_TMPDIR` equals `$IMPLEMENT_TMPDIR`. That is wider than the Step 5 coder grant (`--add-dir "$round_dir"` in `skills/review-and-fix/scripts/review-and-fix.sh`). The over-broad write grant lets the implementer write anywhere in `$IMPLEMENT_TMPDIR`, including orchestrator-trusted control files (`session-env.sh`, the plan file, the feature file).

## Why split from #3305

FINDING_17 is pre-existing and outside the #3288 diff. Narrowing it restructures the working /implement Step 2 output layout — the Codex-written `manifest.json`, `qa-pending.json`, and the `--output-last-message` transcript all currently sit directly in `$IMPLEMENT_TMPDIR` — which carries independent regression risk to the core implement path. It belongs in its own design/implement cycle, not bundled with the snapshot-subsystem hardening.

## Suggested direction (for /design to refine)

- Relocate the Codex-written Step 2 outputs (`manifest.json`, `qa-pending.json`, `--output-last-message` transcript) into a dedicated subdir, e.g. `$IMPLEMENT_TMPDIR/codex-step2-out/`, and grant `--add-dir` to only that subdir (keeping the existing `--add-dir "$PWD"` for repo writes). Codex `--full-auto` (workspace-write) still reads plan/feature inputs anywhere; only writes are confined.
- Update `skills/implement/scripts/step2-implement.sh` (path computation ~lines 256-260) and all readers of those paths.
- Keep the launcher's "manifest and qa-pending share a parent dir" assertion intact (now the new subdir) and extend it to cover the transcript.
- Parity note: Cursor's launcher (`scripts/launch-cursor-implement.sh`) uses `--workspace "$PWD"`, not `--add-dir`, so there is no symmetric grant to narrow. Document this as an intentional asymmetry per `.claude/rules/external-tool-launcher-parity.md`, not a parallel change.
- Same-PR harness coverage required per `.claude/rules/launcher-argv-test-coverage.md`: `skills/implement/scripts/test-codex-implementer.sh` and `test-step2-dispatch.sh`.
