The batch row format, `$IMPL_TMPDIR` usage inside `refresh-run-logs.sh`, `[ -f ]` guard requirement, `|| true` requirement, and placement after the post-merge short-circuit are all addressed by the written plan.

The missing integration point is that `refresh-run-logs.sh` is a pre-push refresh path. On the exit-3/exit-4 bail paths, `restore-finalize-state.sh` writes `$IMPLEMENT_TMPDIR/final-bail-reason.txt` during Step 18, after those pre-push refreshes. Step 18 then refreshes token/final-report artifacts and runs teardown, but does not call `refresh-run-logs.sh` or another `larch-log.sh write --batch final-bail-reason` plus commit before cleanup.
