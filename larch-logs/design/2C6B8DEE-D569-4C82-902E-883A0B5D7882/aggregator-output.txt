- **Severity**: important

- **Suggested revisions (informational for voters; coder decides)**:
  - From Codex-Arch: Add `skills/design/references/plan-review.md` to the plan and update the competition notice and competition scoring text to the same strict-majority YES-voter language.
  - From Cursor-Innovation: Add ### UPDATED: skills/design/references/plan-review.md with strict-majority +2 wording in both the Competition notice blockquote and the Competition scoring paragraph, mirroring skills/shared/voting-protocol.md
  - From Codex-Pragmatic: Add skills/design/references/plan-review.md to the plan and change the competition notice and scoring text to strict-majority-of-YES-voters wording while preserving panel severity and body_severity exclusion
  - From Codex-Requirements: Add UPDATED: skills/design/references/plan-review.md and replace both any-YES descriptions with strict majority of YES-voter high severities while preserving panel severity and body_severity exclusion

### FINDING_2: Corpus re-score marked optional despite binding Verify criterion
- **Reviewer(s)**: Codex-Arch, Codex-Innovation, Codex-Pragmatic, Codex-Requirements
- **Severity**: important
- **Concern**: The plan treats the committed corpus re-score as optional even though the issue's verification criterion requires re-scoring the committed corpus and observing that the +2 share among accepted findings develops real, stable spread (variance well above ~0). With optional verification, unit tests can pass and the PR can merge without checking whether the intended scoring signal changed at all, leaving the Phase II success criterion unverifiable.
- **Suggested revisions (informational for voters; coder decides)**:
  - From Codex-Arch: Make the corpus re-score a required verification step using existing report or tally tooling. Do not add a new harness unless existing tooling cannot perform the check.
  - From Codex-Innovation: Make the corpus re-score a required verification step and require reporting the observed spread or the exact blocker if existing tooling cannot run
  - From Codex-Pragmatic: Make corpus re-score a required verification step after tests, or require recording a blocking reason if existing tooling cannot run
  - From Codex-Requirements: Move corpus re-score out of Optional and require replaying existing report or tally tooling against committed classification TSVs, then record that the +2 share is no longer near-flat without tuning to a target percentage

### FINDING_3: MainAgent voter prompts lack severity rubric and anchored output shape
- **Reviewer(s)**: Codex-Innovation
- **Severity**: important
- **Concern**: MainAgent voter prompts are outside `render_voter_main` and remain YES/NO-only (`skills/design/SKILL.md`, `skills/design/references/plan-review.md`, `skills/implement/references/step5-review-branches.md`). On a 0-judge design or review fallback, a single MainAgent YES for a data-loss or security finding is parsed with blank severity, so strict-majority scoring awards +1 instead of the intended +2 and bypasses the new severity calibration.
- **Suggested revisions (informational for voters; coder decides)**:
  - From Codex-Innovation: Add the same compact severity rubric and anchored output shape with SEVERITY=<blocker|major|minor|nit|uncertain> to the MainAgent vote instructions, or reuse the rendered voter prompt before writing voter-main-agent.txt
