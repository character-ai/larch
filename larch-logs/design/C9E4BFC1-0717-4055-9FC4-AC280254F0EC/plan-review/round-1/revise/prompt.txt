- Checks, CI, and the ship gates validate the artifact, not the plan. The run ended STALLED on an unrelated defect (#6521); a manual ship then closed #6514 with roughly 60 percent of its scope undone, recovered only by hand-filing #6524.

#### Proposed mechanics (for /design to refine)

1. Escalate the existing Step 2 plan-coverage probe from advisory to a graded mechanical gate keyed on firm-heading coverage. Below a low threshold, keep today's warning. At a middle threshold (for example untouched firm headings at or above 20 percent of the plan, or at or above 10 files), force-inject the plan-fidelity finder (`larch:reviewer-plan-fidelity`) into every Step 5 round for the run, exempt from pruning, regardless of tier. At a high threshold (for example at or above 50 percent or at or above 30 files), hard-gate the ship path: `ship pre-driver` refuses until a recorded scope disposition exists.
2. Define the scope disposition as a small durable artifact (tmpdir plus run log): either `proceed-partial` with a mandatory auto-filed follow-up issue capturing the untouched inventory (via `/issue`, cross-linked to the tracking issue and blocked appropriately), or `bail-rescope` routing to Step 12d and `/design`. Never a silent default.
3. Treat non-empty manifest `todos_left` on `STATUS=complete` the same way: it must flow into the disposition, the final summary, and the PR body automatically. It must not be droppable by omission.
4. Completion surfaces must reflect partial scope mechanically: with a `proceed-partial` disposition, the PR body gets `Part of #N` plus a generated deferred-inventory section instead of a closing keyword, the tracking issue is not renamed `[DONE]`, and the final summary gains a plan-coverage line (for example `Plan coverage: 24/85 firm headings; disposition: proceed-partial; follow-up #NNNN`).
5. Close the acceptance-gaming vector: the coverage comparison must run against the plan's firm headings as materialized at Step 0, never against acceptance checks whose inputs the implementation can rewrite (allowlists, baselines, skip markers).

#### Non-goals

- Reintroducing subjective mid-run scope judgment. NEVER #7 stays. The gate is mechanical: counts, thresholds, and a recorded disposition.
- Changing external implementer dispatch or the one-shot contract. The companion /design plan-size guardrail issue handles oversized plans at the source.

#### Acceptance criteria

1. Replaying the run above (61 of 85 firm headings untouched, non-empty `todos_left`) cannot reach PR creation without either a recorded `proceed-partial` disposition with an auto-filed, cross-linked follow-up issue, or a `bail-rescope` bail. Pinned by tests.
2. With a `proceed-partial` disposition, the PR body contains `Part of` rather than a closing keyword for the tracking issue, the `[DONE]` rename is suppressed, and the final summary carries the plan-coverage line. Pinned by tests on the PR-body and final-report composers.
3. The forced plan-fidelity finder appears in every Step 5 round when the middle threshold trips, exempt from reviewer pruning, at every tier. Pinned by panel-composition tests.
4. Thresholds live in `python/larch/core/config.py` with rationale comments, and `docs/workflow-lifecycle.md` documents the disposition flow.
5. `make py-lint`, `make py-test`, and affected `test-harnesses` shards pass.
