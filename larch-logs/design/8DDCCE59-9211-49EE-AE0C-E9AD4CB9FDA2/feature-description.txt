# difficulty-tiers: tiered panels, 2/2/3 ceilings, escalation, 1:30 audits

**Skills:** /implement Step 5, /review, /design plan review.

**Initiative:** difficulty-tiers, child 2 of 3. Consumes the rating from the rating-capture issue. Procedure is unchanged at every tier: findings aggregator, voting panel (#5887), pruning (#5886), and fix coder (#5888) run identically; only reviewer composition, reviewer model, and the round ceiling vary with the applied tier.

**Tier ladder (/implement Step 5 and /review; per static archetype and the at-most-one dynamic archetype of #5886):**

- **TRIVIAL**: one Codex/gpt-5.4-mini reviewer per archetype (3 static plus at most 1 dynamic = 3-4 reviewers). Ceiling 2. Codex unavailable: flip the whole panel to Cursor/composer-2.5 singles; a singles panel has no pair redundancy, so the #5886 drop-the-half rule does not apply here. Both vendors down: #5889 policy.
- **MODERATE**: Codex/gpt-5.4-mini plus Cursor/composer-2.5 pair per archetype. Ceiling 2. A missing vendor drops that half (#5886 rule).
- **HARD**: Codex/gpt-5.5 plus Cursor/composer-2.5 pair per archetype. Ceiling 3. Same drop-the-half rule.

**/design plan review:** same 2/2/3 ceilings, but every design tier keeps Codex plus Cursor pairs across the 4 static archetypes: the backtest measured 58% of accepted plan findings as Cursor-only, so shedding a vendor half is not yet safe there. v1 collapses design TRIVIAL and MODERATE to the same panel (gpt-5.4-mini plus Composer pairs, ceiling 2); HARD bumps the Codex half to gpt-5.5 with ceiling 3. The design-side escalation trigger (accepted-count per round) is settled during /design of this issue. Plan voters stay per #5887 (unchanged).

**Escalation:** when a round at TRIVIAL or MODERATE trips the existing substantiality gate (`_step5_post_round_gates`: at least 2 high-severity accepted, at least 100 structural LOC of fixes, at least 8 findings fixed, or bulk-skip), the next round runs the full panel of the next tier up. No pruning on an escalated round; escalation precedes pruning. The ceiling follows the tier in effect, so a run escalated to HARD at round 2 may reach round 3. Every escalation is logged with its trigger.

**Random audits:** 1 in 30 runs rated below HARD runs the HARD panel instead, logged `audit_upgrade=true`. This is the only unbiased ongoing estimate of what the cheap panels miss; do not remove it when early confusion matrices look clean.

**Operator override:** `--difficulty=<tier>` on /implement, /review, and /design beats rating and floors and is logged as `override_source=operator`.

**Revisions to #5886 (DONE), explicit:**

1. The blanket archetype Codex bump to gpt-5.5 becomes HARD-only; TRIVIAL and MODERATE archetype halves run gpt-5.4-mini (per-tier `model_role`).
2. The uniform round cap 2 becomes per-tier ceilings 2/2/3. This reintroduces a round-3 branch for HARD only: the prune window generalizes (round 3 prunes on rounds 1-2 data) and must preserve the #5733 `-output` label-join fix.
3. TRIVIAL introduces a singles panel with the flip-to-other-vendor availability rule instead of drop-the-half.

**Backtest evidence for the shape (100 /implement panel runs plus 100 /design runs; full numbers in the umbrella issue):** 92% of runs already complete in one round via the substantiality gate, so ceilings only bite heavy runs; round 3 accepted 21 findings across the 3 heavy runs that reached it; Cursor-only catches are 22% of accepted findings overall but concentrated in large diffs (2 findings across 33 small runs).

**Acceptance:**

- Panels resolve per the tier tables across all three skills; ceilings 2/2/3 enforced; HARD round 3 reachable only via the substantiality gate.
- Escalated rounds run the next tier's full panel; escalation precedes pruning.
- 1:30 audit upgrades occur and are flagged; `--difficulty` override wins and is logged.
- TRIVIAL with Codex down flips to Cursor singles; both-down follows #5889.
- No regression of the #5733 join fix; a regression test asserts the round-1 prune ledger populates non-zero before round-2 pruning relies on it.
