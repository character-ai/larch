**1. correctness — `plan.txt:73-74` / `python/plan_review_tally.py:662-675,280-298`**

The plan tells plan-review tally to reuse `_votes_and_severities_for_item` for agreement severity. That helper drops empty slots, but `voter_votes` always emits three tuples. On degraded panels, severities would shift against voter labels. Severity scoreboards would be wrong even though points at `plan_review_tally.py:679-683` stay correct.

**Fix:** collect severity in the same per-slot loop that builds `voter_votes`.

**2. correctness — `plan.txt:147` / `python/review_tally.py:638` / `python/plan_review_tally.py:576-581`**

Main tally paths append severity after agreement. Zero-judge paths do not. That breaks acceptance criterion 147 and leaves degraded `voting-tally.md` files without the paired header.

**Fix:** append the empty severity scoreboard beside existing zero-path agreement renders.

**3. correctness — `plan.txt:28-29` / `python/voting.py:244-248`**

Reading `vN_severity` from TSV is not enough. Those values must flow through `voter_agreement_row_from_panel` so row dicts match live tally rows. Otherwise calibration analysis diverges from live tallies despite plan.txt:125-126.

**Fix:** pass aligned `voter_severities` at the TSV call site; document zip rules in `voter_agreement_row_from_panel`.
