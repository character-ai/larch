## Approach

Extend the existing Cursor Auto split into three lanes:

- Auto: `config.CURSOR_AUTO_MODEL`.
- Grok: `grok-4.5`.
- Composer: every other model, including unknown and model-less rows.

Add the `("cursor", "grok-4.5")` rate row at input `2.00`, cache read `0.50`, and output `6.00` per million tokens. Do not apply the Teams surcharge to Grok.

Evolve the cost wire additively. Keep `CURSOR_COST` as the sum of all Cursor lanes. Add separate composer, Grok, and Auto cost fields. Preserve aggregate output for artifacts that lack `BUCKETS_cursor_by_model`.

Use one Cursor bucket-classification helper for `/report-tokens` and final-report pricing. This prevents the two consumers from assigning the same model to different lanes.

## Edge cases

- Treat `grok-4.5` as an exact model identifier. Do not classify other `grok-*` names at the Grok 4.5 rate.
- Route empty, unknown, and future unrecognized model names to composer, as resolved in discussion round 1.
- Treat a missing or malformed per-model mapping as legacy aggregate data.
- Preserve zero-valued lane fields when a valid per-model map exists.
- Do not infer a lane split from aggregate `CURSOR_COST`.
- Keep tiny rounded lane costs from hiding known model detail. Use source-shape availability, not monetary truthiness, when selecting split rendering.
- Keep the Teams surcharge override limited to composer pricing. It must not affect Grok or Auto.
- Do not add `tokens.py` enrichment. New reports already contain `BUCKETS_cursor_by_model`.

## Failure modes

- A duplicated classifier could price final reports differently from `/report-tokens`. Reuse one helper.
- Treating legacy aggregate tokens as composer detail could mislabel historical costs. Preserve an explicit unavailable state for lane data.
- Omitting Grok or Auto flags from PR summary forwarding could produce a correct token report but an incorrect final summary.
- Replacing `CURSOR_COST` would break existing wire consumers. Add fields without renaming or removing the aggregate key.
- Applying the Teams surcharge to Grok would overstate the new lane.
- Positional `RunRecord` construction can break if new defaulted fields are inserted before existing fields. Append them.
