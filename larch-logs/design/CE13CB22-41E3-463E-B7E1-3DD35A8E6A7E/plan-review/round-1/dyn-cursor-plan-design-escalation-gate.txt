### Source paths after the plan

**New normal-handoff reports:** The plan closes the active recording paths. Today `_STEP3_EVIDENCE_STATUSES` includes normal handoffs (165-173), `normalize_step3_status_main` records on those statuses (570-573), `step3_loop_emit_envelope` calls `step3_record_report_evidence` unconditionally (145), and `step3_record_report_evidence` writes ledger rows for them (681-713). Shrinking the evidence set, early-returning in `step3_record_report_evidence`, and leaving routing sets untouched matches the plan’s minimum-change intent.

**Legacy normal-handoff reports:** Ledger and fallback filtering addresses the issue’s ledger row (`trigger=main-agent-apply-required`). The unconditional marker check (504-509) and execution-issues tool-failure regex (511-512) can still admit legacy artifacts from prior normal-handoff record attempts; the marker gap is the material one.

### Genuine failures still pass the gate

With any-row trigger scanning, `tally-error` / `panel-failed` / `panel-init-failed` / `degraded-empty-collector` still record via `step3_record_report_evidence` and `normalize_step3_status_main`’s failure warning path (571-572). Existing `test_record_report_evidence_writes_escalation_ledger` and `test_step3_normalizer_escalation_evidence_failure_contract` cover that contract. The planned fixture change in `test_failure_report_escalation_success_from_ledger` plus parametrized normal-only skip tests align with the gate behavior.
