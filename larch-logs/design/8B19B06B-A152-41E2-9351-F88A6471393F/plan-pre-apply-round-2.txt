## Edge cases

- A completed checks result belongs to an older `HEAD` or worktree fingerprint.
- Adapter launch preparation would otherwise erase a checks identity seed.
- A live registry row exists before final result publication, with only validated launch-identity rows present.
- Step 5 has a partial, stale, complete, or retryable-stall envelope.
- The daemon is live while the child has exited and publication is pending.
- Child-only-live and daemon-only-live Step 5 state follows the documented shared adapter liveness policy without a Bash-specific fork.
- `step-5-resume --checks-site` must invoke the checks-resume child route and preserve its status rows.
- Step 3 commit-site, rebase-checkpoint, and forked-target checks retain their existing child route and budget.
- A result, merge env, handoff directory, or lineage file is a symlink or non-regular file.
- `step-5-resume --record-only` must not launch a daemon.
- Repeated CI-fixer start after completion must still provide a finalizable `STARTED` envelope.
- CI-fixer finalization is repeated after lineage was already recorded.
- The repository changes between child validation, checks, and result publication.

## Failure modes

- Fail closed with stable machine tokens on invalid arguments, missing tmpdir state, unsafe paths, registry identity mismatch, or unverifiable process identity.
- Do not launch a duplicate job when live-job input identity cannot be proven.
- Publish identity-integrity failure rows when checks inputs drift.
- Never let generic adapter reuse bypass Step 5 canonical envelope validation.
- Preserve operator-bail output for CI-fixer routing and validation failures.
- Never reuse a stale result or partially written merge envelope.
