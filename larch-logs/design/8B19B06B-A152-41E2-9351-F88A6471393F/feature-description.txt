# contract-unification [FEATURE] Convert /implement bgjob adapters to thin wrappers

Part of the #6997 partition. Child of umbrella #7014. Blocked by the foundation piece (the `bgjob adapt` verb). Converts the /implement bgjob adapter scripts that carry no token grammar.

#### Problem

Each /implement bgjob adapter re-implements the start-or-wait boilerplate: `rehydrate_plugin_root`, the `--bgjob-child` / `--merge-result-env` arg loop, a registry-liveness heredoc, stale-clear, merge-env writers, and self-reexec. This is the bulk of the duplicated ~3.2k Bash lines and the home of the `and`/`or` liveness drift.

#### Goal

Shrink each adapter to the ~8-line `step-7a.sh` delegation pattern, delegating the protocol to `bgjob adapt`. One liveness policy inherited from the verb.

#### Scope

Convert to thin wrappers: `skills/implement/scripts/step-5-review.sh`, `step-5-resume.sh`, `step-6-entry.sh`, `run-step-checks.sh`, `step-8-ci-fixer.sh`. Delete the per-script rehydrate copies, liveness heredocs, stale-clear, merge-env writers, and self-reexec. The child work each script runs stays intact as the `<child argv>` the verb launches.

#### Non-goals

The /design adapters and the two step-8 grammar forks (assessment vocab, ship result-env). Those are separate pieces.

#### Acceptance

- /implement steps 5, 6, run-step-checks, and ci-fixer behave identically to before.
- No liveness `and`/`or` fork remains in these scripts.
- Fence-shape and halt-rate harnesses plus `make lint` and `make py-test` green.

Run /design on this issue before /implement.
