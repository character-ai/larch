## Summary

When the ship-time (or any Step 8+) architectural-guidelines pin drops the note, the accompanying diagnostic warning appended to `$IMPLEMENT_TMPDIR/execution-issues.md` can fail to reach the committed run log. Operators then see `Warnings: 0` and no committed `execution-issues.md`, even though the drop-notice code path is proven (by unit tests) to append a warning. Two independent mechanisms:

1. **Flush timing** (OOS_1, important): Step 8+ guideline-drop warnings land only in the temp `execution-issues.md` after Step 7a's primary pre-ship flush. Step 18's teardown safety-net did not capture the late Step-8 append in the observed run, so the committed `larch-logs/implement/<run-id>/execution-issues.md` was absent.
2. **Swallowed append** (OOS_5, nit): warning-append failures are hidden behind `suppress(Exception)`, so a failed append disappears independently of the flush-timing issue.

## Evidence

- Issue #6059 run RUN_ID `8D812696-8581-4C0C-AEF3-8F6634D73683` reported `Warnings: 0` with no committed `execution-issues.md` despite rendering the drop notice.
- This is issue #6059 Suggested-fix #3 and Open-question #2.

## Provenance

Surfaced as out-of-scope code-review findings (OOS_1, OOS_5) during `/implement` of #6059 (merged as PR #6060). Reviewer-proposed; not independently re-verified.

## Suggested fix

- Guarantee Step 8+ execution-issues warnings reach the committed log: flush immediately after logging the specific guideline-drop warning, or verify Step 18 teardown's safety-net flush captures late Step-8 appends.
- Do not swallow warning-append failures silently.

## Proposed Design Outline

### Goals
- Guarantee Step 8+ (ship-pr) execution-issues.md warnings from the architectural-guidelines drop-notice path reach the committed run-log batch, even when no other CI-fix/rebase retry happens before merge.
- Stop fully swallowing append failures in `_log_guidelines_ship_warning` so a failed append is visible to operators.

### Non-goals
- Rearchitecting the general run-log flush/commit pipeline (`flush_logs_pre`, Step 7a flush, Step 18 teardown safety net) beyond closing this one gap.
- Fixing every `suppress(Exception)` pattern in `ship_guidelines.py` / `architectural_guidelines.py`, only the one the issue names.
- Changing the post-merge teardown's existing guard (skip commit when `post_merge_sentinel` exists or branch is main/master); that guard is correct and must stay intact.

### Approach sketch
- Close the flush-timing gap (OOS_1) without adding a stand-alone git push that would retrigger CI (avoid regressing #5217 / #5186): make the warning ride an already-scheduled push instead of adding a new one.
- Likely touches ordering between `_invalidate_guidelines_note` (`ship.py`) and the CI-fix "normal" push path (`ci_monitor.py`'s `stage_and_push`), and/or the pre-merge transition in the merge loop.
- Replace the fully-silent `suppress(Exception)` in `_log_guidelines_ship_warning` (OOS_5) with a fail-loud fallback (per ARCHITECTURAL_GUIDELINES.md G-Py-4), keeping its best-effort contract: it must never raise and abort ship-pr.
