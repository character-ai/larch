**1. [correctness] `python/stall_recovery.py:669-674` (plan **Approach** / **UPDATED: python/stall_recovery.py`)**

The plan fixes the common single-flush path (`PHASE=pr-prep`, no `PR_NUMBER` yet). It does not fix the structural blocker at ```669:674:python/stall_recovery.py```: `pr-created` requires `not _truthy(merge)`, so any `--merge` run with a `PR_NUMBER` still lands on `bailed`. `flush_logs_pre` also runs at `PHASE=rebase` during CI-fix loops (```1675:1676:python/ship.py```), after `PR_NUMBER` exists. Those refreshes can overwrite a good pr-prep snapshot with `bailed` again.

**Suggested revision:** Add a `MERGE=true` + (`PR_NUMBER` or `PR_URL`) + no `MERGE_RESULT` branch, or extend healthy phases beyond `pr-prep`/`pr-create`. Add a stall_recovery test for `MERGE=true`, `PR_NUMBER` set, `PHASE=rebase`.
