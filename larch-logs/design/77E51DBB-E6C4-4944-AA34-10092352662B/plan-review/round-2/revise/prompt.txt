<feature>
/design plan-size gate: split add/delete thresholds, relax mechanical churn

## Motivation

The `/design` Step 2b.5 hard plan-size trigger (`skills/design/scripts/check-plan-size.sh`) fires on a single `diff_lines: <N>` total-churn estimate with a fixed `> 1500` threshold and offers only **Split / Cancel** (no Continue — hard triggers are non-downgradeable). This conflates two very different kinds of large change:

- **Deletion-heavy rip-outs** — a plan that mostly *removes* code is large by line count but low in review complexity.
- **Trivial mechanical modifications** — large rename / mechanical-substitution churn that is high line count but low complexity.

Concrete trigger case: issue #3118 ("Breadcrumbs Deprecation Stage 3: Monitor contract removal") produced a ~4700-line plan that is overwhelmingly *deletions* (deleting `lint-foreground-markers.sh` ~1037 lines + `test-lint-foreground-markers.sh` ~1721 + `test-breadcrumb-monitor.sh` ~902, plus plumbing removal). The hard gate fired and offered only Split/Cancel on a leaf partition piece (already piece 3 of a 5-way split) where neither fit; the operator had to use the `AskUserQuestion` "Other" escape to override and proceed.

## Proposed change

1. Have `check-plan-size.sh` distinguish **added** vs **deleted** line estimates instead of one `diff_lines` total. Either accept separate trailers (e.g. `diff_added: <N>` / `diff_deleted: <N>`) or derive the add/delete split from the plan, and key the hard trigger primarily on **additions / net new complexity**, not raw total churn.
2. Allow **basically arbitrary-size deletions** — deletions should not trip the hard trigger (or only at a very high, separate cap). Large removals are low-risk.
3. **Strongly relax** the limit for **trivial mechanical modifications** even when the count is very large (e.g. mechanical rename / substitution churn), so high-count-but-low-complexity changes are not forced into Split/Cancel.
4. Consider giving the hard trigger a Continue/override affordance for these low-complexity-large cases, or reclassifying them as soft (advisory) rather than hard.

- The `diff_lines` trailer contract: `skills/design/scripts/emit-plan.sh`, `skills/design/scripts/design-driver.sh`, and the Step 2b "Diff size estimate" instruction in `skills/design/SKILL.md`.
