### FINDING_1: Ambiguous WARN/ERROR replay contract for in-process read_result_env_main
- **Reviewer(s)**: Cursor-Arch, Cursor-Pragmatic
- **Severity**: important
- **Concern**: In-process `read_result_env_main` always replays WARN=/ERROR= via `_replay_warn_error` to process stdout, while the plan also mandates a separate Stage-1 selected-source WARN/ERROR replay. Without one explicit load contract, implementers may double-emit machine WARN= lines ahead of the canonical KV envelope when both paths run without stdout suppression, or drop required WARN= lines when stdout is redirected during the in-process call (as Step 5c-style consumers expect). Either outcome can break orchestrator parsing order and harness contracts such as `test-design-step3-review.sh` stdout-only WARN cases and `INVALID_SLOT_PANEL_WARNING` replay.
- **Suggested revisions (informational for voters; coder decides)**:
  - From Cursor-Arch: Spell out one load contract: either (a) call read_result_env_main with stdout captured, then emit captured WARN=/ERROR= once in Stage 1 before mapping/envelope, or (b) load allowlisted pairs via phase_driver_read_result_env without replay and call _replay_warn_error on the selected source once. Do not reuse _step5c_safe_publish_env verbatim.
  - From Cursor-Pragmatic: Redirect or capture stdout while calling read_result_env_main for quoted temp-file generation; let Stage 1 be the sole WARN/ERROR replay path to wrapper stdout (match the single subprocess emission today).
