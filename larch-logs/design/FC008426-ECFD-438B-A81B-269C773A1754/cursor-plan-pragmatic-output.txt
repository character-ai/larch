**1. [correctness]** `plan.txt:44-45` — `rebase_then_evaluate` handling diverges from bash.

The plan treats `rebase_then_evaluate` like `rebase`: `goto_rebase` only, fix later. Bash runs `run_evaluate_failure` immediately after rebase in the same handler:

```3547:3549:scripts/ship-pr.sh
        rebase_then_evaluate)
            run_rebase_rebump "$phase"
            run_evaluate_failure "$phase"
```

Splitting across driver re-entry plus `poll_ci` can stall on `pending`/`wait` instead of entering the fix loop.
