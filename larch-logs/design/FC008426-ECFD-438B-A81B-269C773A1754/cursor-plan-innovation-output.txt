1. **correctness** (`scripts/ci-wait.sh:263-266`): The plan ports `poll_ci` from `ci-wait.sh` but never states that `iteration` must stay constant across internal polls. Bash explicitly does not increment `ITERATION` during the wait loop; only outer driver cycles bump it. Without that contract, an implementer may increment per poll and trip `ci-decide`’s 50-iteration cap after minutes instead of dozens of rebase/fix cycles.

2. **correctness** (`scripts/ship-pr.sh:2526-2530`): `run_evaluate_failure` bails on detached HEAD before each outer attempt. The proposed `evaluate_failure` has no equivalent guard, so fixes could run on a detached checkout.

3. **correctness** (`python/ci_monitor.py` monitor API): Bash mutates `TRANSIENT_RETRIES` and `FIX_ATTEMPTS` inside `run_evaluate_failure`. `MonitorResult` exposes `did_fixing` and `iterations` but not updated `transient_retries` / `fix_attempts`, and the plan does not say how the Phase-7 driver should persist them before the next `poll_ci`.

4. **architecture** (`scripts/ship-pr.sh:1727-1730`, plan monitor mapping): Both `fix-attempts-exhausted` and `first-fixer-non-health` map to `NEEDS_USER_INPUT`, but bash treats `first-fixer-non-health` as exit-3 **without** `BAIL_NEEDS_USER_INPUT` (autonomous Step 8+ path). `StepResult` alone does not encode that distinction unless `detail` is normative for the driver.

5. **[OUT_OF_SCOPE]** (`scripts/ship-pr.sh:2540-2565`): The `CI_FIX_REBASE_PENDING` branch (stage/push retry after rebase without re-running the vendor waterfall) is omitted from Phase 6 by design; Phase 7 must own it or accept a behavioral gap on failed post-rebase push.
