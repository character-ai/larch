# Plan — Remove HARD/SIMPLE workflow classification from /implement and /report-tokens (#3432)

/implement hardcodes HARD at every call site today; the classification is vestigial. This plan deletes the notion from /implement and from report-tokens' implement reports. Every /design surface stays untouched: `design_classification`, `write-run-params.sh --workflow-path`, design run-params `workflow_path`, the design summary Path bullet, `read-workflow-path.sh`, and the report-tokens `--skill=design` SIMPLE/HARD split.

Scope anchors (Round 1): implement-side only; implement summaries drop the Path bullet; report-tokens removes the workflow dimension fully for implement; launcher timeout fixed at 7200s (current effective behavior).

## Approach

Delete the classification at its three layers:

1. **Producer plumbing** (`/implement` shell): drop the `--workflow` flag, the `WORKFLOW_PATH` run-flags key, and the timing-ledger workflow row. Fix the launcher timeout at 7200s.
