### FINDING_2: `DESIGN_TMPDIR` guard is stricter than bash prepare prelude
- **Reviewer(s)**: Cursor-Innovation, Cursor-dyn-step5b-parity
- **Severity**: important
- **Concern**: The plan uses `_require_design_tmpdir`, which requires an absolute, existing directory. Bash only rejects an empty `DESIGN_TMPDIR`; prepare then runs `mkdir -p "$DESIGN_TMPDIR/.completed"`, which can create a missing tmpdir root. Relative or not-yet-created tmpdir values that reach Step 5b today would fail in the port before step-4b / prepare work runs.
- **Suggested revisions (informational for voters; coder decides)**:
  - From Cursor-Innovation: Match bash: reject only empty DESIGN_TMPDIR, or document and test the stricter guard as an intentional contract change
  - From Cursor-dyn-step5b-parity: Match bash prepare prelude: after non-empty DESIGN_TMPDIR check use mkdir(parents=True exist_ok=True) on design_tmpdir/.completed before touching step-4b; do not require is_dir() on the root unless bash did (annotate may keep stricter read-only checks)


### FINDING_6: In-process capture helper may not preserve subprocess failure boundary
- **Reviewer(s)**: Codex-dyn-step5b-parity
- **Severity**: important
- **Concern**: The planned in-process capture helper is not required to translate callable crashes into an rc plus captured stderr like today's subprocess boundary. An `OSError` or other uncaught exception inside `design_oos` during prepare could raise out of `step5b_prepare_main` before the non-zero branch, with no stderr log, `STEP5B_STATUS`, or completion sentinel; annotate crashes could likewise lose `OOS_ANN_RC` and `STEP5B_STATUS=annotate-failed`.
- **Suggested revisions (informational for voters; coder decides)**:
  - From Codex-dyn-step5b-parity: Specify that the new capture helper catches SystemExit as its rc and catches ordinary exceptions by writing a traceback to the target stderr log and returning rc 1, so the existing non-zero prepare and annotate branches run unchanged.
