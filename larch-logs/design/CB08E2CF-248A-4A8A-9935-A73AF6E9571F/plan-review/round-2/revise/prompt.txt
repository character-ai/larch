CI fixing via bgjob lanes with evidence-based timeouts; make ship-step autonomous

## Goal

The ship step (Step 8+) must be aggressively autonomous: CI and lint failures are repaired by delegated background lanes, and the main agent bails to the operator only in exceptional circumstances (fork/repo-unavailable, auth/infra hard failure, exhausted generous budgets, red base branch beyond a recorded repair). ## Required change 1 — bgjob execution, not in-session sub-agent

Replace the Step 8 Agent-tool CI fixer with a bgjob lane (same start/wait contract as step-5-review and step-8-ship): the fixer runs as a detached claude_sub/codex/cursor subprocess under the fixer waterfall (`implement.ci_recovery_fixer`), the orchestrator only starts the bgjob and repeats the documented `bgjob wait`. ## Required change 2 — evidence-based timeouts

Size every CI/lint fixer lane timeout from measured duration distributions so the timeout does not fire in most cases:

- Raise the checks repair-loop external-lane budget from 300s to at least 1800s (p95+ of measured claude-lint-fix and codex-ci durations; align with SUBPROCESS_DEFAULT_TIMEOUT_SEC / CI_WAIT_TIMEOUT_SEC).

- Timeout firing should be rare enough to be an execution-issue anomaly, not a routine escalation path.
