### Item 1 — Mermaid fence parse validity not tested in harness

- **Location**: scripts/test-render-review-phase-detail.sh:84-97,121-140
- **Severity**: low
- **Description**: Test harness asserts Gantt/mermaid substrings (gantt/dateFormat/axisFormat) via grep but never runs `mmdc --parseOnly` or `python/cli.py lint mermaid-fences` on generated output. A typo in label sanitization or task-line emission would produce invalid Mermaid syntax that passes CI. Affects test cases 4, 5, and 9 (lines 84-97 and 121-140). Suggested fix: add a `mmdc --parseOnly` or `python/cli.py lint mermaid-fences` assertion on generated Gantt output in the sandbox.

### Item 2 — render-final-summary.md absent from edit-in-sync list

- **Location**: skills/design/scripts/render-final-summary.md:58-60

### Item 3 — Vendor row filtering misses skill and task_kind columns

- **Location**: scripts/render-review-phase-detail.sh:224-236
- **Description**: The plan matches vendor rows to rounds by time overlap only and does not filter `$2=="vendor"` rows by `$4` skill or review-related `$7` task_kind. Non-reviewer vendor tasks (e.g., lint-fix, ship-pr) can bleed into the review-phase vendor cost display.

### Item 4 — write-final-report.md doc-sync slated but render-final-summary.md excluded

### Item 5 — Live progress appends "No review rounds" for in-flight reviews

- **Location**: python/progress_report.py:382-405
- **Description**: Live progress reports call the shared renderer during in-flight review when round dirs exist but `round-meta.json` does not yet exist. Step 5 progress output can append "No review rounds" erroneously while a review is in progress.
