I’d implement this in two phases: first make the re-entry observable, then narrow the terminal prompt contract. Current `larch-logs/design/*/manifest.json` can show repeated runs for the same issue, but it does not prove whether a duplicate came from anti-halt continuation, wakeup, loop, subagent return, or a parent harness. Add lightweight invocation telemetry at `/design` Step 0 and Step 6: run id, issue, argv hash/raw-safe summary, parent skill, Claude PID/PPID, start/cleanup timestamps, and terminal outcome. Then add an audit helper that groups `larch-logs/design/<RUN_ID>/manifest.json` by issue and close start times to disambiguate “same operator request immediately re-fired” from legitimate later reruns.

Primary edits would be in `skills/design/SKILL.md`, plus a small helper under `skills/design/scripts/` or `scripts/` for the audit. The likely prompt-side fix belongs in `skills/design/SKILL.md`: the anti-halt reminder currently says it applies “from Step 0 through Step 6” and asks for the next numbered step after every numbered step. Since Step 6 has no next step, make Step 6 an explicit terminal boundary: after cleanup, do not call any skill, do not schedule wakeups, do not emit “continue” prose, and end the turn. Keep the Step 0b `[DESIGNED]` / `larch:plan` safety bail unchanged. Update `scripts/test-design-structure.sh` to pin the Step 6 terminal-stop wording and to ensure `ScheduleWakeup` / loop sentinels remain absent from the `/design` path except documented prohibitions.

The main tradeoff is prompt-contract fix versus runtime lockout. A hard cross-session “already active/completed” lock could mask the symptom, but it would not identify whether the real trigger is anti-halt text, a background notification, or parent orchestration. I’d avoid that until instrumentation proves it is needed. The risks are mostly behavioral: making the anti-halt text too weak could regress earlier bugs where `/design` stopped after Step 5b or other intermediate artifacts, while making Step 6 too verbose could itself create another terminal-output ambiguity. Keep the change surgical: strong continuation through Step 5, explicit stop after Step 6, and tests that pin both sides.
