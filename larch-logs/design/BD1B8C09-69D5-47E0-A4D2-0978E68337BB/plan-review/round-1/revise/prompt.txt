# md-to-py-V: fold commit-route (COMMIT_OUTCOME to NEXT_ACTION) into the commit verb

Follow-up to the md-to-py-IV umbrella (#5161). The one genuinely new logic-move in round V; two independent audit lanes converged on it.

**Context.** Round IV made `review-and-fix commit-fixes` emit `COMMIT_OUTCOME=ok|noop|failed` (`_emit_commit_fixes_kvs` in `python/review_and_fix.py`). But the orchestrator still hand-parses that token and hand-seeds durable-bail state at three `/implement` sites: Step 5 self-review, Step 5 resume-handoff (MAV/coder), and Step 7. **Proposed.** Add a `commit-route` verb (or extend the commit-gate) that runs the commit, gates on `COMMIT_OUTCOME`, seeds durable-bail itself on failure, and emits one `NEXT_ACTION=continue|stall` keyed by a `--site` argument.
