# md-to-py-V: fold commit-route (COMMIT_OUTCOME to NEXT_ACTION) into the commit verb

Follow-up to the md-to-py-IV umbrella (#5161). The one genuinely new logic-move in round V; two independent audit lanes converged on it.

**Context.** Round IV made `review-and-fix commit-fixes` emit `COMMIT_OUTCOME=ok|noop|failed` (`_emit_commit_fixes_kvs` in `python/review_and_fix.py`). But the orchestrator still hand-parses that token and hand-seeds durable-bail state at three `/implement` sites: Step 5 self-review, Step 5 resume-handoff (MAV/coder), and Step 7. Each repeats the same tree: parse line-anchored `^COMMIT_OUTCOME=`, continue only on `ok`/`noop`, else log to `execution-issues.md`, set `STALL_TRACKING=true` / `STALL_STEP` / `STALL_REASON`, seed-or-key-rewrite `ship-pr-state.sh` with the durable-bail pattern, and skip to Step 18. Sites differ only in the `STALL_STEP` literal (5/5/7), the `STALL_REASON` literal, and the "do not proceed to X" continuation list.

**Already partly built.** `step5_resume_main` (`python/implement_dispatch.py`) already encapsulates the `ok`/`noop` allowlist gate for the MAV/coder path. The seeder (`ship seed-initial-state` via `step-8-seed-initial.sh`) already accepts `--stall-tracking` / `--stall-step` / `--bail-reason`.

**Proposed.** Add a `commit-route` verb (or extend the commit-gate) that runs the commit, gates on `COMMIT_OUTCOME`, seeds durable-bail itself on failure, and emits one `NEXT_ACTION=continue|stall` keyed by a `--site` argument. The three SKILL prose blocks collapse to "branch on `NEXT_ACTION`: continue, or skip to Step 18."

**KEEP-safe.** Pure mechanical parse to state-seed to route; no voting, dedup, plan drafting, or conflict resolution.

**Value:** High (hot path; three fragile hand-parsed copies; gate logic largely exists). **Effort:** M. Land this before the stall/checks dedup issue, which it partly absorbs.
