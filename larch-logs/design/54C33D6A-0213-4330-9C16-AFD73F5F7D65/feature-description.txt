# contract-unification [FEATURE] gh issue-list wrapper + raw-argv funnel

#### Problem

`git/gh.py` is internally clean (single `_gh` choke point, `_retry_read` transient retry) but has no issue-list wrapper — verified, and `audit_runs.py:141` says so in a noqa comment ("no public issue-list wrapper yet"). Listing is therefore reimplemented 8+ times with divergent pagination limits, field sets, and failure grammars: `analyze_bugs.py:323-363`, `learn_from_bugs.py:370-398`, `analyze_issues.py:229-267` (raw subprocess with field-fallback retry), `audit_runs.py:591,711,1274`, `combine_issues.py:1174`, `deps_audit.py:256-259`, `issue_create.py:811-872` (whose `_json_documents` at :791 re-parses the concatenated-pages format `gh._loads_json_paginated_list` already owns at gh.py:213), `rejected_analysis.py:939`, plus `skills/voter-calibration/scripts/voter-calibration.py:175`. Beyond listing, 24 modules build raw `["gh", ...]` argv (issue view 8 sites, `repo view --json nameWithOwner` 9 sites despite `gh.py:1788 resolve_repo`, issue edit/close 11) and most skip `_retry_read`. Repo resolution is defined 11 times.

#### Goal

Add `gh.issue_list_read(runner, *, repo, state, labels, search, fields, paginate)` and the few missing view/edit/close variants; repoint callers; delete `_json_documents`; repoint repo-resolution copies to `gh.resolve_repo`. Adoption-ratchet lint banning `"gh"` argv literals outside `larch/git/` (test fixtures suppressed).

#### Size

~240 lines of fetch/parse/error plumbing to ~70; one retry/pagination/error authority.

#### Dependencies

Conflicts with in-flight analyze-bugs and learn-from-bugs work editing the same modules; land after those.
