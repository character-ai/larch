== TRADEOFFS ==

- Coupling vs drift prevention: ONE renderer prevents future hand-written drift, at the cost that any prompt-wording change now affects both /design and /review. Mitigation: byte-preserve existing wording where possible (intentional content changes are limited to: adding the general rule to /design, adding the OOS problem-vs-solution clause to both).
- Behavior risk: voter-panel behavior could shift if the new wording reads differently from the old. Mitigation: /review's existing prose is preserved exactly; /design's new general rule is a guardrail (voters should already be voting this way), not a behavior change; the OOS clause is emphatic reinforcement.
- Test fixture compatibility: existing harnesses use exact-match snapshots in places. The unification will require those snapshots to be regenerated, or the assertions changed to substring grep checks. Substring grep is preferred (more maintainable, less fragile to whitespace/ordering tweaks).
