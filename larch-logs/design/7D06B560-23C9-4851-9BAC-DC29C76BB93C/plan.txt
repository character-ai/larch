## Failure modes

- Over-trimming the preamble can move a required safety rule into lazy-read-only context, or drop the visible-output continuation trigger so the model halts after a plan print, voting tally, or skip breadcrumb.
  - Avoid this by keeping the operative no-recap, no-cost, render-exit, and visible-output continuation rules inline in `skills/design/SKILL.md`, each pinned in `test-design-structure.sh`.
- Recap-ban wording can weaken from the precise `_publish_rc` 0/1/3 plus non-empty-cancellation-file trigger to vaguer "after Step 5c" timing, so agents recap too early or miss the `_publish_rc=1` path.
  - Avoid this by keeping the operative trigger verbatim in the preamble stub and pinning it in `test-design-structure.sh`.
- Final-summary sites can lose source identity.
  - Avoid this by naming the source at each site and pinning source names in both harnesses.
- Dedup can drop the render-exit carve-out from Step 5c item 5 while leaving only `Regardless of \`PLAN_WRITE_OK\``.
  - Avoid this by preserving the carve-out in the preamble stub and Step 5c item 5, and pinning it in both harnesses.
- Harness checks can become too brittle against wording changes.
  - Prefer literal checks for contract tokens and source names, not full paragraph text.
- The shared anti-halt target can imply `/design` is part of the anti-halt banner harness.
  - Avoid this by adding a note, not changing the scope list.
- Updating `skills/design/SKILL.md` without retargeting `test-render-cost-line-callsites.sh` leaves `make lint` red despite passing `make test-design-structure`.
  - Avoid this by landing both harness updates in the same PR.
