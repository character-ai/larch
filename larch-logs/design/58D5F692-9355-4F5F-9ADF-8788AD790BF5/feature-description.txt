# review-points-overhaul Value-weight reviewer points by severity/impact instead of flat plus or minus one

Replace the flat `+1` per accepted finding with a value-weighted reward.

**Problem.** Every accepted finding earns `+1`, whether it is a critical security regression or a necessary one-line test. A reviewer optimizing score is indifferent between hunting hard, high-value bugs and racking up easy completeness findings. Effort flows to the easy points. The planned token allocation would then fund finding-count, not value.

**Proposed change.** Tier the reward by severity/impact. Keep it simple: e.g. accepted `Blocking`/`security`/`correctness` finding `+2`, accepted ordinary finding `+1`. Two tiers, not a baroque table. Optionally scale the penalty symmetrically.

**Evidence.** Severity is highly predictive of acceptance: `important` findings accept at 75–93%, `nit` at 18–36%. Yet **28.8% of accepted `/implement` findings were themselves reviewer-severity nit/latent**, and nit+latent are ~52% of raw volume. The reward should track the value spread the data already shows.

**Affected surfaces.** Scoring in `python/voting.py`, scoreboard rendering (`skills/shared/voting-protocol.md`, `python/progress_report.py`, `python/review_pipeline.py`), `docs/point-competition.md`.

**Depends on / conflicts with.** The ballot/classification reshape in #4764 (shared scoreboard + `findings-classification.tsv` surface).
