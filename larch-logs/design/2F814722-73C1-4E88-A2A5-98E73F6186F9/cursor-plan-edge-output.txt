Claude `SessionStart` ([`hooks/hooks.json`](hooks/hooks.json)) runs [`scripts/sessionstart-health.sh`](scripts/sessionstart-health.sh) only. `/design` uses [`scripts/write-design-current-env.sh`](scripts/write-design-current-env.sh) and never calls `write-session-env.sh`. `/review` and `/research` invoke `session-setup.sh` without `--write-session-env` ([`scripts/session-setup.sh`](scripts/session-setup.sh):471). Daily use without `/implement` can leave mtimes stale and reproduce the original eviction surprise.

[`collect_active_session_versions`](skills/upgrade-larch/scripts/upgrade-larch.sh) only reads `LARCH_CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT=` from `session-env.sh` files; `/design` writes `export CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT` in `source-env.sh` ([`scripts/write-design-current-env.sh`](scripts/write-design-current-env.sh):127). Design-heavy users get neither mtime refresh (Finding 1) nor active-session protection for their running plugin version.
