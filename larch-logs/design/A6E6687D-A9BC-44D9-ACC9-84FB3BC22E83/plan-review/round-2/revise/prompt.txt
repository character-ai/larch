## Agreement definition

- Count only rows with:
  - `voting_result` of `accepted` or `rejected`.

- Exclude rows with:
  - `voting_result=neutral`.

- A voter agrees when:
  - `accepted` and voter vote is `YES`.

- Treat empty, missing, or `JUDGE_ERROR` voter cells as **missing**, not disagreement.
- Track `missing` separately so parse-rate failures do not look like outlier voting.
- Compute `agreement_rate` as `agree / (agree + disagree)` when the denominator is positive; missing votes are **not** in the rate denominator.
- **Chronic outlier rule** (single deterministic default, shared by shared math, live tally scoreboards, analyzer, docs, and tests):

- **Neutral verdicts**: exclude from agreement denominators.

- **Rejected unanimous NO**: all NO voters agree.
- **Single-voter fallback**: undefined, excluded.

- **Dead slots**: count missing cells separately; missing excluded from `agreement_rate`.
- **Legacy 21-column design TSVs**: parse with `body_severity` optional.

# review-points-overhaul Score and calibrate voters against realized outcomes

Measure and reward the voting panel, not just the reviewers.

**Problem.** Reviewers are scored; **voters are not**. The panel is the measuring instrument for every reviewer point, but nothing audits or incentivizes panel accuracy. Any score, and the future token allocation built on it, inherits panel error with no feedback loop. A rubber-stamp voter and a careful voter face identical (zero) consequences.

**Proposed change.** Reconcile panel verdicts against realized outcomes and reward voters by calibration. Signals already exist post-hoc: `/audit-runs`, `/analyze-issues`, issue fate, and later reverts of accepted findings. Lightweight start: compute inter-voter agreement and flag chronic outlier voters; richer version: a per-voter calibration score (verdict vs. realized value).

**Affected surfaces.** New voter-calibration report (extend `skills/fluff-analysis` or add a sibling), tally in `python/voting.py`, per-voter `vN` cells in `findings-classification.tsv`.

**Depends on.** #4764 (strip ballot attribution) — calibrate voters *after* the de-biasing change that alters voter behavior, and on the same reshaped classification/tally surface.
