# md-to-py-V: slim plan-review.md (drop rendered-string dupes + dead loop prose)

Follow-up to the md-to-py-IV umbrella (#5161). Highest-leverage context-bloat win in round V.

**Context.** `skills/design/references/plan-review.md` is ~42 KB and is pulled into context `MANDATORY — READ ENTIRE FILE` on every Step 3 review round (the hottest `/design` path). That same SKILL directive already states that panel dispatch, collection, aggregation, voting, and tally run inside `python/plan_review.py`. So a large fraction of the reference is dead weight.

**Two problems.**
- **Verbatim copies of Python-rendered strings.** The Voter-prompt blockquotes (the `## Voter prompts` section, including the `Severity floor (mandatory)` rubric) and the `Competition notice` blockquote are byte-duplicates of what `python/cli.py render voter` and `python/cli.py render plan-review` already emit (`python/rendering.py`). The `Competition notice` is triplicated: `rendering.py`, `plan-review.md`, and `skills/design/SKILL.md`.
- **Narration of loop-internal logic the orchestrator no longer runs.** The `## Collecting External Reviewer Results`, `## Voting Panel launch-order and tally`, and most of `## Finalize Plan Review` sections give hand-instructions for collect/dedup/vote/tally that `python/plan_review.py` owns in loop mode, plus a "mechanical argv reference" snippet for a path the prose itself calls the loop driver.

**Proposed.** Replace the duplicated prompt blockquotes with one-line pointers to the `render` verbs. Demote the collect/vote/tally sections to a short "loop-internal, no orchestrator action" note. Keep the few genuinely orchestrator-consumed bits: the semantic-dedup judgment rule, the `FINDING_N` / `OOS_N` artifact templates, and the "interpret voting-tally.md after the driver returns" guidance.

**KEEP-safe.** Does not touch the semantic-dedup judgment (KEEP) or the byte-preserved templates. The verbatim-re-emit KEEP item is about re-emitting Python output as chat; this only removes a static duplicate the orchestrator never emits in loop mode.

**Value:** High (largest always-loaded reference on the hot path; zero behavior change). **Effort:** S to M.
