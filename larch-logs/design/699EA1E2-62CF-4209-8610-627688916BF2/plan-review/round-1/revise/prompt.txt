# md-to-py-V: condition the unconditional MANDATORY-READ reference loads

Follow-up to the md-to-py-IV umbrella (#5161). Cross-skill bloat pattern flagged by three audit lanes.

**Context.** `MANDATORY — READ ENTIRE FILE` directives force whole-file reference loads roughly 15 times in `skills/design/SKILL.md` and 19 times across `skills/implement/SKILL.md` plus its references. Several fire unconditionally even when the triggering condition is rare or already known to be a no-op. Examples: `execution-issues-tracking.md` is force-read on entry to the dual-write gate even on runs with zero pre-existing-code findings; `summary-comment-template.md` is force-read before any Q/A even when none occurs; `phantom-probe.md` and the rebase-routing references are read whole even when the relevant KV already indicates the no-op path (`PHANTOM_STATUS=clean`, `CHECKPOINT_NEXT=continue`); `settle-rc-dispatch.md` opens by force-reading `readability-style.md` although branching on a wrapper rc emits no prose.

**Proposed.** Sweep each `MANDATORY — READ ENTIRE FILE` and gate the load on the condition that actually needs it (read the tracking reference only when recording a pre-existing-code item; read the summary template only when a Q/A pair will publish). Where a reference is small and always needed, downgrade the all-caps directive to a plain "read". Some sites already hedge with `(if not already loaded at Step 1e)`; make that consistent.

**KEEP-safe.** Conditioning a read on its trigger removes no judgment and changes no branch; it only defers the load. Does not touch the adversarial dispatcher-envelope cross-check or any verbatim summary re-emit.

**Value:** Med (per-turn context across the hottest steps; the single largest reload source). **Effort:** M (per-directive condition analysis; low risk per edit; do as a focused sweep). Sequence after the plan-review.md slim, which removes one of the load targets.
