## Summary

The plan matches the issue on **fail-open** behavior: keep `_slot_row` fallback, preserve manifest shape, and do not fail `dispatch_panel` on render errors alone (`plan.txt:50-59`, `130`).

**Gaps** are in **warning propagation**, not fallback semantics:

1. **`_dynamic_slot_rows` → `dispatch_panel` contract** is implied but not specified.
2. **Early `PANEL_PRUNED_EMPTY` return** can skip panel stdout warnings unless emission happens before that branch.
3. **Reusing `INVALID_SLOT_PANEL_WARNING`** without merge can drop one warning class before round env write.
4. **Tests** cover panel stdout but not `.step3-review-result.env`, which is where orchestration reads carried warnings.

**Aligned areas:** reusing `INVALID_SLOT_PANEL_WARNING` leverages existing `MERGE_KEYS` / `_STEP3_ROUND_CARRY_KEYS` plumbing (`python/plan_review.py:39-51`); stale-summary clearing before render closes the success-path gap (`plan.txt:26-33` vs `python/design_lifecycle.py:1260-1261`); `stdout=subprocess.DEVNULL` on `named-block write` matches the fd1 leak triage (`plan.txt:17-20` vs `python/design_publish.py:287-302`).
