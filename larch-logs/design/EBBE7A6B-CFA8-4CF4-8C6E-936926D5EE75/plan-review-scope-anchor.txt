**Problem.** The state-management subsystem (run-log / session / token) has three correctness/safety smells that can mask or cause data loss. The hardened atomic write exists in one place but is not used everywhere.

**Evidence.**
- Hardened writer `python/session_env.py:369` uses `O_EXCL|O_NOFOLLOW` + `fchmod 0o600` + re-check-symlink; the `python/run_logs.py:164` and `python/tokens.py:1411` copies lack that hardening, so writes through them bypass the protections.
- Non-atomic publish window: `python/run_logs.py:1826` does `rmtree(dest)` then `replace`. A crash in between loses the committed tree.
- Broad `with suppress(Exception)` around commit/manifest IO at `python/run_logs.py:1893`, `:1903`, `:2241` swallows exactly the corruption this subsystem must surface. (Tree-wide: 273 `suppress` calls, mostly narrow `OSError`/`FileNotFoundError`; the broad `suppress(Exception)` subset around state/git IO is the dangerous one.)

**Proposed change.** After the shared `atomic_write` lands, repoint the weak copies to it. Reorder publish to write-temp then atomic-rename (no destructive delete before rename). Narrow the broad `suppress(Exception)` around state/git/manifest IO to specific exceptions and log on swallow so corruption is visible.

**Out of scope / don't-touch.** Keep the fail-closed secret-scrub path and symlink-escape guard semantics exactly (`python/run_logs.py:1692`, `:1550`). Do not narrow the deliberate `BaseException` capture seam in `python/review_and_fix.py`.

**Acceptance.** One hardened atomic-write used by all state writers; no rmtree-before-rename; broad suppressions around state IO narrowed and logged; parity/units green.

**Effort / risk.** Low / low-medium. Removes latent data-loss paths.

**Dependencies.** Blocked by the shared `larch_io` item (needs the shared hardened `atomic_write`). Wired via `/block-issue`.

### Goals
