- **Gate B pause/resume**: pre-apply idempotency guard must run before `Execute the Gate B body`; Shared post-apply alone cannot prevent double-apply.
- **Gate-B-bypass partial sentinels**: keep helper refusal and repair instruction.
- **Probe failure**: fail closed before step-3b completion, sidecar write, or Step 4 tail launch.
- **Internal step-3b write in helper**: probe ordering is impossible unless `run_step3b_finalize` stops touching the marker.
- **Finalize stdout leakage**: driver/probe chatter on wrapper stdout breaks `STEP4_MODE` parsing.
- **Pause between Step 3b and Step 4**: `resume@4` skips finalize; Step 4 reads `$DESIGN_TMPDIR/.step4-mode.env`.
- **`--skip-approve`**: probe-only should emit `foreground`.
- **Cached digest**: probe-only should emit `foreground` when debate is already satisfied.
