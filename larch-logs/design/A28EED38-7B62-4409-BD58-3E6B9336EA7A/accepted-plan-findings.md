### FINDING_1: Plan misstates how test harness exercises `require_key`; `scripts/test-ship-pr.sh` needs `NO_LOGS_COMMIT` added to its `write_state` helper
- **Concern**: The plan's Testing strategy says the existing harnesses "cold-start with `write_initial_state` so they exercise the newly-required keys naturally." This is incorrect for `scripts/test-ship-pr.sh`: its `write_state()` helper manually composes a state file via a `cat <<EOF` heredoc, bypassing `write_initial_state`. That heredoc already emits 6 of the 7 newly-required keys (`BAIL_REASON`, `BAIL_FAILURE_DETAIL_LOG`, `DESIGN_ONLY_DONE`, `EXPECTED_SESSION_ID`, `EXPECTED_TMPDIR_BASENAME_PREFIX`, `IMPLEMENT_TMPDIR`) but does **not** emit `NO_LOGS_COMMIT`. After the `require_key` tightening lands, every `write_state` → `run_subject` scenario in `test-ship-pr.sh` (and the included `test-ship-pr-fix-loop-2632.inc.sh`) will die with `state-file missing required key: NO_LOGS_COMMIT` before exercising its assertions, breaking the harness for unrelated test paths.
- **Proposed resolution**: Add `scripts/test-ship-pr.sh` to the plan's `Files to modify/create` section as a third `UPDATED:` entry. Extend the `write_state()` helper heredoc to also emit `NO_LOGS_COMMIT=false` (matching the bool-literal default written by `write_initial_state`). Mention in the Testing strategy section that `test-ship-pr-fix-loop-2632.inc.sh` reuses the same `write_state` and is therefore covered by the single edit. Remove the misleading "cold-start" claim from the Testing strategy section.
