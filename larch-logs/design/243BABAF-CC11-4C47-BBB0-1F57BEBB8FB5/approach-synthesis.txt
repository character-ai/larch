### Risks raised by Codex (incorporated into the plan)

- **stdout KV-only discipline**: helper script outputs must be parsed (via `ingest_kv_block` or similar) without leaking non-KV lines onto stdout. `larch_err` (stderr) for diagnostics.
- **Partial-adoption states**: when `larch-log.sh init` succeeds but `post-tracking-issue.sh` fails, the manifest is present but no sentinel and no metadata comment. Emitting `DEFERRED=true` lets the orchestrator continue without double-writing on resume (no sentinel ⇒ Branch 1 doesn't fire next time).
- **Sentinel/log ordering**: `post-tracking-issue.sh` already writes `parent-issue.md` only after the metadata post succeeds — bootstrap must NOT duplicate that write.
