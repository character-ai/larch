Tier 1, reviser: the existing autonomous plan-revision machinery (the plan-review apply lane) applies a scoped plan fix for the named `violation` or `deviation` and nothing else; exact re-entry wiring is resolved during /design of this piece; the revise lane stays autonomous per the standing operator decision (#6496, #6498). Re-judge: after any fix, a FRESH `larch:arch-assessor` spawn re-assesses the revised plan; the persist verbs revalidate fail-closed; the judge never evaluates its own fix. Tier 2, main agent: for invariants (`violation`) the main agent MUST attempt the plan fix, and if the re-judge still reports `violation`, Gate C must not approve and the run ends through the existing cancellation outcome with nothing published, no waiver. For guidelines (`deviation`) the main agent chooses: fix the plan, or decline and record a documented exception in the persisted Gate C assessment note (mandatory machine-checkable block: an `Exception:` line with rationale, author `main-agent`, and date), surfaced in the Gate C approval presentation and, under `--skip-approve`, in the final summary. Publish-gate rules mirror #7193: `violation` is never publishable; `deviation` publishes ONLY when the note carries the exception block; a bare `deviation` after ladder exhaustion fails closed. Bounds: one tier-1 round and at most one tier-2 round per kind.
