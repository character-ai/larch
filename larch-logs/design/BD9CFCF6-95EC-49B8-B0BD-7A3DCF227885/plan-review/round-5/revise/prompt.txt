- **Already-fixed CI failures**: helpers no-op and fall back to the delegated fixer path.
- **Non-incident legacy-prefix path**: never auto-expand `ALLOW=`; delegate to Claude or manual review.
- **Partition variants**: do not infer arbitrary `-k` fixes; only the known `cleanup_target_ok` overlap on `test-implement-cleanup-script`.
- **Dual CI failures in one log**: both mechanical helpers may run; verify must pass for **all** fixable jobs before skipping delegate; otherwise rollback and delegate in the same cycle.
- **Mixed known + unrelated CI failures**: partial mechanical fix must not skip delegate or exhaust the cycle; rollback then `agents.launch_tier`.
- **Lint-fix on clean baseline**: `run_lint_fix` commits inline; post-loop delta commit is a no-op.
- **Lint-fix on dirty baseline**: commit paths changed since the pre-lint snapshot via snapshot diff union, never unrelated pre-existing dirty or staged hunks outside that set.
- **Pre-dirty tracked in-place lint edit**: snapshot diff against `pre_lint_head` captures paths even when `delta_paths` omits them.
- **Lint-fix no-op**: do not create an empty review-fix commit.
- **Resume handoff commit failure**: wrapper exits non-zero before re-entering the review loop; orchestrator stalls at Step 5 with `resume-handoff-commit-failed` instead of advancing to Step 6/8 dirty.
- **Resume handoff commit success with clean tree**: `COMMITTED=false` on an already-clean tree is not a failure; resume loop proceeds.
- **Resume Step 5 stall after successful handoff commit**: non-zero wrapper exit with `STEP5_REVIEW_STATUS=stall` routes through the normal Step 5 stall table, not `resume-handoff-commit-failed`.
- **Commit failure**: stall at Step 5 (`lint-fix-commit-failed` or `resume-handoff-commit-failed`) with durable bail wired through `config.py` and Step 18a, rather than `push.assert_clean_worktree` at Step 8.
