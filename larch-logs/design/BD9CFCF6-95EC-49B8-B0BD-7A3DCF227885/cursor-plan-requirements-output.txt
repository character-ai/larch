## Findings

**1. [correctness] `python/review_and_fix.py` — `_lint_fix_snapshot_paths` ambiguity**

The plan’s edge cases forbid committing unrelated pre-existing dirty hunks, but the helper spec’s second union member (“paths lint-fix may have touched”) is not mechanically defined. An implementer could diff all porcelain against the pre-lint snapshot and stage files that were dirty before lint-fix ran, which violates the stated constraint and risks scope creep in the commit.

**Suggested revision:** Restrict candidates to the union of per-iteration `delta_paths` and `git diff --name-only <pre_lint_head>` (for in-place edits on already-dirty tracked files). Remove or replace the open-ended porcelain-diff clause.

**2. [completeness] `python/review_and_fix.py` — missing `commit_sha` handoff from `_run_lint_fix_loop`**

`checks.FixOutcome` already carries `commit_sha`, and `run_lint_fix` commits inline when the pre-lint baseline is clean (`python/checks.py:2108-2139`). The plan’s post-loop no-op rule and tests depend on detecting that case, but the only explicit `_run_lint_fix_loop` extension is `LINT_FIX_DELTA_PATHS`. `_step5_post_round_gates` cannot distinguish “lint already committed” from “lint applied on dirty tree, still needs commit.”

**Suggested revision:** Propagate `commit_sha` from each `applied` outcome through `_run_lint_fix_loop`; skip `_commit_lint_fix_delta_paths` when the loop already produced a commit and porcelain is clean.

**3. [OUT_OF_SCOPE] [risk-integration] `skills/implement/SKILL.md` — `--self-review` lint-fix gap**

Gap #3 work targets the scripted review loop (`_step5_post_round_gates`, `step-5-resume.sh`, Step 7). Self-review already commits with `--stage-all` after inline fixes, but its prompt-side lint-fix repair path does not get the new snapshot commit logic. That is a parallel dirty-tree risk, not part of the #4712 incident or the approved outline surfaces.
