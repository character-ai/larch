### 1. [risk-integration] `python/plan_review.py:880-946` — Embedded plan-review aggregate consumer missing from cutover

The plan correctly retargets **prune-nit** in embedded `plan-review-loop.sh` / `dispatch-plan-review-panel.sh` via `_rewrite_prune_asset` and adds `test_embedded_plan_review_prune_nit_uses_review_cli`. It does **not** do the same for **aggregate-findings**, even though G2 deletes `python/legacy_review_shell/aggregate-findings.sh`.

`run_plan_review_round` still runs the gzip-embedded loop (`python/plan_review.py:1236-1237`). Prior work already had to retarget the collector in that blob (`test_embedded_plan_review_loop_uses_migrated_collector`); aggregate is the same dependency class. `prune-nit-findings.md` documents plan-review calling aggregate between prune and tally.

**Scenario:** After the shell delete, `/design` Step 3 plan-review rounds invoke a missing aggregate script. Aggregation fails (rc 127), findings stay unmerged, and downstream tally/vote behavior diverges from today.

**Suggested revision:** Add an embedded-loop aggregate rewrite in `_decode_legacy_asset` (argv-array + `LARCH_PLAN_REVIEW_AGGREGATE_SH` override, same pattern as prune and waterfall). Preserve plan-mode flags (`--input-mode plan`, `--allow-findings-outside-tmpdir true`). Add `test_embedded_plan_review_aggregate_uses_review_cli` alongside the planned prune embedded test.
