## Approach

- Keep the fix **test-only**.
- Do not change `python/duplicate_code.py` or launcher timeout semantics.
- Do not weaken the `PermissionError` fallback. It is correct production behavior.
- Do not touch deliberate timeout rejection cases: `--timeout 0`, `--timeout 1`, or `--timeout abc`.

## Edge cases

- A sandbox may deny process creation before any future is collected.
  - The fake executor prevents real process creation, so the test reaches the intended patched failure path.
- The fallback path may still be exercised by other tests or production.
  - This plan does not remove or change it.
- The fake executor should not mask logic outside this one test.
  - Keep the monkeypatch local to `test_worker_failure_exits_2`.

## Edge cases

  - Do not change those unless they are success-path vendor-stub launches.

## Failure modes

- If a success-path `_run([... "--timeout", "2" ...])` call is missed, the timeout flake may remain.
- If deliberate timeout-rejection cases are changed to the new constant, parser coverage can weaken.
- If the fake executor raises `PermissionError`, the duplicate-code test can still hit the production fallback and return `8`.
