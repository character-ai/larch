### FINDING_9: Per-run-and-step lock blocking semantics unspecified

  - From Cursor-dyn-Process Lifecycle Auditor: Specify blocking `fcntl.flock(..., LOCK_EX)` (no `LOCK_NB`) for the per-run-and-step lock, with release in `finally` after registry classification and `start_daemon` return. Extend the concurrency test to assert the second caller re-attaches with `STARTED`, not a lock error.
