- **Generated prompt drift:** editing only `agents/_implementer-base.md` without regeneration fails `python3 python/cli.py generate check`.
- **Brittle test literals:** paraphrased `assert_contains` strings fail `grep -Fq`. Copy substrings verbatim from edited sources.
