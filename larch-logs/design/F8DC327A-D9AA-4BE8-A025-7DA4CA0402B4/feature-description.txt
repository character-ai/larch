# auto-error-reporting: port terminal-failure reporting to /design

Part 3 of 3 of the auto-error-reporting rework. Part 1: #3988. Part 2: #3991 (cross-repo filing; this issue's blocker).

#### Context

`/design` has no failure-reporting machinery today: no `STALL_TRACKING` analog, no classifier, no auto-filing. Hard failures halt or log to `$DESIGN_TMPDIR/execution-issues.md` and produce no durable larch issue. Likewise, script-to-main-agent escalations inside /design (a script-owned fix loop fails and the main agent repairs inline) leave no trace even though they are expensive.

#### Requirements

1. **Define the /design hard-failure and escalation surface**: terminal halts after the skill's own internal retries did not recover (publish failure, clarify-loop exhaustion, judge-panel collapse, helper hard failures), plus escalation events where a script-owned repair loop (plan-review/revision loop, validation and publish repair helpers) failed to fix a problem and handed it to the main agent. Enumerating the exact set is the first design task.
2. **Stage failure and escalation state in `$DESIGN_TMPDIR`** (a design-side equivalent of the implement stall state and escalation ledger) and gate reporting at design teardown, mirroring /implement Step 18a.
3. **Reuse the shared core** extracted in #3988 (skill-parameterized classification, attempts bookkeeping, escalation ledger, two-tier composition) and the filing layer from part 2 (cross-repo filing plus signature dedup). No forked copy of the helper.
4. **Same policy as /implement**: terminal filing plus escalation-event filing on otherwise-successful runs; at most one issue per run; mandatory root-cause with cited evidence and a `larch-defect | environment | operator-action` verdict; skip filing on `operator-action` (record in chat and the run log); Tier A full info in larch dev clones; Tier B larch-internal-only fields and a bounded narrative elsewhere; no log tails in Tier B (v1).
5. **SECURITY.md and docs** updated for the new /design surfaces.

#### Dependencies

Blocked by #3991 (which is blocked by #3988): the /design port wires into the final shared filing layer once, instead of being rewired after cross-repo filing lands.
