## Risks flagged

- **Missing-ref fallback parity**: today, when `origin/main` is missing, the classifier returns false (i.e., falls through to generation). In fork mode, when `upstream/main` is missing the classifier should *also* fall through to generation, not skip. The fix must preserve "fail-closed → generation" symmetry across both modes.
- **Test blind spot on second callsite**: the existing generator stub means a regression in `generate-code-flow-diagram.sh:58` would not be caught by the harness unless the test also verifies that step-7a.sh **passes** the new base args to the stubbed generator (call-log assertion), or a focused test exercises the real generator's diff line.
