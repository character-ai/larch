### FINDING_2: `--skill` combined with `--write` can corrupt the committed baseline
- **Reviewer(s)**: Codex-Arch, Cursor-Innovation, Codex-Innovation, Cursor-Pragmatic, Codex-Pragmatic, Codex-Generic
- **Severity**: blocking
- **Concern**: The new `--skill` filter is not constrained against `--write`. If `main()` honors the filter during baseline regeneration, `python3 python/cli.py lint skill-closure-growth --write --skill design` can rewrite `python/skill-closure-baseline.json` with only one row. `load_baseline()` expects exactly one row per gated skill (`design`, `implement`) with a fixed `BASELINE_KEYS` set, so the committed two-row ratchet becomes invalid and subsequent default lint runs fail closed until manual repair.
- **Suggested revisions (informational for voters; coder decides)**:
  - From Codex-Arch: Reject `--skill` when `--write` is present, or make `--write` always ignore the filter and regenerate both gated skills.
  - From Cursor-Innovation: In main(), keep --write on scan_all(root) only; reject --skill when --write is set (exit 2) or document and enforce that --skill is ignored for --write
  - From Codex-Innovation: Keep `--write` on the full two-skill scan, or reject `--skill` whenever `--write` is set.
  - From Cursor-Pragmatic: Specify that `--write` always scans and writes both skills (ignore or reject `--skill`), and add a test that `main(["--write", "--skill", "design", ...])` exits 2 or still emits the two-row canonical baseline
  - From Codex-Pragmatic: Reject --skill whenever --write is set, or force --write to always regenerate the full two-row baseline and keep the filter read-only.
  - From Codex-Generic: Revise the plan so --write always scans and writes all gated skills, or reject combining --write with --skill with exit 2
