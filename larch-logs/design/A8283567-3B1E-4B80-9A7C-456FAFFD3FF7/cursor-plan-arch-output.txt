The new helper must preserve that contract or local “pass” can be wrong.

2. **[correctness]** [.github/workflows/ci.yaml:71-73](.github/workflows/ci.yaml) / [Makefile:15-18](Makefile) — CI `lint` runs `make lint-only` with `SKIP=agnix,lint-mermaid-fences,shellcheck`, not `make lint`.
