# contract-unification [FEATURE] Mandate run_log_corpus for run-log scanners

#### Problem

`report/run_log_corpus.py` is the intended shared corpus walker ("Shared safe helpers for committed larch run-log corpus walks") but has exactly two consumers (`report_tokens_scan.py`, `tokens.py` — verified). Roughly ten scanners bypass it: `calibration/difficulty_calibration.py:216` (near-verbatim copy of `_safe_child_run_dirs`), `issue/_ground_truth.py:263,277,293,378` (four copies of the manifest-name loop in one file), `issue/rejected_analysis.py:290` and `review/_voting_calibration.py:530` (both know the `manifest.json` vs `run-manifest.json` dual-name fork that other readers do not), `report/gc_run_logs.py:74`, `report/final_report.py:268,622`, `issue/audit_runs.py:420`, `skills/fluff-analysis/scripts/fluff-analysis.py:96-105`, `skills/voter-calibration/scripts/voter-calibration.py:95`. Round-number-from-path is parsed 7 times; the classification triple-glob is copied 3 times verbatim. Skill scripts half-import/half-copy: fluff-analysis.py imports two helpers from `rejected_analysis` while hand-copying their siblings, and its `_run_has_multiple_rounds` copy already dropped a term.

#### Goal

Grow `run_log_corpus.py` into the single API: safe dir walk, manifest load (owning the dual-name adapter), `run_started_at`, `larch_version`, `round_num_from_path`, `discover_classifications`. Repoint all in-repo scanners and the two skill scripts (they already sys.path into `python/`). Add an adoption-ratchet lint for new `larch-logs` walkers outside the owner.

#### Size

~400 duplicated lines; net reduction 250-300, and manifest parsers drop from ~8 to 1.

#### Dependencies

Land after the dead-code deletion batch, which removes three of the bypassing walker modules outright.
