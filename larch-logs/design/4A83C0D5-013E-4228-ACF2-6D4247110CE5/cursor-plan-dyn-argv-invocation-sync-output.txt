## Findings

1. **[correctness]** `skills/design/SKILL.md` (Step 0b init fence; plan.txt:270-273): The proposed post-gate fence uses `_init_out=$(design-init-runparams.sh …)` while the route fence correctly uses the `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/design/scripts/` path (plan.txt:242). That breaks the repo’s skill-runtime path convention and will fail in a clean subshell because the new script is not on PATH.

2. **[correctness]** `skills/design/SKILL.md` (Step 0b driver fences; plan.txt:237-283) vs driver argv specs (plan.txt:97-102, 175-178): The plan models handoff on Step 3’s fully spelled fence (`skills/design/SKILL.md:854-857`) but leaves both driver call sites as `…`. Required args are declared in the drivers yet not pinned at the orchestrator:
   - **Route:** `--design-tmpdir`, `--issue`, `--issue-title`, `--issue-body-file` (orchestrator writes `$DESIGN_TMPDIR/issue-body.txt` per plan.txt:238-239), `--has-clarify-label true|false`, `--claude-pid`
   - **Init:** `--design-tmpdir`, `--issue`, `--session-id`, `--claude-pid`, `--classification SIMPLE|HARD`, `--partition-requested`, `--brainstorm-requested`, `--manual-requested`
   
   Removing sub-step 5 tier prose (plan.txt:270-283 replaces current `skills/design/SKILL.md:280-290`) without documenting `--classification "$( [[ "$hard_requested" == true ]] && echo HARD || echo SIMPLE )"` (or equivalent) leaves a required driver flag unsourced at the call site.

3. **[risk-integration]** Same SKILL section vs driver internals (plan.txt:112-114, 191-192): `--repo` is optional on both drivers and used for `design-pause-load.sh` and `tracking-issue-write.sh rename`, but the SKILL rewrite only adds body-file + clarify-label forwarding. Current orchestrator resolves `REPO` immediately before those calls (`skills/design/SKILL.md:208-211`, `281`); the plan does not say who resolves or forwards `REPO` after extraction, so pause resume and designing rename can regress on non-default repos.
