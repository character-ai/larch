For **design-route**, the driver allowlist (plan NEW `design-route.sh` ~143–147) and companion `.md` include `WARN` and `ERROR` alongside branching keys `ROUTE`, `BRAINSTORM_PREFIX`, etc. The proposed SKILL handoff does not:
