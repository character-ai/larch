# contract-unification [FEATURE] plan_grammar.py: single plan-format owner

#### Problem

The plan grammar (heading forms for NEW/UPDATED/REWRITTEN/MAY_UPDATE sections and the `diff_lines:`/difficulty trailer set) has no owning module. Six trailer parsers: `design_step5c.py:444-538`, `design_step2b.py:751,876`, `implement/preflight.py:444`, `calibration/difficulty.py:38,594` (the only full-set regex), `agents/_drafter.py:456,521`, `design/plan_quality.py:446`. The two heading regexes have already diverged (verified): `plan_quality.py:1087` requires `^###` and knows all four headings; `_plan_quality_commands.py:502-510` accepts `^#{2,3}`, knows only NEW/UPDATED, and adds a bracket form. The grammar is also restated as serializer prose in `design_step2b.py:411-436` and `_drafter.py:369`. Adjacent marker drift: `design/decompose.py:336-339` composes the plan-block markers inline instead of calling `issue_wire.compose_named_block`; `learn_from_bugs.py:57` uses a laxer case-insensitive marker regex; `design_router.py:128` hand-checks marker literals.

#### Goal

One `plan_grammar.py` (in `larch/design/` or `larch/issue/`) owning heading and trailer regexes, trailer composition, and section iteration; `issue_wire` remains the marker owner and the bypass sites repoint. docs/issue-anchored-plan.md names the module as normative. Adding a trailer key becomes a one-file change instead of six coordinated regex edits.

#### Size

~200 scattered lines to one ~80-line module; six parsers to one.
