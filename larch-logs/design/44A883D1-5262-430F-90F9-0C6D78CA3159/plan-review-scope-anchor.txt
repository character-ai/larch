## 3. Retire `reviewer-architectural-compliance` from /implement Step 5

Remove `architectural-compliance` from `_CODE_REVIEW_ARCHETYPES` (`python/larch/core/config.py:427`), dropping the Step 5 panel from 4 specialists per vendor (correctness, edge-cases, testing, architectural-compliance) to 3 (correctness, edge-cases, testing).

Rationale: the post-review Step 8 architectural assessment (`larch:arch-assessor` read-only subagent over the materialized diff, plus the publish-side invariants/guidelines gate) already covers architectural invariants/guidelines compliance. Today `reviewer-architectural-compliance` is the only Step 5 panel member that receives rendered `I-*`/`G-*` knowledge; retiring it means no Step 5 panel member receives rendered architectural knowledge — acceptable since Step 8 owns that surface.

Follow-up surface to update together: the hand-maintained `agents/reviewer-architectural-compliance.md`, the `implement.review_and_fix.panel_hard` topology row (`docs/topology.md`), and the static-specialist text in `docs/review-agents.md`. The Step 8 arch-assessment path and `/design` Gate C assessment are unaffected.
