- **Reviewer(s)**: Codex-Arch, Codex-Requirements

- **Concern**: Exact-union completeness rows for `skills/design/SKILL.md` and `skills/implement/SKILL.md` undercount targets that `_DIRECT_TARGET_RULES` already adds from overlapping rule rows. - **Suggested revisions (informational for voters; coder decides)**:
