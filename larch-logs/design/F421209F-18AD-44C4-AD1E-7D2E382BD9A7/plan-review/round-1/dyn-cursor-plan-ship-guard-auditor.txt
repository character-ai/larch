### 1. [correctness] `python/larch/implement/dispatch_ship.py:606-611` — Phase14 skip needs handoff coupling, not flag presence alone

The plan moves skip behind guards and limits it to the no-checks case, but it only says “read flag contents.” In the repo, the same flag basename is written at least three ways: empty conflict handoff (`python/larch/git/rebase.py:108-114`), `RESUME_PHASE`-only resume seed (`python/larch/implement/ship_resume.py:247-248`), and `REASON=mergeStateStatus=…` for no-checks (`python/larch/implement/ship.py:192-197`). Route-exit already pairs flag presence with `detail == no-ci-checks-observed` (`python/larch/implement/dispatch_ship.py:220-231`). Pre-fix-rebase has no exit payload, so it must read `.ship-route-exit-handoff.env` in addition to flag bytes. Without that, conflict-shaped flags can still satisfy a content-blind skip after checkout guards pass.

### 2. [risk-integration] `skills/implement/SKILL.md:692-698`, `skills/implement/references/ship-pr-ci-fix.md:5-9` — Sentinel writer without ci-fix reader

Add the read/check before autonomous repair and pin it in tests.

### 3. [correctness] `python/larch/implement/dispatch_ship.py:531-547` — In-progress conflict path must write `PHASE=rebase` fail-closed
