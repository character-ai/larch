# md-to-py-VIII: elide the /design step1d5 entry fence on the brainstorm-off path

**Lever:** turn-count.

**What:** On the dominant `--brainstorm`-absent path, `step1d5 --mode entry` is a near-no-op — it emits `STEP1D5_ACTION=skip` and acts as a pause checkpoint. When brainstorm is off, the `.completed` sentinels for 1c/1d/1d.7/1e are written by the drafter prep, not the 1d.5 prelude, so the entry fence produces nothing consumed downstream, and `step1d7` is the very next pause checkpoint. `brainstorm_requested` is already known mentally from Step 0b.

**Change:** When `brainstorm_requested=false`, skip the entry fence, print the skip breadcrumb, continue to Step 1d.7. Verify the entry fence writes no skip-path sentinel consumed by `step1d7` or the drafter; if it does, fold that write into `step1d7`.

**Savings:** ~1 orchestrator turn per run on the majority (non-brainstorm) of runs.

**Risk:** MED. Verbs `step1d5` / `step1d7` already exist; likely pure-prose.
