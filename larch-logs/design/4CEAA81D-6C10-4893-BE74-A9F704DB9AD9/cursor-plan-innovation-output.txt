[risk-integration]** `python/ship.py:755-768` — `quiet_init` tmpdir resolution treats `IMPLEMENT_TMPDIR` export as optional (“may setdefault”). `lib-quiet.sh` prefers `IMPLEMENT_TMPDIR` for log placement; when `--tmpdir` is set but the env var is not, quiet logs land under `TMPDIR` instead of the session tmpdir. **Suggested revision:** Require `os.environ.setdefault("IMPLEMENT_TMPDIR", ctx.tmpdir)` in `main()` immediately before `quiet_init()` whenever `ctx.tmpdir` is non-empty.
