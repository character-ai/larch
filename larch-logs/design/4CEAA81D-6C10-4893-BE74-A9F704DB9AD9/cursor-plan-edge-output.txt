1. **Gap-fill no-op on missing finalize-state for inline-finalized stall types** (`plan.txt:38-40`). The plan tells `_persist_stall_metadata_if_needed` to skip when `finalize-state.sh` is absent and the stall came from a path that normally writes inline (merge-cap, pre-rebase, CI-monitor cap, etc.). Gap-fill would no-op and Step 18 would never see `STALL_TRACKING=true`. The existing `STALL_TRACKING=true` no-op when finalize-state is present already prevents double-writes on success; drop the missing-file heuristic.
