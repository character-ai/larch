1. **correctness** — [`scripts/launch-review.sh:1022-1040`](scripts/launch-review.sh) — The proposed `outputTokens > 1000` and `result < 500` bytes heuristic has no carve-out for valid short plan-review outcomes. A committed design log (`larch-logs/design/CCA26A49-5E7F-44B0-AE5C-19787126BDD1/`) shows `outputTokens=6918` with a `{"no_issues_found": true}` body (~28 bytes after extraction), which would be rewritten to `CURSOR_DEGRADED_RESPONSE` and then collapsed to `STATUS=CURSOR_EMPTY_RESPONSE`, forcing unnecessary Codex waterfall slots. The plan’s claim that such responses “typically” have `outputTokens < 100` is not supported by repo evidence. **Suggested revision:** exempt `json_no_issues_found`, `NO_ISSUES_FOUND`, and `schema_version` TSV prefixes before writing the degraded sentinel; extend case B3 with a high-token JSON-sentinel negative control.
