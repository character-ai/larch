1. **correctness** (`skills/cleanup/scripts/cleanup.sh` proposed Pass 1–2): The plan’s Approach line claims `find -mtime +N` is “semantically equal” to `newest < NOW - N*86400`. On BSD/macOS find, `+N` compares floored 24-hour period counts, not raw seconds, so an entry can satisfy the old cutoff while failing `+N` for almost one extra day. That is conservative (less deletion), acceptable for #3212’s performance fix, but the “strictly older than N×24h” wording does not match BSD’s floored-day rule. **Suggested revision:** document floored-period semantics and the small boundary slack; no code change required unless exact second parity is a product requirement.
