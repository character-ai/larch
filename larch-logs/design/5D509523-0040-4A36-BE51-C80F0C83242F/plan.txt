- **Readability line damage**: rewording line 19 while keeping `<READABILITY_STYLE>` fails `python3 python/cli.py lint readability-preamble` and `test_prompt_kinds`; the full sentence is byte-stable.

- **Baseline freshness failure**: skipping regen passes one-directional `lint skill-closure-growth` on shrink but fails `test_committed_baseline_matches_fresh_scan` in CI.
- **Template damage**: changing fenced voter lines or finding/OOS templates can break render or parser expectations.
