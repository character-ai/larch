CI fixing via bgjob lanes with evidence-based timeouts; make ship-step autonomous

## Goal

The ship step (Step 8+) must be aggressively autonomous: CI and lint failures are repaired by delegated background lanes, and the main agent bails to the operator only in exceptional circumstances (fork/repo-unavailable, auth/infra hard failure, exhausted generous budgets, red base branch beyond a recorded repair). Today, multiple fallback paths silently dump repair work back onto the main agent, and the delegate lanes are killed by undersized timeouts.

## Evidence (committed larch-logs + code, 2026-07-10)

- Share of merged implement PRs containing a `Fix CI failure (main-agent)` commit: ~4.5% mid-June -&gt; 31% week of Jun 28-Jul 4, 23% week of Jul 5-11 (139 such commits since Jun 28). Runs with CI issues cost ~2x in main-agent tokens (Step 8 claude lane 14.1M vs 6-7M).
