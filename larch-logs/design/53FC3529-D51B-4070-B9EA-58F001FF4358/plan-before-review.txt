## Approach

Create one shared review dispatch module. Move common state and contract logic into it, while keeping prompt rendering, availability policy, warnings, and launch behavior in each owning dispatcher.

Parameterize shared panel and voter row builders with topology keys such as `review.panel`, `design.plan_review_panel`, `review.voters`, and `design.plan_voters`. Preserve each family’s configured slots and model roles.

Converge these known drifts for focused review:

- Use the canonical `_resolved_model_for_row(tool, model_role, default_model)` signature. Remove the code-review voter’s hardcoded vote-role variant.
- Store voter paths as `Path` in `DispatchState`. Convert them with `str()` only at subprocess, manifest, and KV boundaries.
- Use `python/cli.py voting voter-status-block` for both final emitters. Preserve the existing 14-key contract, order, path-file gate, and `DISPATCH_OK`.
- Share calibration snapshot creation while retaining each family’s repository-root resolution and fail-soft behavior.
- Share parse-rate result validation while retaining family-specific retry arguments, prompt paths, context flags, runner seams, and launch labels.

## Edge cases

- A voter path may be absent, skipped, failed, empty, or lack a successful `.done` sidecar.
- A voter paths file may exist but be empty. Preserve the status-block path gate.
- Tool configuration may omit Codex or Cursor, or use an explicit Cursor model.
- A model role may be missing or invalid. Resolve it as `default`, not `vote`.
- Calibration may be disabled, unable to resolve a log root, fail its subprocess, or produce an empty file.
- Parse-rate retry may return diagnostics before its status. Read the final non-empty line only.
- Dynamic plan-review rendering may fail for one slot. Preserve its per-slot warning and accounting.

## Failure modes

- Treat calibration failure as unavailable feedback, not dispatch failure.
- Treat parse-rate subprocess failure or malformed output as `NOT_SUBSTANTIVE`.
- Preserve partial-waterfall accounting and per-slot failed or skipped states.
- Do not emit a successful dispatch when no effective judge remains.
- Do not silently change manifest attribution or the final voter KV grammar.
- Keep tally unification, snapshot-family parameterization, and broader dispatch redesign out of scope.
