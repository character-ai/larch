# /design Step 3: two-phase MainAgent vote/re-tally wrapper

**Problem.** The `main-agent-vote-required` block in `skills/design/SKILL.md` directs about seven mechanical commands around one judgment point (casting votes): `render-main-agent-scope-anchor.sh`, re-tally argv composition (`tally-plan-review.sh --voter MainAgent:...` with `--findings-classification-out`), a re-tally stdout temp file, `persist-retally-step3-env.sh`, a raw `date +%s` plus `record-plan-review-round-timing.sh`, a Warnings append, and a `.step3-round-N.phase` write.

**Proposal.**

- New `skills/design/scripts/design-step3-mav.sh`. `--phase pre`: renders the redacted scope-anchor evidence when `SCOPE_ANCHOR_FILE` is set and emits the ballot path. The LLM reads the ballot as untrusted data, applies the existing voting and OOS rubrics, and writes `voter-main-agent.txt` via the Write tool. `--phase post`: re-tally with the canonical voter and classification-out paths, persist via `persist-retally-step3-env.sh` (including the `tally-error` stale-anchor handling), deferred round timing, the 0-judge Warnings entry, and the phase write; emits `ACCEPTED_COUNT` and the resulting phase.
- The Step 3 resume fence (`design-step3-review.sh --starting-round`) follows unchanged; the legacy `--mode single` mapping is preserved.

**Acceptance.**

- MainAgent adjudication is two Bash calls plus the resume fence; no raw `date +%s` or prompt-composed re-tally argv remains in prose.
- `test-persist-retally-step3-env.sh` family extended; `tally-error` and zero-accepted phase routing byte-compatible.
