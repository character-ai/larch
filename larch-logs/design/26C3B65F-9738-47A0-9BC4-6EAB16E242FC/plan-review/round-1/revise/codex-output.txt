## Edge cases

- A normal launcher exits successfully after creating a committed HEAD with an expected subject but no trailer.
- The trailer names another lane step.
- Multiple provenance trailers make identity ambiguous.
- Git cannot read the full commit body.
- A normal salvage commit is created but fails subsequent provenance verification.

## Failure modes

All provenance, ancestry, or commit-body read failures block reship. Preserve the candidate commit for operator inspection, but do not treat it as an authorized fixer result or advance lineage.
