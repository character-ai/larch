Bind each salvage commit to its deterministic lane step. Keep the existing subject, ancestry, and single-commit checks. Require exactly one matching `Larch-Salvage-Step` trailer before either normal dispatch or crash recovery authorizes reship.

## Edge cases

- The commit has the expected subject but no trailer.
- The trailer names another lane step.
- Multiple provenance trailers make identity ambiguous.
- Git cannot read the full commit body.
- Normal dispatch creates a commit but cannot verify its provenance.

## Failure modes

All provenance read or validation failures must block reship. Preserve the commit for operator inspection, but do not treat it as an authorized fixer result.

## Testing strategy

Run the changed CI tests in `python/tests/implement/test_ci.py`. Confirm valid normal and crash salvage paths reship, while subject-only and malformed-provenance commits bail.
