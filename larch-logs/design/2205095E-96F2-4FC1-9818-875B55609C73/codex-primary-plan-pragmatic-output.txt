1. **[correctness] `python/voting.py:656-705`**
   - **Concern:** The plan changes voter labels to `cursor-validity`, `cursor-plan-fidelity`, and `cursor-pragmatism`.
   - **Breakage:** `parse-rate-retry` passes `--voter-tool` into `launch_voter_retry`.
   - **Result:** A retry for any archetyped Cursor voter can fail as an unknown tool.
   - **Suggested revision:** Add a minimal launcher-tool normalization for `cursor-* -> cursor`, or add a separate launcher-tool field.

2. **[risk-integration] `python/legacy_review_shell/tally-code-votes.sh:421-437`**
   - **Concern:** The plan adds `--voter-labels`, but parse-rate filtering removes voter files after labels are paired.
   - **Breakage:** Effective votes can compact while labels do not.
   - **Result:** `vN_tool` can silently attribute one archetype's vote to another.
   - **Suggested revision:** Filter labels and files together into `EFFECTIVE_VOTER_FILES` and `EFFECTIVE_VOTER_LABELS`.

3. **[risk-integration] `python/legacy_review_shell/tally-code-votes.sh:303-318`**
