## Plan

### Goal

Bring `skills/design/SKILL.md` to the D3 terminal end-state:

- **Every Bash fence** in `skills/design/SKILL.md` is one direct call to a repo wrapper script.
- **No inline Bash** remains in any fence.
- **No consecutive executable script-call fences** remain unless separated by a real prompt-side boundary.
- **Existing `/design` behavior stays unchanged**, including argv parsing, degraded tools, verbal issue creation, route/result-env handoffs, pause/resume, postplan branches, OOS filing, publish recovery, validator branches, Step 3 review handoffs, and Gate B bypass behavior.

### Approach

**Collapse inline Bash into wrappers.**

- Add `skills/design/scripts/design-step*.sh` wrappers with `.md` siblings.
- Make every new direct-called wrapper executable.
- Make each wrapper self-rehydrate from:
  - `--session-env-path "$HOME/.cache/larch/sessions/current-design-env-$PPID.sh"`
  - `--claude-pid "$PPID"` only where root Claude PID is needed.
- Do not let wrappers derive root Claude PID from `$PPID` internally.
- Keep `design-step3-state.sh` as an internal helper only.
- Do not invoke `design-step3-state.sh` directly from `SKILL.md`.

**Preserve prompt-side boundaries.**

- The orchestrator still owns both-tools-down Continue/Abort.
- The orchestrator still owns verbal `/larch:issue` creation.
- The orchestrator still owns clarify and already-planned `AskUserQuestion` branches.
- The orchestrator still owns OOS `/larch:issue` batch creation.
- Step 3 remains split because the plan preview is a required user-interrupt and plan-review-loading boundary.

**Preserve result-env contracts.**

- Do not introduce `.design-step0-result.env`.
- Keep `.design-route-result.env`.
- Keep `.design-init-runparams-result.env`.
- Keep `.design-publish-result.env`.
- Keep `.design-postplan-emit-result.env`.
- Allow non-result sidecars for wrapper-owned state, such as `.design-step0-parsed.env`.

**Preserve pause contracts.**

- Require pause-check before work for every direct `design-step*.sh` wrapper that replaces a post-Step-1c SKILL fence and sources session env.
- Do not use `.md` metadata to waive that invariant for post-Step-1c direct wrappers.
- Add explicit wrapper-level ordering checks for pause-before-work and sentinel placement.
