### Finding 1 — correctness (`docs/python-migration.md:36-42`, proposed `AGENTS.md` / `.claude/rules/python-first-scripts.md` bullets)

The plan’s thin-wrapper definition standardizes on `python3 "${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/python/cli.py" <domain> <verb> [args...]` on all three surfaces. Recipe **step 4** already requires bash callers to derive plugin root from the script directory first and only then fall back to `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}` so direct checkout execution does not depend on a prehydrated env var. Existing wrappers follow that pattern (`scripts/implement-preflight.sh:73`, `skills/implement/scripts/step-8-ship.sh:5-11`).

**Concern:** Policy text that omits script-dir-first derivation will produce new thin wrappers that break under direct execution and conflict with the migration playbook the plan cross-links.

**Suggested revision:** Make all three surfaces quote the same two-step resolution (script-dir → `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}` → `cli.py`) and point to recipe step 4 for the canonical bash-caller shape. Keep the no-direct-`python/<module>.py` and no-migration-shim distinctions as written.
