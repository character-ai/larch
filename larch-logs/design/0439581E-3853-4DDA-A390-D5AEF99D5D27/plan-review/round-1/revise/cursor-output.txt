## Edge cases

- **Zero findings:** accepted `0`, rejected `0`, total `0` prunes by net score once two launched rounds are recorded (including zero-finding `review_core` and settled plan-review rounds).
- **Exactly 1/3 acceptance:** do not prune by the floor.
- **Old ledger header:** fail open through existing error handling, or get dropped by ledger rewrite. Do not migrate old run data.
- **Neutral-only findings:** prune by net score because accepted minus rejected is `0`.
- **Two launched rounds only:** keep the current rule that a combo is not prunable until it has at least two recorded launched rounds.
- **Round 5:** never prune, even with poor precision history.
- **Plan-review `panel_pruned_empty`:** no ledger row for that round; prior rounds still count toward the two-round window.
- **Plan-review MAV / tally-error / panel-failed:** no ledger row; do not treat as a launched precision-history round.

## Failure modes

- A malformed ledger can disable pruning for that filter call through existing fail-open behavior.
- A bad classification TSV can produce zero counts, which can prune later rounds. Existing record warnings should remain visible.
- A mismatch between manifest labels and classification reviewer labels can undercount findings. Preserve current normalization and the plan-review label map to avoid widening that risk.
- Plan-review ledger record failure is warn-only; a failed record leaves round 3+ fail-open (no precision pruning) for that combo until a later round records successfully.
