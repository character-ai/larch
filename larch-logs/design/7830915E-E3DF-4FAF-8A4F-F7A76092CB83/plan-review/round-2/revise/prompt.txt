# review-points-overhaul Tie OOS finding points to realized issue fate

Stop awarding permanent points for OOS findings whose filed issues later die unfixed.

**Problem.** An accepted out-of-scope (OOS) observation earns `+1` forever, even when the GitHub issue it spawns is later closed wontfix, combined away by `/combine-issues`, or left to stale as `[OOS]`. Panel acceptance is a one-time proxy; it never reconciles against what actually happened to the issue. This rewards speculative OOS that inflates the backlog, which is the very bloat `/combine-issues`, `/analyze-issues`, and the `[OOS]` machinery exist to mop up.

**Proposed change.** Make OOS scoring fate-aware. Retroactively dock or zero the OOS point when its filed issue is closed unfixed or combined away. Feed `/analyze-issues` and `/combine-issues` outcomes back into reviewer scoring.

**Evidence.** Only **27.8% of `/design` OOS proposals are file-worthy** per the panel, and OOS ballot rows are unanimously rejected ~48% of the time. Acceptance is not the same as durable value.

**Affected surfaces.** OOS scoring in `skills/shared/voting-protocol.md`, `python/voting.py`, integration with `python/analyze_issues.py` and the combine-issues path.
