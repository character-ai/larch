### Finding 1
`write_final_report` writes `summary-final.md` at line 969 before any later work. The plan adds splicing after `render_run_summary` but does not say to move that write. Without reordering, all sinks stay compact-only.

### Finding 2
Issue #3794 is covered in `skills/implement/scripts/test-write-final-report.sh` (lines 1147–1176). CI runs `make test-write-final-report`, which is pytest on `test_pr_body.py`, not that shell script. Planned tests monkeypatch the helper and will not catch wrong rounds-root selection.

### Finding 3
`/design` upsert reads `final-summary.md` from disk (line 377). Splicing must be persisted to that file before stdout and upsert, not only emitted to chat.

### Finding 4 (out of scope)
Duplicating `progress_report.py` wiring is a maintainability trade-off, not a ship blocker. The plan’s separate module and no `_strip_md_for_terminal` are justified for markdown final reports.
