# /design Gate B: shared post-apply settle wrapper across three rewrite sites

**Problem.** The post-rewrite mechanical chain (trailer dedup via `gate-b-dedup-plan.sh --dedup`, the merged postplan fence, the `.completed/step-2b.5` marker, round snapshot, cursor advance, and the `.step3-round-N.phase` write) is respelled at three sites: `references/approval-gates.md` section "Shared post-apply pipeline", the SKILL.md Step 1e Gate A optional-trailer guard, and `references/discussion-rounds.md` Round 2. Each site also directs prompt-side `set +e` capture, `printf` of captured output, and a seven-arm `case` composition around the postplan wrapper.

**Proposal.**

- New `skills/design/scripts/design-step35-settle.sh` runs the post-rewrite chain in one call, parameterized by `--site` (gate-b, gate-a, discussion-round2). `gate-b-dedup-plan.sh --snapshot-trailers` stays a separate pre-rewrite call because it brackets the LLM edit.
- Operator brakes still bounce out: the wrapper relays rc 10 (shared validator-failure procedure), 12 (hard plan-size prompt where applicable), and 13 (Split-path) with the same envelopes; clean rc 0 settles markers, snapshots, cursor, and phase internally.
- Drop the prose-directed `set +e` capture, `printf`, and case-arm composition from both references: the contract becomes the wrapper call plus exit-code branching.
- Account for #4019: HARD-only snapshot sub-steps removed there are not reimplemented here.

**Acceptance.**

- Each of the three sites makes one settle call after a plan rewrite; rc 10/12/13 routing byte-compatible.
- `test-gate-b-dedup-plan.sh` and `test-gate-b-apply-mode.sh` extended to cover the wrapper.
