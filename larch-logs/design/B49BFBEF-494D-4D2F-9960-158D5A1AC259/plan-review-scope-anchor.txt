## Proposed Design Outline

### Goals
- Stop the `/implement` Stop hook from leaking a stale inherited `LARCH_TOKEN_SESSION_ID` into `resolve-implement-tmpdir` when the Stop payload has no `session_id`.
- Restore the hook's documented "empty session_id then fall through to TTL" contract.

### Non-goals
- No change to `scripts/sessionstart-health.sh`; it already exports-or-unsets correctly and is the reference pattern.
- No new regression test (operator decision at Step 1c); rely on the existing static `scripts/test-implement-anti-halt.sh`.
- No broader refactor, no shared-helper extraction, no audit of unrelated export sites.

### Approach sketch
- In `hook-stop-fail-close.sh`, replace the one-line `[[ -n "$SID" ]] && export ...` with an `if/else` that runs `unset LARCH_TOKEN_SESSION_ID || true` on empty `SID`.
- Mirror the SessionStart hook's exact export-or-unset block for consistency.
- Update the adjacent comment so it states the unset, not just "falls through to TTL".
- Document the session-id surfacing and stale-unset in the `.md` sibling contract.
