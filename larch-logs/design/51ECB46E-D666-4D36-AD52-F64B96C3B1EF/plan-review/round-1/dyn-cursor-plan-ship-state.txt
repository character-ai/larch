## Findings

### 1. phase14 runs before monitor (blocking, correctness)

**Plan:** `### UPDATED: python/ship.py` (lines 103–112 in plan.txt)

**Code:** `python/ship.py:1672-1716`

phase14 rebase runs at the **top** of each merge-loop iteration, before `ci_monitor.monitor`. The plan groups phase14 with `goto_rebase` and `MERGE_RESULT_MAIN_ADVANCED` under `_ship_rebase_phase` but does not pin that ordering. A helper only called from post-monitor branches would break phase14 resume, handoff clearing, and coverage in `test_phase14_flag_rebase_success_clears_handoff_and_conflict_files`.

**Fix:** In the plan, split loop-head phase14 from post-monitor rebase. Document call order in Edge cases.

---

### 2. Three rebase paths differ in counters and handoff (important, correctness)

**Plan:** `### UPDATED: python/ship.py`; Edge cases (plan lines 174–175)

**Code:** `python/ship.py:1687-1699`, `1902-1919`, `2006-2016`

The three rebase entry points do not share one counter pattern:

- **phase14:** `rebase_count += 1`, clears `RESUME_PHASE`/`CALLER_KIND`, `continue` (no monitor-path iteration bump).
- **goto_rebase:** `rebase_count += 1`; `iteration += 1` only when `monitor.action == "wait" or monitor.goto_rebase`.
- **MERGE_RESULT_MAIN_ADVANCED:** always bumps `rebase_count` and `iteration`.

A single undifferentiated `_ship_rebase_phase` risks wrong durable counters or stale handoff keys.

**Fix:** Add a variant parameter or enum to `_ship_rebase_phase` and document per-variant deltas in the plan.

---

### 3. Postmerge wrapper needs both call sites named (important, risk-integration)

**Plan:** `### UPDATED: python/ship.py` (lines 107–108); Failure modes (plan line 182)

**Code:** `python/ship.py:1404-1424`, `2097-2114`

The plan says `_ship_postmerge_phase` handles the duplicated wrapper around `run_postmerge_phase` but does not list the two `run_ship` tails that must use it. Partial migration leaves inconsistent `PHASE`/`done` ordering.

**Fix:** Mandate one helper for merged-resume and merge-loop success paths, preserving: pre `phase=postmerge` write, executor call, post `phase=done` only on `Outcome.OK`.

---

### 4. Merge-loop extraction must carry CI head seeding (important, correctness)

**Plan:** Edge cases (plan line 175) mentions counters only; `### UPDATED: python/ship.py` `_ship_merge_loop` bullets omit head seeding

**Code:** `python/ship.py:1628-1743`, `821-859`

`_seed_last_monitored_head` and `initial_startup_deadline_available` feed `_empty_checks_params_for_monitor` (#4867). They are not assigned to `_ship_merge_loop` in the plan. Dropping them on extract can break resume empty-checks grace (`test_cold_resume_zero_counters_still_gets_initial_startup_deadline`).

**Fix:** Move seeding into `_ship_merge_loop` and add an Edge cases bullet for `LAST_MONITORED_HEAD` durability.

---

### 5. `_ship_merge_loop` needs `ResumePlan`, not just ints (important, correctness)

**Plan:** `### UPDATED: python/ship.py` (lines 113–119)

**Code:** `python/ship.py:1243-1253`, `1623-1627`

`_merge_loop_uses_resume_counters(resume)` depends on `resume.start`. Passing only counter ints loses open-pr vs fresh rules and can zero or wrongly preserve counters on resume.

**Fix:** Pass `ResumePlan` into `_ship_merge_loop` and keep `_merge_loop_uses_resume_counters` inside the helper.

---

### 6. Pre-rebase stall must use terminal writer pair (important, risk-integration)

**Plan:** Testing strategy (plan lines 159–161) covers flush skip; `### UPDATED: python/ship.py` does not pin writer choice

**Code:** `python/ship.py:1863-1876`, `1967-1980`, `420-455`

Pre-rebase flush-skip stalls use `_write_terminal_state` plus `_publish_post_pr_terminal_snapshot`, not plain `_write_ship_state`. Collapsing that in `_ship_rebase_phase` can skip finalize-state overlay and the post-PR snapshot.

**Fix:** Pin the terminal-writer pair in the plan and add the focused helper test already sketched in Testing strategy.
