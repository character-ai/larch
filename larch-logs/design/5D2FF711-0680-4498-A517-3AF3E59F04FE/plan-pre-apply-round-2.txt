## Edge cases

- Main-agent OOS blocks have no vote tally. They must still promote when non-security.
- Security-tagged main-agent OOS must not promote into the public accepted review sink.
- Voted pool OOS without `Fileable=true` must stay out of `oos-accepted-review.md`.
- Security-tagged OOS must stay out of both `OOS proposed` and `OOS fileable` render columns.
- Old round metadata may lack proposed/fileable split fields. Rendering should degrade without crashing.
- Classification TSV rows may have fewer voter columns than expected. Missing votes or severities should not count as fileable.
- Markdown-only old tallies may not contain severity data. Prefer safe fallback labels instead of inventing fileability.
- Removing prune args must not break `aggregate_findings --input-mode`.
- Both prune callers (`plan_review_round` and `review_core_body`) must drop `--input-mode` before or with the parser change.

## Failure modes

- If `OOS_ACCEPTED_COUNT` is redefined back to vote-accepted, #6430 regresses.
- If either prune caller keeps `--input-mode` after the parser cleanup, argparse rejects the command; plan-review nit pruning or `/review` code-review nit pruning fails before voting.
- If both main-agent and aggregate-pool promotion read the same block, duplicate OOS entries can appear. Keep identity-based dedup unchanged.
- If report labels stay ambiguous, operators may still mistake proposed OOS for filed OOS.
