Main tradeoff: the default rates make cost visible by default, but they are estimates over total tokens only, not input/output/cache-aware pricing. That is acceptable for this issue because detailed splits and streaming are out of scope, but docs should label them as defaults/estimates. The risk is output ordering: `/design` has a strict “last line” contract, especially nested under `/implement`, so the cost line must be inserted before the machine footer and nothing after the footer. The other risk is schema drift in `/implement`; avoid changing `render-run-summary.sh` flags or markdown block shape except for cost values becoming numeric by default, and cover this with `scripts/test-render-run-summary.sh` plus `skills/implement/scripts/test-write-final-report.sh`.
