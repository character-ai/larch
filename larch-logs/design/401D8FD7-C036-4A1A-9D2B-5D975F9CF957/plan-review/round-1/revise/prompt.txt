**Motivation.** Stale-result rejoin is the most-recurring defect class in larch that no invariant covers:

The cure in both families was the same: key the persisted result to the inputs that produced it and validate at consumption time.

**Required change.** Add to `ARCHITECTURAL_INVARIANTS.md` under `## Workflow integrity`. Proposed text, ready to adapt:

```markdown
### I-Stale-1: A persisted step result is consumed only against the inputs that produced it

Any step result persisted for later consumption (a bgjob result env, a staged
or durable assessment, a cached verdict, a completion sentinel with a payload)
carries an identity of the inputs that produced it, such as a content hash,
HEAD SHA, or diff fingerprint. Every consumer validates that identity against
the live inputs before acting. On mismatch the consumer re-runs the producing
step or fails loudly; it never silently reuses the stale result. Evidence of
violation: /design review re-entry rejoined a pre-edit bgjob result env after
`plan.txt` changed, returning the pre-edit verdict without re-running (#6633);
the /implement guideline note was repeatedly consumed after HEAD drift
invalidated the diff it assessed (#5337, #5675, #5969, #6059, #6106).
Mechanical backing: input fingerprints in persisted result envs plus
consumer-side validation, mirroring the `DIFF_FINGERPRINT`/`HEAD_SHA` checks in
`python/larch/core/architectural_guidelines.py` (`note_consumable`,
`_staged_fingerprint_valid`); extend the same pattern to bgjob result envs
consumed on re-entry.
```
