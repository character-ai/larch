# /design Step 5: fold prelude into 5b-prepare and marker-based summary emission

**Problem.** Step 5 entry is two consecutive mechanical calls (`design-step5.sh` prelude, then `design-step5b-prepare.sh`) under an empty "5a" heading. The `/larch:issue` stdout bridge is a prose-directed raw `printf` to `oos-issue.stdout.txt`. Step 5c and the cancellation Final summary fence both require a separate Read of `final-summary.md` before verbatim emission.

**Proposal.**

- Fold `design-step5.sh` (step-4b sentinel write, timing) into `design-step5b-prepare.sh`; delete the empty 5a heading.
- Replace the raw `printf` bridge with a Write-tool directive (or an `--issue-stdout-file` default on the annotate phase); the mandatory write-before-annotate ordering is unchanged.
- `design-step5c.sh` and `design-step-final-summary.sh` print `final-summary.md` between stable BEGIN/END markers on stdout so the orchestrator re-emits the body verbatim from captured output with no separate Read. All existing emission gates are preserved: the non-empty-file condition, not gated on render exit 0, no free-form recap, WARN replay rows.

**Acceptance.**

- One Bash call enters Step 5; summary emission needs no Read call on success, failure, or cancellation paths.
- Verbatim-emission contract intact (full body, no paraphrase); `test-design-publish.sh` and the structure harness extended.
