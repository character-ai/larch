### 1. `ACTION=bail` must short-circuit before progress branches

The plan correctly targets passive-wait bugs at `python/ci_agentic_fix.py:391-429`, but it does not pin **control-flow order**. Today `_wait_for_ci` treats `ACTION=bail` as parse-valid (`python/ci_agentic_fix.py:209-210`), and `_run_cycle` then falls through to `next_run = wait.get("FAILED_RUN_ID") or run_id` (`python/ci_agentic_fix.py:428`). `test_wait_for_ci_accepts_action_bail` only covers the parser.

The plan must require bail handling **immediately after** the `wait_err` branch and **before** rebase/behind/pass logic, or bail can still reuse the current `run_id`.
