## Where Codex's framing is load-bearing

Codex's call-out — "make any cap-boundary MAV special case narrow and explicit rather than turning the loop into `>= cap`" — is important. Naively flipping `>` to `>=` in the cap check would break the documented round-5 MAV → restart-at-6 contract. The fix must:

1. Keep the existing `round_num > effective_round_cap` check intact for the documented path.
2. Move the check to fire BEFORE the artifact guard so a legitimate past-cap restart never trips the missing-file stall.
3. Add a defensive retry / sync probe around the artifact-existence check (one retry after a brief `sync` invocation) to defeat Hypothesis A filesystem-timing races without changing semantics.
4. When the artifact is still missing after the retry, emit diagnostic logging and fall through to `starting-round-invalid` — but with `STALL_TRACKING=false` per Decision 4. This handles the residual Hypothesis-B (path mismatch) case so the orchestrator can continue manually without a [STALLED] rename.
