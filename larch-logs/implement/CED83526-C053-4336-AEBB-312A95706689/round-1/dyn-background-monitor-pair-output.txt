### In-Scope Findings

- **risk-integration** `skills/design/scripts/dispatch-plan-assessors.sh:47-50` — The background writer never calls `larch_quiet_write_paired_pid_file` after `export DESIGN_TMPDIR`, even though `assess-plan-round.sh:132-157` allocates `LARCH_PAIRED_PID_FILE` and passes it to `breadcrumb-monitor.sh --paired-pid-file`. The voter precedent (`scripts/dispatch-plan-voters.sh:47-48`) writes the writer PID immediately after tmpdir setup. Without that write, a monitor timeout hits `WARN paired-pid-file-missing` and cannot TERM the long-running assessor dispatch tree (incident `984F0AA4` class: done sentinel may appear while external review children are still running). **Suggested fix:** After `export DESIGN_TMPDIR` (and before launching `launch-claude-review.sh` / `dispatch-with-waterfall.sh`), add `larch_quiet_write_paired_pid_file`, mirroring `dispatch-plan-voters.sh`. Keep the existing `unset LARCH_PAIRED_PID_FILE` immediately before the waterfall call so nested slots do not clobber the top-level PID file.

- **risk-integration** `skills/design/scripts/assess-plan-round.sh:138` — Dispatch is launched with `LARCH_QUIET_DISABLE=1` while `LARCH_BREADCRUMB_STREAM` is exported (`assess-plan-round.sh:127-128`). That weakens the background+monitor pair’s purpose (live stall/retry visibility during a ~30-minute panel). **Suggested fix:** Drop `LARCH_QUIET_DISABLE=1` on the background dispatch line (rely on stdout redirect to `LARCH_QUIET_LOG_FILE` for KV capture, as Family B SKILL fences do for `collect-agent-results.sh`), or only set `LARCH_QUIET_DISABLE` around `emit_kv` calls if stdout must stay machine-parseable.
