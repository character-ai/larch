- **risk-integration** `skills/report-tokens/scripts/test-run-analysis-quiet.sh:48-63` — The new quiet-mode harness only checks that `## Report Tokens Analysis` (and `### HARD` for design) reaches caller stdout and that `Scanning ` reaches stderr.
