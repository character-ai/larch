### In-Scope Findings

1. **Important** (`correctness`, `requirements` / `both`) — [scripts/test-harness-timer.sh](scripts/test-harness-timer.sh):60-66 — The `sleep 2` case only accepts `^[12]\.[0-9]{2}s$`, so any wall-clock duration that rounds to `3.xx` seconds (or higher) fails even though `sleep 2` was requested. **Concrete scenario:** an overloaded CI host where `sleep 2` returns after ~3.0s of wall time; the harness prints e.g. `3.01s` and the regex rejects it. **Suggested fix:** assert a numeric band (e.g. awk like `timing_in_range` with a generous upper bound) or widen the pattern to allow a small overrun window while still proving “about two seconds” and two decimal places.

2. **Latent** (`correctness`, `requirements`) — [scripts/test-harness-timer.sh](scripts/test-harness-timer.sh):51-58 — The `sleep 0.5` regex `^0\.[4-6][0-9]s$` excludes values just below `0.40s` (e.g. `0.39s`) from normal timer jitter or a fast scheduler. **Concrete scenario:** timing prints `0.39s` for a successful `sleep 0.5` run; the test fails despite acceptable behavior. **Suggested fix:** widen the low bound slightly (e.g. include `3` in the tenths digit) or switch to a numeric range with small tolerance, if CI flake reports appear.
