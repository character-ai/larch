### In-Scope Findings

- **risk-integration** `scripts/verify-run-log-completeness.sh:47-76` — `condition_reached` for `step9a1` treats the run as having reached that step only when `oos-issues.ndjson` / `run-statistics.md` already exist, `manifest.json` carries a non-empty `pr_number`, or `status` is exactly `done`; v2 manifests omit `pr_number` and in-progress/teardown paths no longer set `status=done` on the committed snapshot the way postmerge may do locally, while `audit-scan-run.sh` now keys “step skipped” off `steps_ran.<condition>=false` (`audit-scan-run.sh:93-96`). The two gates therefore diverge: verify can skip `step9a1` rows even when `final-summary.md` proves step 8 completed, whereas the audit harness can pass/fail on `steps_ran` instead. **Suggested fix:** Teach `verify-run-log-completeness.sh` the same `steps_ran` rule (and/or treat `schema_version>=2` plus presence of `final-summary.md` as having reached step 9a.1 unless `steps_ran.step9a1` is explicitly false), so CI/local completeness checks stay aligned with `audit-scan-run`.

- **risk-integration** `scripts/ship-pr.sh:1692-1746` — `run_postmerge_phase` still calls `larch-log.sh manifest` with `status=done` and `pr_number=$pr_num` after merge, while `scripts/implement-finalize.sh` teardown was changed to stop writing `status` / `pr_number` on normal paths (`scripts/implement-finalize.sh:1493-1501`). Anyone reading `scripts/implement-finalize.md` alone can conclude those fields are gone from the lifecycle, but ship-pr continues to reintroduce them on the tmpdir manifest for the closed-PR case. **Suggested fix:** Either remove these postmerge writes if the schema policy is “never persist post-flush keys,” or document ship-pr as the sole sanctioned writer and update `scripts/ship-pr.md` (still describing `status=done` / `pr_number`) and tests so the split contract is explicit.
