- **architecture** `skills/design/scripts/lib-phase-driver.md:14-17,42-44` — The **File-based handoff** section still normatively routes “contract breadcrumbs” through `emit_kv` on FD 3, while **Thin orchestrator fence** (and the Step 3.6 pilot) treat FD 3 as the human display channel and move machine state to `.env` plus exit codes.
