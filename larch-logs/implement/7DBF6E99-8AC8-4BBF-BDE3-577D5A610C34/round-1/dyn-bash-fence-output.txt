### In-Scope Findings

- **security** `skills/design/SKILL.md:1208-1210` — The rc=10 trailer loop feeds `_assessor_trailers` through an unquoted heredoc (`<<EOF` / `$_assessor_trailers` / `EOF`), so any command substitution or backtick syntax in captured trailer text is expanded before the `while` loop runs. Trailer bytes come from driver FD‑3 capture, not from numeric validation alone; a compromised or buggy driver (or future FD‑1 leakage into the capture stream) could turn trailer parsing into shell execution. **Suggested fix:** Drop the heredoc and iterate with a non-expanding reader, e.g. `printf '%s\n' "$_assessor_trailers" | while IFS= read -r _assessor_trailer_line …`, or use a quoted here-string (`<<<"$_assessor_trailers"` still expands once — prefer pipe/read from the awk output directly).
