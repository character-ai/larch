- **Character class `/-` before `]`:** In `[A-Za-z0-9._/-]`, the `-` is the last member before the closing `]`. Under POSIX ERE and Bash 3.2 `[[ =~ ]]` rules, a hyphen in that position is a literal hyphen, not a range endpoint; it does not form a `/`–`]` range. The same class appears in [`scripts/rebase-push.sh`](scripts/rebase-push.sh) (102–108), [`scripts/ci-status.sh`](scripts/ci-status.sh) (64), and [`scripts/ci-wait.sh`](scripts/ci-wait.sh) (116).
