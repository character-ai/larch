### In-Scope Findings

- **risk-integration** `skills/upgrade-larch/scripts/upgrade-larch.sh:405-410` — Post-install stamping now runs before stable verification (`write_install_stamp "$ACTUAL_VERSION"` at lines 406–408) while pruning remains gated on `VERIFIED_TARGET=true` (lines 409–410). When verification fails but `claude plugin install` leaves a version-shaped directory in cache (e.g. unexpected numeric tag), that directory receives a fresh `date +%s` stamp even though prune is skipped on this run. On the next successful prune, that orphan ranks as recently installed (stamped, high timestamp) and can displace legitimately useful rollback directories from the eight-slot retention window, even though the failed install was never a verified stable target. This is the opposite of mtime-based `backfill_install_stamps` behavior (lines 221–244), which preserves true age. **Suggested fix:** Restrict post-install `write_install_stamp` to the verified-stable path (keep unconditional stamping on the already-latest idempotent branch at lines 357–360), or stamp unverified installs with directory mtime (same semantics as `backfill_install_stamps`) instead of `date +%s`, so failed installs do not inflate retention priority on subsequent prunes.
