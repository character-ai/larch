Not a correctness defect — render non-zero is intentionally tolerated per plan — but the branch could be removed without changing behavior. **Suggested fix:** Remove the dead `if [[ "$_render_rc" -ne 0 ]]; then :; fi` block; `return 0` already ensures tolerance.
