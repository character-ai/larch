### In-Scope Findings
- **risk-integration** `CHANGELOG.md:20` — The `dedup-sweep` Gate B behavior change for issue #2730 is documented only in the `[Unreleased]` section of `CHANGELOG.md`, not in the `[42.4.5]` section, even though `plugin.json` ships at version `42.4.5` and the change is on this branch. Operators reading the `[42.4.5]` release notes will not find the new `dedup-sweep` breadcrumb behavior or its effect on `plan.txt` rewriting; they would need to notice the `[Unreleased]` section and infer it shipped in 42.4.5 — non-obvious because `[Unreleased]` conventionally means "not yet shipped." This creates a rollback-decision ambiguity and upgrade-note gap for anyone using the CHANGELOG to understand behavioral differences between versions. **Suggested fix:** Move the `- **\`/design\` Gate B (#2730)** ...` line from `[Unreleased]` into the `[42.4.5]` ### Changed section alongside the #2715 harness entry, or bump the plugin to `42.4.6` and open a new version section for the Gate B dedup change, preserving `[42.4.5]` for the harness expansion only.
