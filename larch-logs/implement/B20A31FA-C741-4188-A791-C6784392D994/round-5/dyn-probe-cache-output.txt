### In-Scope Findings

- **correctness** `scripts/check-reviewers.sh:319-321` — Auth-mode split stamps fix “env-key ignores stale login `false`” (test t6e), but the cache gate is asymmetric in the other direction: on the login branch (`codex-login`), any fresh cached `false` is honored (`_CODEX_STAMP_KEY != "codex-env-key"` is always true). A successful env-key probe only updates `larch-codex-env-key-present-*.stamp`, not the login stamp. If login failed earlier (or before the key was set), then `OPENAI_API_KEY` is unset/emptied within TTL, the operator can get `CODEX_PRESENT=false` without a live login probe even when `codex login` / `~/.codex/auth.json` would work now. **Suggested fix:** When persisting a probe result, invalidate the opposite auth-mode stamp (e.g. `rm -f "$(larch_stamp_path codex-login)"` after a live env-key probe, and the converse after login-mode probes), or mirror env-key semantics for login by only trusting a fresh cached `true` on `codex-login` (treat cached login `false` as a miss).

- **correctness** `scripts/check-reviewers.sh:319-321` — In env-key mode the gate only bypasses cache for cached `false`; a fresh cached `true` on `codex-env-key` skips the live probe entirely until TTL expiry. After a successful probe, a revoked, expired, or quota-blocked key that remains set in the environment can still surface `CODEX_PRESENT=true` / `CODEX_AVAILABLE=true` to `session-setup.sh` and the degraded-tools gate, which weakens the plan’s “bad key fails loud at health check” intent (launch may still fail later, but Step 0 misreports availability). **Suggested fix:** For `codex-env-key`, only allow cache hits on `true` when combined with a cheap re-validation (shorter TTL for env-key, or always re-probe env-key when `LARCH_PROBE_TTL_SECONDS > 0` but cache negative results only), or document that TTL applies to positives and accept the tradeoff explicitly in `check-reviewers.md`.
