  **Suggested fix:** Either strip the same larch-owned selector/provider/legacy `env_key` lines on the env-key path before exec (narrow scope, not a full TOML rewrite), or skip appending user config when `external_codex_env_key_enabled` and rely solely on `-c` overrides plus launcher-controlled `instructions`.
