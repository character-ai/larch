### FINDING_15: Unset `LARCH_CURSOR_LAUNCH_JITTER_MS` clears default and skips jitter
- **Reviewer(s)**: dyn-shell-var-parsing-output.txt
- **Severity**: important
- **Concern**: At `scripts/launch-review.sh:992-997`, `LARCH_CURSOR_LAUNCH_JITTER_MS` parsing uses `case "${LARCH_CURSOR_LAUNCH_JITTER_MS:-250}"` for matching but assigns `_CURSOR_JITTER_MS=$LARCH_CURSOR_LAUNCH_JITTER_MS` in the `*)` branch. When the variable is unset, the case word is `250` (from `:-250`), so `*)` runs and assigns from the still-unset variable, clearing the initial `_CURSOR_JITTER_MS=250` to an empty string. `(( _CURSOR_JITTER_MS > 0 ))` then treats that as 0 and skips jitter entirely, contradicting the documented default of 250 ms in `docs/configuration-and-permissions.md:212-214`.
- **Suggested revisions (informational for voters; coder decides)**:
  - From dyn-shell-var-parsing-output.txt: Adopt the probe-style two-step parse, e.g. `_CURSOR_JITTER_MS="${LARCH_CURSOR_LAUNCH_JITTER_MS:-250}"` followed by `case "$_CURSOR_JITTER_MS" in ''|*[!0-9]*) _CURSOR_JITTER_MS=250 ;; esac` (no `*)` assignment from the raw env var), or assign `_CURSOR_JITTER_MS=$REPLY` / the case match word in `*)` instead of `$LARCH_CURSOR_LAUNCH_JITTER_MS`.
