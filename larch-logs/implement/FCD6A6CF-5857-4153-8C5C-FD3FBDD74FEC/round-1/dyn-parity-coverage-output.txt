Parity review summary (for context):

- **`ci-decide.sh` vs `python/ci_monitor.py:decide()`** — Logically equivalent for all four `(behind, conflicted)` combinations when `CI_STATUS=pass`:
  - Bash: `pass && (behind==false || conflicted!=true)` → merge (`scripts/ci-decide.sh:116`)
  - Python: `pass and (not behind or not status.conflicted)` → merge (`python/ci_monitor.py:139`)

- **`_conflicted_from_merge_state()` vs `ci-status.sh`** — Matches the `case` rule: `DIRTY`/`UNKNOWN`/empty/unknown → `true`; `CLEAN`/`BEHIND`/`BLOCKED`/`UNSTABLE`/`HAS_HOOKS` → `false` (`python/ci_monitor.py:110-120`, `scripts/ci-status.sh:201-206`). Bash tests cover `DIRTY`, `BEHIND`, `UNKNOWN`, and empty (`scripts/test-ci-status.sh`).

- **`BEHIND` removals in `merge-pr.sh` / `merge.py`** — Four dedicated `BEHIND → main_advanced` short-circuits removed in each file; behavior is preserved by adding `"BEHIND"` to `ADMIN_ELIGIBLE_MERGE_STATES` and the inverted admin-eligible gates (`scripts/merge-pr.sh:239`, `313`; `python/config.py:199-205`, `python/merge.py:119`, `335`).
