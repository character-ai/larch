- **code-quality** `scripts/test-revise-plan-with-waterfall.sh:463-476` — C6 is a weaker regression for old case **4** than the harness it replaced: it uses `assert_has_key REVISE_PATCH_PATH` (presence-only) instead of anchored empty-value checks for `REVISE_PATCH_PATH=` and `REVISE_TIER=`, and it no longer verifies `plan.txt.before-revise` is preserved or that the plan hash is unchanged on total failure.
