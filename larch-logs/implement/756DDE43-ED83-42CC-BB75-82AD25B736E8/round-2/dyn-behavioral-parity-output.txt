Bash `ci-wait.sh` keeps the prior poll’s `CI_STATUS` / `BEHIND_COUNT` / `CONFLICTED` / `FAILED_RUN_ID` variables on that exit path (see `scripts/ci-wait.sh:191-195` with trap emission at `154-157`).
