### In-Scope Findings
- **risk-integration** `python/ci_cli.py:37-88` — The new CI CLI entrypoints dropped legacy argument validation: `status_main`/`wait_main` accept unsupported `--base-remote`/`--base-ref` and negative numeric values, then `gather_status` converts the resulting fetch failure into `CI_STATUS=pending` (`python/ci_monitor.py:401-403`); `decide_main` also accepts arbitrary `--ci-status`, negative counters, and non-boolean `--conflicted`, producing actions such as `ACTION=wait` instead of the old usage failure.
