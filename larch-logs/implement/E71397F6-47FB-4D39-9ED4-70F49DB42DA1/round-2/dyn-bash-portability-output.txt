### In-Scope Findings

- **code-quality** `skills/implement/scripts/test-stall-recovery-report.sh:850-885` — The new `read-session-env-key.sh` test stub parses and consumes all of `"$@"` in its `while` loop, then falls through to `exec "$real" "$@"` with an empty argument list. In `STALL_READ_STUB=dest-fail` mode, temp-file reads (`*.tmp.*`) take the empty branch and hit that fall-through, so `read-session-env-key.real.sh` is invoked with no `--file`/`--key`/`--default` and exits 1 immediately. The `case22-clear-dest-read-fail`, `case22-seed-dest-read-fail`, and related cases therefore fail on the **pre-`mv` temp read**, not on the post-`mv` destination assertion in `skills/implement/scripts/stall-recovery-report.sh:1197-1208` and `329-332`; they pass for the wrong reason and do not exercise the production failure path they claim to cover. **Suggested fix:** Capture the original argv before parsing (e.g. `saved=( "$@" )` at the top) and use `exec "$real" "${saved[@]}"` on fall-through, or only parse `--file`/`--key` from `"$saved[@]"` without shifting the array that gets forwarded to the real helper.
