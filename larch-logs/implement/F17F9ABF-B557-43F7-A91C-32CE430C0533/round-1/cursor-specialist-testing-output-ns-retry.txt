### FINDING_1: Convergence overwrites load-bearing `REVIEW_AND_FIX_STATUS` values
- **severity**: Important  
- **focus_area**: correctness  
- **location**: [skills/review-and-fix/scripts/review-and-fix.sh](skills/review-and-fix/scripts/review-and-fix.sh):1075-1130  
- **issue**: Part A runs whenever `exit_code == 0` and does not require `status == complete`. Any round that already resolved to `fix-applied`, `main-agent-vote-required`, or `no-changes` can still satisfy the low-accept + no-Important predicate and be overwritten with `converged-small-changes`.  
- **scenario**: A round applies fixes (`fix-applied`) while `ACCEPTED_COUNT` and the prior round’s `ACCEPTED_COUNT` (from `round-(N-1)/review-core.env`) are both ≤ threshold; Step 5 / wrappers lose the `fix-applied` signal, skip or mis-order `run-relevant-checks-captured --site step5-review-fixes`, or mishandle halt semantics for `no-changes`. Same class of bug for `main-agent-vote-required`.  
- **suggested_fix**: Gate Part A on terminal “clean review” outcomes only (e.g. only when `status == complete` from the `zero-findings|ok` branch, or explicitly exclude `fix-applied`, `main-agent-vote-required`, `no-changes`, and other non-complete statuses).

### FINDING_2: Plan’s “do not burn round cap” for degraded rounds is only documented, not integrated
- **severity**: Important  
- **focus_area**: risk-integration (plan / requirements)  
- **location**: [skills/review-and-fix/scripts/review-and-fix.md](skills/review-and-fix/scripts/review-and-fix.md):95-105; [scripts/run-step5-review.sh](scripts/run-step5-review.sh) (no `DEGRADED_ROUND` / cap logic in diff); [skills/implement/SKILL.md](skills/implement/SKILL.md) Step 5 loop (unchanged; no `DEGRADED_ROUND` handling)  
- **issue**: The branch emits `DEGRADED_ROUND` and documents that the orchestrator must not count the round toward the cap, but `run-step5-review.sh` and `/implement` Step 5 instructions do not read that key or adjust `round_num` / cap accounting.  
- **scenario**: A degraded-but-best-effort round still consumes a full slot in the prompt-side review loop; under panel stress the run hits `round_cap` sooner than the new contract implies.  
- **suggested_fix**: Wire `DEGRADED_ROUND=true` into the Step 5 loop (skill text and/or launcher) so cap increments and “substantial re-review” decisions skip degraded rounds as specified.
