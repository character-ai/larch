Reviewing the branch diff and publish-tail lifecycle code.
### In-Scope Findings

- **architecture** `skills/design/scripts/design-publish.sh:274-354` and `scripts/design-log-publish.sh:348-365` — The publish tail always runs `--pre-publish-only` with `--outcome approved` before `design-log-publish.sh`, and the flush copies every top-level tmpdir file (including `final-summary.md`) into the PR commit at that moment. When publish later fails (`SUMMARY_OUTCOME=failed-publish`), post-publish render and the tracking-issue upsert show the correct failure, but any pushed/open flush PR still carries a pre-failure `final-summary.md` headed as an approved run. That splits operator truth across the open PR tree, the issue summary comment, and chat. **Suggested fix:** Defer staging `final-summary.md` until publish outcome is known (e.g. run pre-publish only to a sidecar path, or have `design-log-publish.sh` re-stage/overwrite `final-summary.md` from the post-publish render before `git commit`), or drop pre-publish bundling of the terminal summary when flush is live.

- **architecture** `skills/design/scripts/design-publish.sh:222-237` and `skills/design/scripts/design-publish.sh:282-356` — `design_reentry_marker_write` runs immediately after a successful `plan-block-write.sh`, but `[DESIGNED]` rename stays gated on `PUBLISH_OK=true`. Re-enabling the real flush makes `PUBLISH_OK=false` common again, leaving `larch:plan` on the issue, a `[DESIGNING]` title, and a fresh same-session reentry marker. A quick `/design` retry within the 300s TTL hits `cancel-reentry-guard` instead of resuming publish recovery. That lifecycle coupling was masked while #3378 forced `PUBLISH_OK=true`. **Suggested fix:** Move the reentry marker write to after successful publish (and rename), or clear/short-circuit the marker when publish fails so operators can retry publish or `/design` without waiting for TTL expiry.

- **risk-integration** `skills/design/SKILL.md:1573-1590` — Step 5d tells the orchestrator that tmpdir removal is “not optional on the happy path” whenever `PLAN_WRITE_OK=true`, while Step 6 prose correctly requires `PUBLISH_OK=true` (when `SESSION_ID` is set) before running `cleanup-tmpdir.sh`, and the Step 6 fence still always contains a `cleanup-tmpdir.sh` invocation. After a failed flush, an orchestrator following the Step 5d anti-halt line literally can destroy the preserved `$DESIGN_TMPDIR` that Step 6 and `design-publish.md` rely on for `design-log-publish.failure.log` and recovery metadata. **Suggested fix:** Tighten Step 5d to say “continue to Step 6” without implying cleanup, and gate the Step 6 Bash fence on the same `PUBLISH_OK` predicate (or add an explicit “do not run the cleanup fence when publish failed” bullet next to item 7).

### Out-of-Scope Observations

- **architecture** `skills/design/SKILL.md:1545-1546` — The orchestrator writes `.completed/step-5c` whenever `PLAN_WRITE_OK=true`, even when `PUBLISH_OK=false`. Pause/resume therefore advances the step registry past publish failure; a resume at step `6` only reaches cleanup, not a publish retry. That predates this branch but matters more now that failed flushes are live again.
