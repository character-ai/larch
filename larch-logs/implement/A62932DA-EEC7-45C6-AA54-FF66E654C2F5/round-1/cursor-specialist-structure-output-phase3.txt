1. **Nit** `code-quality` `skills/design/scripts/design-postplan-emit.sh:185-192` — `_postplan_resolve_repo` duplicates the 120-character awk one-liner (including the `BEGIN{q=sprintf("%c",39)}` quote-stripping logic) verbatim from `_postplan_resolve_issue` at line 176–183; only the variable name and the matched key differ.
