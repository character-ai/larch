1. **Latent** · `correctness` · [scripts/dispatch-code-voters.sh:263-265](scripts/dispatch-code-voters.sh)  
   After `cp "$voter_path" "$first_pass_sidecar" 2>/dev/null || true`, the breadcrumb always says first-pass content was “preserved” even when `cp` failed (full disk, permissions, read-only fs). Operators and log triage can be sent to a missing or empty sidecar while believing it exists.  
   **Suggested fix:** Gate the breadcrumb on a successful copy (for example `cp … && emit_breadcrumb …` or test `-s "$first_pass_sidecar"` after `cp` and emit a different breadcrumb when the copy failed).
