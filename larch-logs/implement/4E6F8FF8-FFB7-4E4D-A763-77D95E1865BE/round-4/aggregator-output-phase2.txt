### FINDING_15: `cleanup.sh` mtime day-bucket gap at retention boundary
- **Reviewer(s)**: dyn-find-mtime-depth-portability-output.txt
- **Severity**: important
- **Concern**: Retention uses paired `find -mtime +N` and `find -mtime -N` day buckets instead of second-precision cutoffs. On BSD and GNU `find`, mtimes in the exact *n*-day bucket can match neither `+N` nor `-N`, so a stale top-level candidate can pass `+N` while a descendant at the retention floor fails `-N` and the tree is deleted—unlike the old `newest >= NOW - N*86400` behavior.
- **Suggested revisions (informational for voters; coder decides)**:
  - From dyn-find-mtime-depth-portability-output.txt: Add a `test-cleanup.sh` case that seeds a stale top-level mtime and a child at the retention boundary (not only `20000101` / `20990101` extremes), or combine `find` with a `stat`-based second check for the descendant probe; document the coarser semantics if intentional.
