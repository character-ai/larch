1. **Latent**, `correctness`, [scripts/merge-pr.sh](scripts/merge-pr.sh):159-171 — Recovery uses `git log --format='%s' "${PR_HEAD_OID}..HEAD"` and subject checks but never asserts that `PR_HEAD_OID` is an ancestor of `HEAD`. The plan/feature describe a **local-ahead** flush tail; if OID mismatch ever occurs with a non-ancestor relation, `A..HEAD` is not “ahead only” and could still yield 1–5 lines that all match the flush prefix, misclassifying divergence as recoverable and running `git-force-push.sh`. **Scenario:** broken or manually rewired refs after `gh pr create`. **Fix:** gate recoverability with `git merge-base --is-ancestor "$PR_HEAD_OID" HEAD` (or equivalent) before treating the range as flush-only ahead.
