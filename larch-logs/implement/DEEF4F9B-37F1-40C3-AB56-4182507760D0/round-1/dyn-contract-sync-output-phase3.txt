### In-Scope Findings

- **risk-integration** `skills/design/SKILL.md:795-812` — The Step 2a.5 compatibility bash fence (which writes the missing `.completed/step-2a.5` for old paused SIMPLE sessions) is placed *after* the prose instruction "proceed directly to Step 2b." An orchestrator following SIMPLE branch logic encounters "proceed directly to Step 2b" before seeing the compatibility fence, and may skip the fence entirely — leaving `.completed/step-2a.5` unwritten on pre-existing paused SIMPLE sessions that resume at Step 2a.5 with only `.completed/step-2a` present. There is no harness assertion that verifies the fence executes before the skip; the `test-design-pause-resume.sh` fixtures only confirm that the pause-save script routes to STEP=2a.5, not that the compatibility write actually runs. **Suggested fix:** Move the compatibility bash fence *before* the "If `design_classification == SIMPLE`, … proceed directly to Step 2b" sentence, or restructure the prose to read "If `design_classification == SIMPLE`, first run the compatibility guard below (to write any missing `.completed/step-2a.5`), then print the skip breadcrumb and proceed directly to Step 2b" so the fence is unambiguously mandatory on the SIMPLE path.

- **risk-integration** `skills/design/references/sketch-launch.md:5` — The updated **Contract** line reads "its sentinel artifacts and `.completed/step-2a` / `.completed/step-2a.5` markers are written *only* by `skills/design/SKILL.md` Step 2a entry fence when `design_classification == SIMPLE`." However, this PR also introduces a Step 2a.5 compatibility bash fence in `SKILL.md` that writes `.completed/step-2a.5` for old paused SIMPLE sessions where `.completed/step-2a` exists but `.completed/step-2a.5` is absent. The "only" claim is therefore false for that edge case and could mislead a future maintainer into removing or bypassing the compatibility write site, believing the entry fence is the sole authority. **Suggested fix:** Change "written only by … Step 2a entry fence when `design_classification == SIMPLE`" to "normally written by the Step 2a entry fence when `design_classification == SIMPLE`; the Step 2a.5 compatibility guard writes `.completed/step-2a.5` only for pre-existing paused SIMPLE sessions that resume after the entry fence was absent (see `SKILL.md` Step 2a.5)."
