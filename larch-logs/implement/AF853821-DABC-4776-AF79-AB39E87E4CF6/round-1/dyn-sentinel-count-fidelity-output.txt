Reviewing the branch diff and tracing sentinel fallback logic in `render-final-summary.sh`.
### In-Scope Findings

- **risk-integration** `skills/design/scripts/file-design-oos.sh:327-331` — `cmd_annotate` now returns exit 0 with `FILE_DESIGN_OOS_STATUS=annotate-skipped-empty-stdout` when `--issue-stdout-file` is empty or missing, but Step 5b in `skills/design/SKILL.md` only branches on non-zero `_oos_ann_rc` (partial-failure vs parse failure). An annotate skip therefore produces no `Tool Failures` entry, no operator warning, and no `oos-issues-created.md` / `- **Filed URL**:` annotations in `oos-accepted-design.md`, while `/issue` may still have written `oos-issue-sentinel` with `ISSUES_CREATED>=1`. The new sentinel fallback in `render-final-summary.sh` can make the final summary look successful (`OOS filed: N`) even though the durable design artifacts `/implement` Step 9a.1 uses for idempotency were never updated. **Suggested fix:** Treat `annotate-skipped-empty-stdout` as a first-class degraded outcome in Step 5b (parse annotate stdout and/or fail non-zero when `oos-issue-sentinel` exists but stdout is empty); keep the summary fallback, but also append a `Warnings`/`Tool Failures` breadcrumb and avoid implying full filing success when `oos-accepted-design.md` still lacks `Filed URL` lines.

- **risk-integration** `skills/design/scripts/render-final-summary.sh:345-354` — The sentinel fallback sets `OOS_COUNT` from `ISSUES_CREATED` only and does not read `ISSUES_FAILED` or `ISSUES_DEDUPLICATED`. Under the normal `/issue` contract this is not an overcount problem: `write-sentinel.sh` suppresses the sentinel whenever `ISSUES_FAILED>0` (`skills/issue/scripts/write-sentinel.sh:129-131`), so a compliant `oos-issue-sentinel` should always carry `ISSUES_FAILED=0` and `ISSUES_CREATED` already equals successfully created issues. The integration gap is defense-in-depth and semantic mismatch: the fallback does not verify `ISSUES_FAILED=0` before trusting the sentinel, and it can under-report dispositions when `ISSUES_DEDUPLICATED>0` (annotate would still record `DUPLICATE_OF_URL` lines in `oos-issues-created.md`, but the fallback only surfaces new creates). **Suggested fix:** When falling back, require `ISSUES_FAILED=0` from the sentinel (and optionally surface `ISSUES_DEDUPLICATED` in the note); if the sentinel is missing, malformed, or shows `ISSUES_FAILED>0`, keep `OOS_COUNT=0` and emit an explicit degraded warning instead of inferring from `ISSUES_CREATED` alone.
