- **architecture** `python/ship.py:1918-1925`, `python/config.py:15-24` — `_is_infrastructure_ship_error` maps only `"cannot read existing ship state"` `ShipError`s to `Outcome.INTERNAL_ERROR` (exit 1); all other uncaught exceptions in `main()` also become `INTERNAL_ERROR`.
