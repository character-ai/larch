### In-Scope Findings

- **architecture** `skills/fluff-analysis/scripts/fluff-analysis.py:126-137` — `normalize_design_severity()` does not map voter vocab tokens that `parse-judge-vote-and-rating.sh` allows (`blocker`, and unlike `normalize_severity()`, also omits `critical`/`trivial`). `parse_design_tsv()` normalizes `v1_severity`/`v2_severity`/`v3_severity` through this narrower function, so when `body_severity` is empty (concise rounds without `findings.md`), modal voter severity can collapse to `(none)` even though the TSV carries `blocker` or `major`.
