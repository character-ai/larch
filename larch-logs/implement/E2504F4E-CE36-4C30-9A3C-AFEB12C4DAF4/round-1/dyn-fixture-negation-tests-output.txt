The `! grep -Fq -- "$unexpected" "$err" || fail ...` construct at `scripts/test-lint-readability-preamble.sh:121-122` is sound under `set -euo pipefail`. The negation check runs after `set -e` is restored (line 117), inside an `if [ -n "$unexpected" ]` guard, and as the left-hand side of an `||` list. Bash does not treat a failing `! grep` there as a fatal errexit event: when the unexpected substring is absent, `grep` exits 1, `!` inverts to 0, and the list succeeds; when it is present, `grep` exits 0, `!` yields 1, and `fail` runs. That matches the established repo idiom (e.g. `scripts/test-design-log-publish.sh:259-260`, `scripts/test-scrub-submodule-paths.sh:61`).
