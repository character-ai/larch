### In-Scope Findings

- **code-quality** `skills/design/scripts/file-design-oos.sh:240-242` — The new `_sent_created` assignment inside `cmd_prepare` is not declared `local`, unlike every other function-scoped scratch variable in that function (`issue_sentinel`, `n`, `nonsec`, etc.). In Bash, undeclared names inside a function are global, so `_sent_created` leaks into the script namespace and can be clobbered or accidentally reused if `cmd_prepare` is extended or called from a sourced context. **Suggested fix:** Add `local _sent_created` (either alongside the other `local` declarations at the top of `cmd_prepare` or immediately before the assignment) before the `awk` command substitution.
