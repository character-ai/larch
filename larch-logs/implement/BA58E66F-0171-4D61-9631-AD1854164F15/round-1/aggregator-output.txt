### FINDING_8: `--clear-cross-session-cache` cannot bypass in-session sentinel
- **Reviewer(s)**: cursor-specialist-edge-cases-output.txt, dyn-sentinel-clear-cache-output.txt
- **Severity**: important
- **Concern**: `prepare --clear-cross-session-cache` clears the cross-session cache but still honors `oos-issue-sentinel`, so an operator expecting to force a normal re-file can be hard-stopped by a same-session sentinel with no documented escape hatch.

  - From cursor-specialist-edge-cases-output.txt: Document sentinel vs cache semantics and require manual rm oos-issue-sentinel to re-file in-session; or add --clear-issue-sentinel and update file-design-oos.md re-file contract
  - From dyn-sentinel-clear-cache-output.txt: Document the precedence explicitly in `file-design-oos.md` and `skills/design/SKILL.md` (cross-session clear does not remove or bypass `oos-issue-sentinel`; same-session recovery should run `annotate`, not re-`prepare`). If intentional force re-file in a persisted tmpdir is ever required, add a separate, loudly warned flag (e.g. `--clear-issue-sentinel`) rather than overloading `--clear-cross-session-cache`, and add harness case `S2` asserting `--clear-cross-session-cache` + present sentinel still yields `skip-already-filed-sentinel`.
