- **code-quality** `skills/design/scripts/record-plan-review-round-timing.sh:50-65` — The OOS counter uses `oos=$(awk ... "$DESIGN_TMPDIR/voting-tally.md")` under `set -euo pipefail` without the `2>/dev/null || true` guard applied to the accepted/rejected `grep -cE` paths on lines 45–48. - **code-quality** `skills/review-and-fix/scripts/record-implement-review-round-timing.sh:36-38`, `skills/design/scripts/record-plan-review-round-timing.sh:35-37` — Both deferred helpers validate `--start-s` / `--end-s` as strict digit strings but never strip trailing `\r` (or other whitespace) before validation.
