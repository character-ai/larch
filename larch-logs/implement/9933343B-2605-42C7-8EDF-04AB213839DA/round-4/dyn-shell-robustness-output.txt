The truncations `git_porcelain=$(printf '%s' "$git_porcelain" | head -c 32000)` and `rebase_patch=$(printf '%s' "$rebase_patch" | head -c 32000)` can therefore yield a non-zero pipeline status (SIGPIPE / 141) whenever the blob is larger than the byte cap, which aborts the subshell under `set -e` before `jq` runs and drops the sidecar without surfacing a launcher error. **Suggested fix:** Avoid that pipeline under inherited `pipefail` (for example wrap only those lines in `set +o pipefail` / `set -o pipefail`, use a temp file plus `head -c` on the file, or append `|| true` with an explicit empty fallback only if you still validate the truncated content before `jq`).
